### **Overview**
The Curio Corner is a treasure trove within the [[Allspar Market]], known for its unique and rare finds. This enchanting section of the market attracts adventurers, collectors, and scholars from all over [[Ruban]] and beyond. The Curio Corner is where magic and mystery intertwine, offering items that are as intriguing as they are valuable. Located in [[Strakhaven]], this section is a magnet for those seeking the extraordinary.

### **Market Layout**
The Curio Corner is a labyrinth of narrow lanes and winding paths, lined with stalls and shops overflowing with ancient artefacts, magical items, and peculiar oddities. Each vendor here has a story to tell, adding to the mystique and allure of this fascinating section.

#### **Permanent Merchants**
- **Antiquarian's Nook**: Specialising in ancient artefacts and relics, this stall is a treasure trove for historians and collectors.
- **Enchanter's Booth**: Offering magical items and enchantments, this booth is popular among adventurers seeking an edge.
- **Oddities Emporium**: A place for strange and unusual items, from shrunken heads to enchanted mirrors.

#### **Temporary Stalls**
- **Fortune Teller's Tent**: Seasonal seers and mystics offering readings and predictions.
- **Herbalist's Alcove**: Featuring rare herbs and alchemical ingredients, this stall is frequented by potion makers and healers.
- **Curious Curios**: A rotating selection of rare and unusual items from distant lands.

### **Detailed Walkthrough**

#### **Antiquarian's Nook**
Entering from the main avenue of the Curio Corner, the Antiquarian's Nook is immediately noticeable for its collection of ancient artefacts and relics. The stall is filled with items that span centuries, from ancient weapons and armour to scrolls and tomes. Historians and collectors often gather here, eager to discover the next piece for their collection.

#### **Enchanter's Booth**
Continuing deeper into the Curio Corner, visitors will find the Enchanter's Booth. This stall is a haven for those seeking magical items and enchantments. Shelves lined with wands, amulets, and potions catch the eye, each item possessing unique magical properties. The enchanter is always on hand to offer advice and demonstrate the power of their wares.

#### **Oddities Emporium**
A few steps further and the Oddities Emporium comes into view. This stall is a curiosity shop of the highest order, offering a wide range of strange and unusual items. From shrunken heads to enchanted mirrors, the Oddities Emporium is a place of wonder and surprise. Each item comes with its own story, told by the eccentric vendor who runs the stall.

#### **Fortune Teller's Tent**
Among the permanent merchants are the temporary stalls like the Fortune Teller's Tent. Here, seasonal seers and mystics offer readings and predictions to those who seek to glimpse their future. The tent is adorned with mystical symbols and filled with an air of mystery, drawing in visitors with the promise of insight and foresight.

#### **Herbalist's Alcove**
The Herbalist's Alcove is a temporary stall that features rare herbs and alchemical ingredients. Potion makers and healers frequent this stall, sourcing the unique ingredients they need for their concoctions. The herbalist is knowledgeable and offers advice on the properties and uses of each herb.

#### **Curious Curios**
Curious Curios is a rotating selection of rare and unusual items from distant lands. Each visit to this stall is a new experience, with the vendor constantly updating their inventory with fascinating finds. This stall attracts collectors and adventurers alike, all eager to discover something extraordinary.

### **Conclusion**
The Curio Corner is a captivating part of the [[Allspar Market]], embodying the magic and mystery of [[Strakhaven]] and [[Ruban]]. It is a place where ancient history and powerful magic come to life, offering visitors a chance to find unique and valuable treasures. Whether seeking a powerful enchantment or a rare artefact, the Curio Corner provides a shopping experience like no other, filled with wonder and discovery.
