#### **Overview**
Ondine are a unique and enchanting race of aquatic humanoids known for their bioluminescent markings and delicate, translucent fins. In the sunken city of [[Neridia]], they play a crucial role in maintaining the fragile balance among the diverse aquatic races.

#### **Biology and Appearance**

- **Bioluminescence**: Ondine are distinguished by their bioluminescent markings that can glow in various colours, allowing them to blend into their surroundings or communicate through intricate patterns of light. These markings are not just beautiful but serve practical purposes in the dark depths of their underwater environment.
- **Fins and Gills**: Ondine have delicate, translucent fins along their arms, legs, and backs, aiding in swift and agile swimming. They possess both lungs and gills, enabling them to breathe in water and on land.
- **Skin and Features**: Their skin is smooth and often a pale, iridescent shade. They have large, expressive eyes that can see well in low light conditions, and their hair tends to be long and flowing, often shimmering with the same bioluminescence as their skin.

#### **Race Traits**

**Ability Score Increase**: Your Dexterity score increases by 2, and your Wisdom score increases by 1.

**Age**: Ondine mature at a similar rate to humans but can live up to 150 years.

**Alignment**: Ondine value harmony and balance, often leaning towards neutral alignments. They can be good or evil, but most strive for a peaceful existence.

**Size**: Ondine are similar in height to humans, ranging from 5 to 6 feet tall. Your size is Medium.

**Speed**: Your base walking speed is 30 feet, and you have a swimming speed of 40 feet.

**Amphibious**: You can breathe air and water.

**Bioluminescent Skin**: You can cause your skin to glow, shedding bright light in a 10-foot radius and dim light for an additional 10 feet. You can control the colour and intensity of this light. As an action, you can dim or extinguish the light.

**Camouflage**: You have advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made to hide in aquatic environments due to your bioluminescent ability to blend into your surroundings.

**Darkvision**: Accustomed to the dark depths of the ocean, you have superior vision in dim and dark conditions. You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern colour in darkness, only shades of grey.

**Water Affinity**: You know the shape water cantrip. Once you reach 3rd level, you can cast the create or destroy water spell once per day. Wisdom is your spell-casting ability for these spells.

**Languages**: You can speak, read, and write Common and Aquan.

#### **Culture and Society**

Ondine society is structured around communal living and environmental stewardship. They are often seen as the caretakers of the underwater ecosystems, ensuring the health and balance of their habitats. Their settlements are built into coral reefs and underwater caverns, illuminated by their natural bioluminescence.

Ondine are known for their artistic expressions, creating intricate patterns of light during communal dances and ceremonies. They are also skilled healers and herbalists, using the abundant aquatic flora to create potent remedies and enchantments.

Despite their peaceful nature, Ondine can be fierce defenders of their territories, using their knowledge of the underwater environment to their advantage in conflicts. They are respected and sometimes feared by other aquatic races for their ability to manipulate water and light.

#### **Notable Locations** 
- **[[Neridia]]**: Once a grand island city, now submerged and in ruins, Neridia serves as a refuge for many aquatic races, including the Ondine. It is a hub of trade, conflict, and ancient mysteries, with each race claiming different districts within the sunken city. 
- **Silvershore**: A beautiful coastal town where the Ondine are known for their art and music, often performing enchanting melodies that draw visitors from across the region. 
- **Lakeshire**: Nestled on the shores of [[Mirrormere]], this town is known for its crystal-clear waters and the Ondine communities that thrive there, living in harmony with the lake’s ecosystem.

#### **Notable Figures**

- **Lyra Brightscale**: A renowned healer and shaman, known for her extensive knowledge of aquatic herbs and her ability to communicate with sea creatures.
- **Aeloria Wavesong**: A famous bard and diplomat who has brokered many peace treaties between warring aquatic factions with her enchanting voice and persuasive skills.
- **Thalor Deepcurrent**: A respected warrior and protector of Ondine territories, famed for his bravery in defending [[Neridia]] from external threats.

Ondine are a harmonious blend of beauty, wisdom, and resilience, making them a unique and valuable race in the world of Madhen.
