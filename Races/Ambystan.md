#### **Overview**
Ambystan are a unique race of amphibious beastkin resembling axolotls, known for their regenerative abilities and their distinctive external gills. They thrive in the waters of [[Neridia]] and [[Mirrormere]], but possess a rare, arduous transformation process that allows them to become [[Caudati]] if forced to endure prolonged dry conditions.

#### **Biology and Appearance**
Ambystan have a humanoid form with features reminiscent of axolotls. They possess smooth, moist skin that varies in colour from pink to dark brown, feathery external gills that extend from their necks, and wide, expressive eyes. Ambystan are known for their regenerative abilities, capable of healing minor injuries quickly and even regrowing lost limbs over time.

#### **Transformation Process**
Ambystan have the unique ability to undergo a torturous transformation into [[Caudati]] beastkin, known as the "Drying." This process occurs when an Ambystan is subjected to arid conditions for extended periods, typically taking several weeks to complete. The transformation is extremely painful and arduous, with only a 10% success rate. During this time, the Ambystan's gills retract, their skin becomes tougher and more adapted to terrestrial life, and they gain enhanced physical resilience suited for survival on land.

#### **Race Traits**

**Ability Score Increase:** Constitution score increases by 2, and Wisdom score increases by 1.

**Age:** Ambystan reach maturity by age 15 and can live up to 120 years.

**Alignment:** Ambystan tend towards neutrality, with a strong respect for nature and life. They value community and harmony but can be fiercely protective of their homes.

**Size:** Ambystan are generally between 4 and 5 feet tall. Your size is Medium.

**Speed:** Ambystan base walking speed is 30 feet and have a swimming speed of 30 feet.

**Amphibious:** Ambystan can breathe air and water.

**Regenerative Abilities:** Ambystan have the ability to recover from injuries rapidly. As an action, they can spend one Hit Die to regain hit points as if you had taken a short rest. Additionally, they can regrow lost limbs over the course of a long rest.

**Gills and Lungs:** Ambystan have advantage on saving throws against gases and vapours, including inhaled poisons and diseases.

**Transformation (Drying):** If subjected to arid conditions for more than two weeks, Ambystan begin a transformation into a [[Caudati]]. This process is extremely painful and has only a 10% success rate. If successful, their Constitution score increases by an additional 1 (up to a maximum of 22), and they gain resistance to fire damage. They lose their swimming speed and ability to breathe underwater.

**Languages:** Ambystan can speak, read, and write Common and Aquan.

#### **Culture and Society**
Ambystan live in close-knit communities and hail originally from the waters of Mirrormere. They have a deep respect for the natural world and often act as guardians of their aquatic homes. Ambystan society is organised around family units and communal living, with decisions made through consensus.

#### **Notable Locations**
- **[[Neridia]]**: Shared with the [[Ondine]], Neridia is a significant location for the Ambystan, who occupy the deeper, darker parts of the sunken city, using their regenerative abilities to navigate the hazardous ruins. 
- **[[The Marshes]]**: A sprawling, wetland region where many Ambystan make their home. The Marshes are rich in biodiversity, offering both a habitat and a source of sustenance for the Ambystan communities. 
- **Marshlight Haven**: A town within [[The Marshes]] known for its bioluminescent flora and fauna. It serves as a center for trade and culture among the Ambystan.
- **[[Oasisvale]]**: A town nourished by the waters flowing from [[Veron]], Oasisvale is a unique settlement within the [[Umbral Plains]] where Ambystan and [[Caudati]] live together. The town is known for its lush greenery amidst the desert, providing a rare haven for both races.

#### **Notable Figures**

- **Larissa Wavecaller:** A respected elder and healer within the Ambystan community, known for her wisdom and deep connection to the waters of Neridia.
- **Korin Tidehunter:** A legendary warrior who defended the Ambystan from numerous threats, both aquatic and terrestrial. His bravery and strength are celebrated in Ambystan lore.
- **Sylra Moonshadow:** A skilled diplomat and negotiator, instrumental in maintaining the fragile peace between the various aquatic races of Neridia.

#### **Conclusion**
The Ambystan are a resilient and adaptable race, deeply intertwined with the aquatic environments they call home. Their unique ability to transform into [[Caudati]] under extreme conditions highlights their versatility and enduring spirit, making them a fascinating and integral part of the world of Madhen.
