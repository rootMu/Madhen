#### **Overview**
Caudati are the terrestrial form of the [[Ambystan]], resulting from a rare and torturous transformation known as the "Drying." These beastkin are known for their incredible resilience and adaptability, traits honed through their arduous transformation. The Caudati live in arid environments and are distinguished by their toughened skin and enhanced physical capabilities.

#### **Biology and Appearance**
Caudati bear a strong resemblance to their Ambystan origins, yet their physiology is adapted for life on land. They have tougher, scaled skin, often in shades of brown, red, or black, and lack the external gills characteristic of Ambystans. Their eyes remain large and expressive, but their bodies are more muscular and robust, reflecting their enhanced strength and resilience.

#### **Transformation Process: The Drying**
The transformation from Ambystan to Caudati, known as the "Drying," occurs when an Ambystan is subjected to arid conditions for an extended period, typically several weeks. This process is extremely painful and only has a 10% success rate. Those who survive the transformation gain significant physical adaptations, including resistance to fire and enhanced durability.

#### **Race Traits**

**Ability Score Increase:** Your Constitution score increases by 2, and your Strength score increases by 1.

**Age:** Caudati reach maturity by age 15 and can live up to 120 years, similar to their Ambystan counterparts.

**Alignment:** Caudati tend towards neutrality with a pragmatic view of the world. They value strength and survival, often prioritising community and resilience.

**Size:** Caudati are generally between 4 and 5 feet tall. Your size is Medium.

**Speed:** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.

**Fire Resistance:** You have resistance to fire damage, a trait gained from your transformation process.

**One in Ten:** Once per short rest, when you fail a saving throw, you can roll a d10 and add the number rolled to the saving throw, potentially turning a failure into a success.

**Toughened Skin:** Your tough, scaled skin provides you with a natural Armour Class of 13 + your Dexterity modifier.

**Survivor’s Resilience:** You have advantage on saving throws against exhaustion and effects that would cause you to become paralysed or stunned.

**Languages:** You can speak, read, and write Common and one additional language of your choice.

#### **Culture and Society**
Caudati live in small, close-knit communities, often in arid or semi-arid environments where their unique adaptations provide them with significant advantages. Their society values strength, endurance, and the ability to adapt to harsh conditions. Caudati often form protective enclaves, fiercely defending their territories and resources.

#### **Notable Locations** 
  
- **[[Veron]]**: The heart of the [[Umbral Plains]], Veron is a city where the Caudati thrive, protected by the powerful magic of their [[Vorenus||lich king]]. It is a center of commerce and refuge, drawing Caudati from across the desert. 
- **Sandwatch**: A fortified town on the edge of the [[Umbral Plains]], known for its role in guarding the borders and its vibrant market where goods from the desert and beyond are traded. 
- **Oasisvale**: A town nourished by the waters flowing from [[Veron]], Oasisvale is a unique settlement where Caudati and [[Ambystan]] live together. The town is known for its lush greenery amidst the desert, providing a rare haven for both races. 
- **Drywater**: Located deep within the [[Umbral Plains]], this town is a testament to Caudati resilience. Built around a rare underground spring, Drywater is a crucial waypoint for travellers and traders in the harsh desert environment.

#### **Notable Figures**

- **Thalor Sandstrider:** A legendary warrior known for leading his people through the harshest deserts and safeguarding them from numerous threats. His tactical genius and bravery are celebrated in Caudati lore.
- **Nerith Flameweaver:** A renowned healer and shaman, revered for her knowledge of desert flora and fauna, and her ability to harness the land’s natural resources for medicinal purposes.
- **Draxus Ironhide:** A formidable blacksmith and craftsman, known for creating some of the most resilient weapons and armour in the arid regions of [[Karth]]. His work is highly sought after by both Caudati and other races.

#### **Conclusion**
The Caudati are a testament to the power of resilience and adaptation. Their ability to endure and thrive in the harshest conditions, combined with their formidable physical capabilities, makes them a unique and integral part of the world of [[Madhen]]. Their transformation from [[Ambystan]], though rare and arduous, grants them unparalleled strength and a deep connection to their rugged environments.
