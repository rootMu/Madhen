### **Overview**
The Allspar Market is renowned as the greatest market in all of [[Ruban]], and perhaps even [[Madhen]]. Spanning the majority of Aurelian Heights and extending into the Commons, the market is a bustling hub of trade and commerce. The market spans over Ignis, Terra, and Aer Gates. The market is a vibrant tapestry of sights, sounds, and smells, with vendors from every corner of the world selling their wares.

### **Market Layout**
The Allspar Market is divided into several distinct sections, each offering a unique shopping experience. The market is designed to be navigable yet expansive, ensuring that visitors can easily find what they seek while also discovering new delights.

#### **[[The Grand Bazaar]]**
The heart of the market, The Grand Bazaar is where high-end goods and luxury items are sold. It features ornate stalls and pavilions, with merchants offering everything from fine silks and jewellery to rare spices and exotic pets.

#### **[[The Artisan Alley]]**
A haven for craftsmen and artisans, this section of the market showcases handmade goods and unique creations. Visitors can watch blacksmiths at work, commission a custom piece from a woodcarver, or purchase one-of-a-kind artwork.

#### **[[The Spice Row]]**
Bursting with colour and fragrance, The Spice Row is a sensory delight. Merchants here sell an array of spices, herbs, and teas from all over Ruban and beyond. It's a popular spot for both locals and visitors looking to add a new flavour to their cuisine.

#### **[[The Trade Quarter]]**
This section is where bulk goods and essential supplies are traded. It includes warehouses and large stalls where merchants negotiate deals on items such as grains, textiles, and raw materials.

#### **[[The Slave Markets]]**
Nestled within the bustling expanse of the Allspar Market, the Slave Markets form a grim and somber contrast to the vibrant atmosphere of the other sections. This area is dedicated to the sale, auction, and rental of slaves for various purposes, from manual labor to skilled craftsmanship. The Slave Markets are heavily guarded, with enforcers ensuring that the transactions proceed smoothly and without incident. Here, potential buyers can inspect the slaves, attend auctions, and negotiate rental agreements with the slave masters.

#### **[[The Curio Corner]]**
A treasure trove of curiosities and rare finds, The Curio Corner is where collectors and adventurers come to find magical artefacts, ancient relics, and other oddities. It’s a place of wonder and discovery, with each stall offering something different.

#### **[[The Food Court]]**
An array of food stalls and eateries, The Food Court is a culinary paradise. Visitors can sample dishes from all over the world, from street food to gourmet meals. It's the perfect place to take a break and enjoy a delicious meal.

### **Detailed Walkthrough**

#### **The Grand Bazaar**
Entering through the Ignis Gate, visitors are immediately greeted by the opulence of The Grand Bazaar. The area is filled with elegant tents and luxurious stalls, each one adorned with colourful banners and rich fabrics. The merchants here are keen negotiators, offering items that cater to the wealthy and discerning buyers. High-value goods like rare gemstones, fine wines, and exquisite garments are common.

#### **The Artisan Alley**
Walking west from The Grand Bazaar, one finds the Artisan Alley. This area is alive with the sound of hammers, saws, and looms. Artisans display their skills openly, often working on commissions right in their stalls. Items for sale include handcrafted jewellery, bespoke furniture, and intricate tapestries. The air is filled with the smell of fresh wood and molten metal, creating a truly immersive experience.

#### **The Spice Row**
Continuing north, visitors encounter The Spice Row. The stalls are brimming with sacks of spices and herbs, their scents mingling in the air. Brightly coloured powders and dried herbs hang from the awnings, and merchants offer samples to passersby. The Spice Row is not just for cooking; it also offers medicinal herbs and exotic teas that promise health and wellness.

#### **The Trade Quarter**
Further west lies The Trade Quarter, the industrial heart of the market. Here, large shipments of goods are bought and sold in bulk. The sounds of bargaining fill the air as traders haggle over prices for textiles, metals, and grains. The atmosphere is businesslike and efficient, with a sense of purpose and drive.

#### **[[The Slave Markets]]**
Entering the Slave Markets, visitors are met with a stark contrast to the lively atmosphere of the surrounding areas. Rows of cages and platforms are filled with individuals of various races and skills, awaiting their fate. On the left, the auction block draws crowds to witness and participate in the buying and selling of slaves. To the right, tents and offices house slave masters, where negotiations for renting skilled laborers take place. Heavily guarded and maintained with cold efficiency, the Slave Markets are a testament to the rigid control and harsh realities of the Gilded Dominion.

#### **The Curio Corner**
Turning south, one enters The Curio Corner. This area is a labyrinth of curiosities and treasures. Stalls overflow with ancient books, enchanted items, and peculiar artefacts. The vendors here are storytellers as much as merchants, each with a tale about the origins of their wares. This section attracts adventurers, scholars, and collectors, all searching for that one extraordinary find.

#### **The Food Court**
Finally, heading east, visitors reach The Food Court. A diverse array of food stalls offers culinary delights from every culture in Ruban. The air is thick with the smell of roasting meats, baking bread, and simmering spices. Long tables provide communal seating where people from all walks of life gather to enjoy their meals and share stories.

### **Specific Sections**

#### **The Grand Bazaar**
- **Silk Pavilion**: Known for its exquisite fabrics, the Silk Pavilion offers a vast selection of silks, brocades, and velvets. Custom tailoring is also available.
- **Gem Emporium**: This stall boasts a dazzling array of gemstones and jewellery. Rare stones like star sapphires and fire opals are among the highlights.
- **Exotic Pet Stall**: Offering a variety of rare and exotic animals, this stall is a favourite among nobles looking for unique companions.

#### **The Artisan Alley**
- **Blacksmith's Forge**: Visitors can watch the blacksmith create weapons and tools. Custom orders are welcome, and repairs are done on the spot.
- **Woodcarver's Workshop**: Known for its beautifully carved furniture and sculptures, this workshop offers items that are both functional and artistic.
- **Artist's Studio**: A place for painters and sculptors to display and sell their work. Commissions for portraits and statues are common.

#### **The Spice Row**
- **Herbalist's Haven**: Specialising in medicinal herbs and teas, this stall is a go-to for those seeking natural remedies.
- **Exotic Spices**: Offering spices from across the world, this stall is a feast for the senses. Saffron, cardamom, and cinnamon are just a few of the offerings.
- **Tea Merchant**: A variety of teas are available here, from everyday blends to rare and expensive brews.

#### **The Trade Quarter**
- **Grain Merchant**: Selling bulk grains and cereals, this merchant caters to bakers, brewers, and large households.
- **Textile Trader**: Offering bolts of fabric and raw textiles, this trader deals in everything from cotton to silk.
- **Metal Supplier**: A key source for blacksmiths and artisans, this supplier offers raw and processed metals.

#### **The Curio Corner**
- **Antiquarian's Nook**: Specialising in ancient artefacts and relics, this stall is a treasure trove for historians and collectors.
- **Enchanter's Booth**: Offering magical items and enchantments, this booth is popular among adventurers seeking an edge.
- **Oddities Emporium**: A place for strange and unusual items, from shrunken heads to enchanted mirrors.

#### **[[The Slave Markets]]**

- **The Auction Block**: The centrepiece of the Slave Markets, where slaves are presented and bid upon by buyers.
- **The Slaver Tents**: Where negotiations for the rental of skilled labourers take place, and agreements are made.
- **Holding Pens**: Rows of cages and platforms where slaves are kept before being sold or rented.
- **Enforcer Stations**: Heavily guarded posts ensuring the smooth operation of the Slave Markets and maintaining order.

#### **The Food Court**
- **Grill Master's Stall**: Known for its succulent meats and savoury skewers, this stall is a favourite for hearty meals.
- **Baker's Delight**: Offering a wide variety of breads, pastries, and cakes, this stall is a must-visit for those with a sweet tooth.
- **Spice Vendor**: Specialising in spiced dishes, this vendor offers a taste of exotic cuisine with every bite.

### **Conclusion**
The Allspar Market is more than just a place of commerce; it is a vibrant, living entity that reflects the diversity and richness of Ruban. Whether seeking luxury goods, unique crafts, or everyday essentials, visitors are sure to find what they need in this bustling market. The Allspar Market stands as a testament to the city's prosperity and cultural tapestry, drawing people from all walks of life to its bustling stalls.
