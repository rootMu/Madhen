### **Cetus: The City-Smasher**

#### **Overview**
Cetus, known as the City-Smasher, is an ancient kraken whose origins trace back to the [[Timeline of Madhen's Divine Eras#**The Epoch of Dominion ED (Approx. 100 BES - 8000 BES)**||Epoch of Dominion]], a period marked by the rule of the [[Lesser Deities]]. This monstrous being is notorious for its sheer size, immense power, and the trail of destruction it has left across the ages. Known for attacking coastal settlements and sinking ships, Cetus is a name that instills fear in the hearts of sailors and coastal dwellers alike.

#### **Historical Impact**
Cetus is believed to have been born during the [[Timeline of Madhen's Divine Eras#**The Epoch of Dominion ED (Approx. 100 BES - 8000 BES)**||Epoch of Dominion]], as inferred from the destruction of cities ruled by Lesser Deities. Among the notable devastations attributed to Cetus are two ancient cities in [[Misthaven]] and one in the region south of present-day [[Veron]], now covered by the great desert of the [[Umbral Plains]].

- **Destruction in Misthaven:** Cetus is believed to have annihilated two thriving cities in Misthaven, Talrenn and Veylanthe, erasing them from the annals of history. The ruins of these cities, now buried beneath layers of earth and vegetation, serve as silent witnesses to the kraken's wrath.
- **The Great Desert's Origin:** South of [[Veron]], an ancient city once stood where the great desert now lies. Legends suggest that a massive tidal wave, caused by Cetus, submerged the city in water and sand, leading to its ultimate ruin and the formation of the desert. The city in the now [[Umbral Plains]] is known from the constant relics that keep being found in the desert and ruins of areas and ancient tombs believed to be part of the once great city, which was vast and spread to the coast.

#### **Physical Description**
Cetus is a gargantuan kraken, its body covered in dark, leathery skin that blends seamlessly with the ocean depths. Its many tentacles are long and powerful, capable of crushing ships and pulling them into the abyss. Its eyes glow with a malevolent intelligence, reflecting the ancient and terrible wisdom it possesses.

- **Size:** Cetus is colossal, with a body length of over 350 feet and tentacles that can extend even further.
- **Colour:** Dark, almost black, with a glistening sheen that makes it nearly invisible in the deep waters.
- **Eyes:** Glowing with a haunting blue light, filled with ancient malice.

#### **Abilities**
Cetus possesses immense physical strength and numerous supernatural abilities that make it a formidable foe.

- **Tidal Wave Generation:** Cetus can create massive tidal waves capable of inundating coastal cities, as evidenced by the destruction of the ancient city south of Veron.
- **Regeneration:** Cetus has a remarkable regenerative ability, allowing it to heal from wounds that would be fatal to other creatures.

#### **Cultural Significance**
Cetus has become a central figure in the folklore and myths of coastal communities in Karth. Stories of its attacks and the devastation it has wrought are passed down through generations, serving as cautionary tales for sailors and adventurers.

- **Legendary Tales:** Many tales describe heroes and adventurers attempting to slay Cetus, though none have succeeded. The kraken's legend continues to grow with each retelling.
- **Rituals and Offerings:** Coastal communities often hold rituals and offer sacrifices to appease Cetus and protect themselves from its wrath.

#### **Current Status**
Cetus's current whereabouts are unknown, but its legend persists. Some believe it still roams the depths of the [[Cerulean Sea]], while others claim it has retreated to the darkest abysses of the ocean, waiting for the right moment to resurface.

### **Conclusion**
Cetus is a formidable and ancient creature whose legacy of destruction spans millennia. Its origins in the [[Timeline of Madhen's Divine Eras#**The Epoch of Dominion ED (Approx. 100 BES - 8000 BES)**||Epoch of Dominion]] and the tales of its wrath have cemented its place as one of the most feared and revered monsters in the history of [[Madhen]]. As a living embodiment of ancient chaos, Cetus remains a subject of fascination and dread in equal measure.
