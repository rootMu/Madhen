title: Strakh, God of Chains

### **Overview**
Power and domination are revered, epitomised in the worship of Strakh, the mythical God of Chains. The culture is characterised by brutal gladiatorial combats as both public spectacle and religious ceremony, underscoring the Dominion’s values. Strakh is an Old God, worshipped in this very land long before the current era. The city of [[Strakhaven]] itself was reformed after his defeat in ancient wars and has stood in one form or another ever since.

### **Attributes**
- **Domains**: Domination, Control, Strength
- **Symbols**: Chains, Shackles, Iron Fist
- **Favoured Weapon**: Whip, Chains
- **Followers**: Slavers, Gladiators, Enforcers

### **Worship and Practices**
Worship of Strakh is deeply ingrained in the societal structure of the Gilded Dominion. Followers of Strakh believe in the absolute power of strength and the inevitability of domination. His altars are adorned with chains and symbols of control, and his priests are often seen as enforcers of his will.

#### **Rituals**
- **Chain Binding Ceremony**: A ritual where new followers are bound in chains, symbolising their submission to Strakh and his will.
- **Gladiatorial Combats**: Public spectacles where warriors fight to the death, serving as both entertainment and a religious ceremony to honour Strakh.
- **[[Iron Fist Trials]]**: Challenges where followers must prove their strength and dominance to ascend in the ranks of Strakh's devotees.

### **Influence in Strakhaven**
In Strakhaven, Strakh's influence is palpable. The city’s laws and order are heavily influenced by the doctrine of power and domination. The brutal gladiatorial combats held in the grand arena are not just for public amusement but are seen as holy rites that please Strakh and ensure his favour. Despite his ancient defeat, Strakh's presence remains strong, with his worship continuing to shape the city's culture and governance.

### **Clergy and Temples**
The clergy of Strakh are known as [[Chainmasters]], individuals who have dedicated their lives to enforcing the will of Strakh. They wear garments adorned with chains and carry whips or chain weapons as a sign of their devotion. Temples dedicated to Strakh are fortified structures, often resembling prisons or fortresses, symbolising the god's dominion over all.

### **Conclusion**
Strakh, the God of Chains, embodies the harsh and unyielding principles of power and control that define the [[Gilded Dominion]]. His worship through brutal gladiatorial combats and strict rituals reflects the society’s values and the ever-present belief in the survival of the fittest. Despite his ancient defeat, Strakh's legacy endures, influencing the very fabric of Strakhaven.
