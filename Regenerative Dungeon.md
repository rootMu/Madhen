## Overview

**Regenerative Dungeons** are a rare and dangerous classification of dungeon known for their unique ability to renew themselves over time. Unlike typical dungeons, where once-cleared areas remain safe, regenerative dungeons continuously spawn new threats, reset traps, and even generate new treasures. This makes them both highly sought after and incredibly perilous for adventurers. The magical energies or ancient technologies that power these dungeons are not fully understood, but they are believed to be connected to deep, primordial forces that predate even the oldest known civilisations.

The most distinctive feature of regenerative dungeons is their ability to produce **[[Primordial Relics]]**—artefacts that appear spontaneously within the dungeon, bearing no signs of traditional craftsmanship or magical creation. These relics are highly coveted due to their rarity and the powerful, often unpredictable, abilities they bestow.

## Notable Regenerative Dungeons

### The Whispering Hollow - Karth (Northwest of Ardenbridge)

Located in the pastoral fields two days' ride from the northernmost city of Ardenshire and one day’s ride northwest of [[Ardenbridge]], the **[[Whispering Hollow]]** is a sprawling subterranean network of tunnels and chambers hidden beneath rolling hills. The dungeon is renowned for the constant, eerie whispers that seem to emanate from the very walls, unsettling even the most experienced adventurers. These whispers are believed to be the echoes of long-dead spirits, trapped within the dungeon's regenerative cycle. The Whispering Hollow is filled with shifting tunnels, elusive creatures, and ancient artefacts that regenerate over time, making it a favoured destination for those seeking both challenge and treasure.

### The Drowned Bastion - The Hinterlands (Off the East Coast)

Off the east coast of the Hinterlands lies the **[[Drowned Bastion]]**, a once-great fortress that sank beneath the waves long ago. Submerged under the ocean, this regenerative dungeon has transformed into a labyrinth of coral-encrusted ruins, teeming with aquatic life and treacherous currents. Despite being under the sea, the Drowned Bastion continually regenerates its dangers and treasures, drawing adventurers who are willing to brave its depths. Navigating the Bastion requires both skill and specialised equipment, as the dungeon’s layout shifts with the tides, revealing new paths and hidden chambers filled with waterlogged treasures.

### The Abyss of Sorrow - Ruban (Beneath the City of Corva)

Beneath the bustling city of [[Corva]] on the continent of Ruban lies the **[[Abyss of Sorrow]]**, a regenerative dungeon that intertwines with the very foundations of the city. This dungeon is a labyrinth of forgotten tunnels, ancient catacombs, and cursed chambers, all regenerating their traps and guardians with unsettling regularity. The Abyss is notorious for its ability to shift suddenly, with entire sections collapsing or reconfiguring without warning, making it one of the most dangerous dungeons in Ruban. Despite the risks, the Abyss of Sorrow remains a tempting challenge for adventurers seeking to uncover its dark secrets and the powerful [[Primordial Relics]] that lie within.

### The Sunken Vault - Veron (Beneath the Living Waters District)

Located beneath the [[Living Waters District]] of [[Veron]], the **[[Sunken Vault]]** is the last accessible part of an ancient dungeon that once spanned the entire city. Unlike other parts of the dungeon, which were sealed off by [[Vorenus|King Vorenus]], the Sunken Vault remains open to adventurers, though it is heavily guarded and monitored. This regenerative dungeon is known for its constantly shifting layout, resetting traps, and the presence of powerful [[Primordial Relics]]. The deeper one ventures into the Vault, the more dangerous it becomes, with many sections still uncharted and filled with ancient guardians. The Sunken Vault is a popular destination for those looking to test their skills, gather wealth, and uncover the secrets that lie within its depths.

## Classification and Features

Regenerative dungeons are classified based on their ability to constantly reset and renew themselves. These dungeons are believed to be powered by ancient magics or technologies that remain active despite the passage of time. Common features of regenerative dungeons include:

- **Resilient Creatures:** Monsters within regenerative dungeons are not only numerous but also remarkably resilient. They often reappear even after being defeated, with some showing adaptations to previous encounters.
- **Resetting Traps:** Traps within these dungeons are a constant threat, as they reset themselves after a short period. Adventurers must remain vigilant, as areas they previously cleared can become deadly once again.
- **Shifting Layouts:** The internal structure of regenerative dungeons can change, with new passages opening up and old ones closing off. This makes mapping these dungeons a challenge, as no two expeditions are ever the same.
- **Primordial Relics:** Unique to regenerative dungeons, these artefacts appear out of thin air, with no clear origin or maker. They are highly prized for their powerful and often unpredictable effects.

Regenerative dungeons are considered among the most challenging and rewarding locations for adventurers in all of [[Madhen]], offering both great riches and great risks to those who dare to enter.
