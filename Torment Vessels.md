These jars are filled with a hallucinogenic liquid that sustains the host without the need for food or water. Within the liquid are tiny parasites that invade the body, feeding off the host's essence. These parasites are used to transfer skills and abilities to the hosts. However, if the recipient is resistant or unable to withstand the mental and physical strain, the process warps their mind and body, transforming them into grotesque homunculi.

The host's body is gradually broken down by the parasites, which infuse themselves into the very essence of the host. Over time, one parasite will dominate, consuming the original host's will and emerging as a horrific, misshapen creature. These grotesque horrors retain little of their former selves, their minds and bodies irrevocably altered by the torment they endured.
