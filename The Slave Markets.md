#### The Slave Markets

As you traverse the [[Allspar Market]], the atmosphere shifts palpably when you enter the Slave Markets. The lively chatter and the aromatic scents of the nearby Spice Row give way to a more subdued and tense environment. Rows of cages and platforms line the streets, each filled with individuals of various races and skills, all awaiting their fate. The air is thick with the presence of enforcers and the occasional bark of a slaver overseeing the proceedings.

To the left, a raised platform serves as the auction block, where slaves are brought forth, presented, and bid upon by interested buyers. The auctions are a spectacle in themselves, drawing crowds who come to witness the transactions, whether out of genuine interest or morbid curiosity. To the right, several tents and makeshift offices house the slave masters, where negotiations for the rental of skilled labourers take place. Here, merchants and landowners discuss terms and agreements for the temporary use of slaves for their enterprises.

Despite the oppressive nature of the Slave Markets, there is a sense of order and efficiency. The transactions are conducted swiftly and with a cold professionalism, reflecting the market's role in the broader economy of [[Strakhaven]]. This section of the market, though somber, is a testament to the rigid control and unyielding structure that defines the Gilded Dominion.

### **Overview**
The Slave Markets, nestled within the expansive Allspar Market, serve as a stark contrast to the vibrant and lively atmosphere of the surrounding areas. This section of the market is dedicated to the sale, auction, and rental of slaves for various purposes, ranging from manual labor to skilled craftsmanship. The Slave Markets are heavily guarded, with enforcers ensuring that transactions proceed smoothly and without incident. This part of the market, though somber, reflects the rigid control and unyielding structure that defines the Gilded Dominion.

### **Layout**
The Slave Markets are organised to maximise efficiency and order, ensuring that all transactions are conducted swiftly and professionally.

#### **The Auction Block**
The centerpiece of the Slave Markets, the Auction Block is a raised platform where slaves are presented and bid upon by buyers. Auctions are held regularly and draw large crowds, both of genuine buyers and curious onlookers.

#### **The Slaver Tents**
These tents serve as the offices for slave masters and traders. Inside, negotiations for the rental of skilled laborers are conducted. Here, merchants and landowners can discuss terms and agreements for the temporary use of slaves for their enterprises.

#### **Holding Pens**
Rows of cages and platforms are filled with individuals of various races and skills, awaiting their fate. Slaves are kept here before being sold or rented out, under the watchful eyes of enforcers.

#### **Enforcer Stations**
These heavily guarded posts ensure the smooth operation of the Slave Markets and maintain order. Enforcers are always present to prevent any disturbances and to enforce the strict regulations governing the slave trade.
