### **Overview**
Strakhaven is the capital city of the [[Gilded Dominion]], a nation infamous for its entrenched slave trade and rigid caste system. The city serves as the epicentre of economic, cultural, and political activities within the Dominion.

### **Geography**
Strakhaven is located on the northeastern coast of Ruban, characterised by lush coastal plains and stark, rocky interiors. The city is divided into two primary areas:

- **The Commons**: This outer district is bustling with commerce and home to workers, merchants, and smaller-scale slavers.
- **Aurelian Heights**: The luxurious inner city, separated from the Commons by a formidable pentagonal wall. Each gate of Aurelian Heights is guarded by a powerful elemental spirit.

### **Significant Locations**

#### **The Commons**
- **Slave Markets**: Numerous markets where slaves are traded daily.
- **Dockyards**: Busy ports where slaving ships and trade vessels constantly come and go.

#### **Aurelian Heights**
- **[[Allspar Market]]**: Renowned for its assortment of exotic and magical items, including rare meteorite metals and djinni-crafted wares.
- **[[The Crucible of Champions]]**: The most prestigious gladiatorial arena in Strakhaven, hosting grand spectacles and high-stakes battles.
- **The Citadel of Chains**: The fortress of Marvolo the Chainweaver, where he conducts his brutal operations and psychological manipulation.
- **The Velvet Rose** is the most prestigious establishment in Aurelian Heights, renowned not just as a high-class pleasure house but as a sanctuary where all manner of desires and requests can be fulfilled—provided the fee is right. Under the careful management of [[Lady Aurelia]], the Velvet Rose maintains an atmosphere of elegance and discretion. Its opulent interiors are designed to cater to the sophisticated tastes of its elite clientele, ensuring that while anything might happen behind closed doors, the establishment's reputation for class and dignity remains untarnished.

### **City Gates and Wall**
Strakhaven is famously protected by a pentagonal wall that is both extremely high and thick, composed of an arcane-negating stone known as **[[Umbra Stone]]**. 

- **Ignis Gate**: Guarded by a fire elemental, perpetually glowing with a fiery spirit.
- **Terra Gate**: Reinforced with earthen magic, guarded by a stoic earth elemental.
- **Aer Gate**: Guarded by a whirling air elemental, enveloped in a constant breeze.
- **Aqua Gate**: Watched over by a serene water elemental, shimmering with a liquid aura.
- **Entropy Gate**: Protected by an elemental of chaos, this gate shifts and distorts, symbolising the volatile and unpredictable nature of its guardian.

Along the top of the pentagonal wall are alchemical ballistae and magic throwers, ready to repel any attempts to scale the wall. These defences are manned by highly trained [[Spell Slingers]] who ensure that no unauthorised individuals gain entry to Aurelian Heights.

### **Historical Figures**
- **Lord Theodric Ravenshadow**: The founder of Strakhaven, who established the city as the capital of the Gilded Dominion.
- **Lady Vespera Aurelian**: A powerful mage who fortified Aurelian Heights and created the elemental gates.
- **General Horatio Dreadnought**: A military leader known for his brutal tactics and expansion of the Dominion's territory.

### **Notable Figures**
- **Marvolo the Chainweaver**: A prominent slaver in Strakhaven, known for his exceptional control and management of slaves. Publicly perceived as a leader who maintains order within the city's complex hierarchy, Marvolo's methods and authority make him a respected and influential figure.
- **Selene Ravenshadow**: A prominent figure in the [[Allspar Market]], dealing in rare and magical artefacts. She is a direct descendant of Lord Theodric Ravenshadow, the founder of Strakhaven. Her expertise and vast network make her a key player in the city's thriving trade of enchanted items.
- **Lysandra the Gladiatrix**: A famed Gladiator who has never been defeated in the Crucible of Champions.
- **[[Gertrude Hardwick || Madam Gertrude "Gertie" Hardwick]]**: As part of Strakhaven's general security, Gertie is responsible for maintaining law and order, overseeing the training of new recruits, and managing high-risk prisoners.

### **Clothing and Fashion**
In Strakhaven, clothing signifies one's status and wealth. The upper classes in [[Aurelian Heights]] wear luxurious fabrics such as silks and velvets, often adorned with gold and precious stones. Fashion is heavily influenced by ancient Rubanian styles, with flowing robes, intricate embroidery, and elaborate headdresses. In contrast, the lower classes in the [[Commons]] wear practical, durable clothing made from wool and linen, with simple tunics and trousers.

### **Festivals and Traditions**
- **The Festival of Chains**: A grand event celebrating Strakhaven's history and its dominance in the slave trade. The festival features parades, gladiatorial games, and public feasts.
- **The Elemental Conclave**: A biannual gathering where the elemental spirits of the city gates are honoured with rituals, performances, and offerings.
- **Market Day**: Held every month in the [[Allspar Market]], showcasing the city's finest goods and attracting traders from across Ruban.

### **Guilds and Associations**
- **The Slavers' Guild**: The most powerful guild in Strakhaven, overseeing the capture, trade, and management of slaves.
- **The Merchant's Consortium**: A collective of influential traders who control the flow of goods into and out of the city.
- **The Elemental Circle**: A group of mages dedicated to maintaining the elemental gates and studying elemental magic.
- **The Gladiators' Brotherhood**: An association of gladiators and trainers who organise events in the Crucible of Champions and ensure the welfare of their members.

### **Conclusion**
Strakhaven stands as a testament to the might and control of the [[Gilded Dominion]], its grandeur marred by the oppressive practices that sustain it. The city’s blend of opulence and brutality creates a unique, if controversial, cultural and economic hub. Through its historical legacies, notable figures, and vibrant traditions, Strakhaven continues to play a central role in the story of Ruban, a place where power and wealth reign supreme, often at the cost of humanity and freedom.
