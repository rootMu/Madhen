Located between [[Karth]] and [[Ruban]] the Arkadian Expanse is a vast ocean, it is home to the sea fairing nation the [[Krial]]. Violent storms and maelstroms plague the Expanse making it impossible to navigate safely by anyone other than the [[Krial]] for reasons unknown. Because of this intercontinental trade is handled solely by the [[Krial]] with very little personal travel allowed except under strict supervision.
