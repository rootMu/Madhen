#### **Overview**
Oasisvale, known as the Jewel of the [[Umbral Plains]], is a thriving oasis town located in the heart of the vast desert. It is a beacon of life amidst the arid expanse, offering refuge and sustenance to travellers and residents alike. The town is built around a series of natural springs that form lush, green oases, making it a critical hub for trade and sustenance in the region.

#### **Geography**
Oasisvale is situated in the Umbral Plains, surrounded by miles of barren desert. The town itself is an oasis, with fresh water springs feeding into a network of streams and ponds. The area around Oasisvale is dotted with palm groves and fertile plots of land where various crops are cultivated. The outskirts feature smaller settlements and huts that house the local population and travellers.

#### **Economy**
The economy of Oasisvale revolves around trade, agriculture, and tourism. The fertile land around the oases supports the growth of date palms, citrus fruits, and various vegetables, which are traded in the bustling markets. The town is also a major stop for caravans traversing the desert, making it a vibrant center of commerce. Additionally, the natural beauty and tranquility of Oasisvale attract visitors seeking respite from the harsh desert environment.

#### **Culture and Community**
The residents of Oasisvale are known for their hospitality and resourcefulness. The town hosts several annual festivals celebrating the abundance of water and the fertility of the land. These events include music, dance, and communal feasts, reflecting the close-knit nature of the community. The town's culture is a blend of influences from the diverse groups that pass through, creating a rich tapestry of traditions and practices.

#### **Significant Locations**

- **The Verdant Springs**: The heart of Oasisvale, these natural springs provide the lifeblood of the town. They are a popular gathering spot for locals and visitors alike.
- **The Luminous Market**: A bustling market where traders from across the region come to buy and sell goods. The market is known for its vibrant atmosphere and wide variety of products, including rare spices, textiles, and handcrafted items.
- **The Tranquil Gardens**: A series of carefully cultivated gardens that showcase the diverse flora of the region. The gardens are a popular spot for relaxation and reflection.
- **The Whispering Palms**: A renowned inn located near the central oasis, offering luxurious accommodations and a peaceful retreat for travellers.
- **The Temple of Selune**: A place of worship dedicated to the moon goddess Selune, providing spiritual solace and guidance to the townspeople.

#### **Notable Figures**

- **Eldara Windrunner**: The town's mayor, known for her wisdom and dedication to the well-being of the community. She is a skilled diplomat and trader.
- **Khalid Sunseeker**: A prominent merchant who controls much of the trade in Oasisvale. He is known for his vast network of contacts and his ability to procure rare and valuable goods.
- **Mira Watersong**: A healer and herbalist whose knowledge of local plants and natural remedies is unmatched. She runs the town's apothecary and provides care to those in need.
- **Thorin Stonehand**: A master blacksmith who crafts tools and weapons for the townsfolk and visiting traders. His work is highly sought after for its quality and durability.

#### **Conclusion**
Oasisvale stands as a testament to human ingenuity and resilience, a thriving community in the heart of the desert. Its strategic location, rich culture, and abundant resources make it a vital hub in the Umbral Plains. Whether for trade, rest, or exploration, Oasisvale offers a unique and welcoming destination for all who venture into its verdant embrace.
