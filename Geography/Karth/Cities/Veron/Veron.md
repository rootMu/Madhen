### Cultural and Physical Description of Veron

#### Urban Design and Architecture
Often referred to as the Necropolis, veron is a marvel of mystical and ancient architecture. The cityscape is characterised by towering spires and arching gateways made from obsidian and dark granites mined from the nearby [[Umbral Plains]]. These structures are enchanted to absorb light during the day and emit a soft, ethereal glow at night, illuminating the streets with an otherworldly ambiance. [[Vorenus|King Vorenus's]] palace, originally a subterranean dungeon, has been elevated to serve as the city's majestic centre, with its labyrinthine halls and grandiose columns adorned with arcane carvings that tell tales of Veron's genesis and transformation.

#### Flora, Fauna, and Ecosystems 
Veron's environment defies its desert surroundings, thriving with lush greenery and vibrant ecosystems thanks to the enchanted rivers sculpted by [[Vorenus|King Vorenus]]. Exotic orchids and thick moss carpets dominate the landscape, supported by magical waters that encourage growth and vitality. The rivers are lined with silver-leafed trees and luminescent fungi, creating habitats for spectral birds and glowing aquatic life. This blend of flora and fauna contributes to a biologically rich and aesthetically stunning environment, making Veron a green jewel amid the barren Umbral Plains.

#### Demographics and Social Structure
Veron's populace is a blend of the living and the reanimated, creating a unique cultural tapestry. Upon death, citizens are ritually transformed into servile undead, who then support the city in various capacities—maintaining its infrastructure, performing routine tasks, and ensuring its defences. This practice is both a cultural norm and a form of eternal service to the city, preserving knowledge and skills across generations. The living, free from daily toils, primarily engage in intellectual pursuits, arts, and magical research, fostering a society rich in education and innovation.

#### Cultural Festivities and Rituals
Veron is renowned for its vibrant cultural scene, highlighted by the "Festival of Eternal Waters." This festival symbolises the city's rebirth and the perpetual cycle of life and death. Residents celebrate with night-long vigils, floating lanterns on the rivers, and recitations of poetic eulogies. Rituals during the festival include the "Dance of Renewal," performed by both the living and the undead, symbolising the unity and eternal bond of the community. The festival culminates in the "Rite of the Wandering Waters," where families cast biodegradable effigies down the river, carrying messages and wishes to the spirits of their ancestors.

#### Leisure and Intellectual Life
In Veron, leisure activities often involve the arts, from grand theatrical productions performed in open-air amphitheaters to extensive libraries housing ancient tomes and recent works. The city's Academy of Arcane Arts is a hub of magical study, attracting scholars from various disciplines who wish to delve into the deep mysteries of necromancy and elemental magics. Chess-like strategic games, involving real-time spell casting and counter-moves, are also popular among the citizens, reflecting their love for tactics and strategy.

#### Undead Workforce and Military
The undead workforce of Veron performs tirelessly, executing tasks with precision and diligence, guided by the more sentient undead who were once scholars and mages. The military, composed entirely of undead warriors, is formidable. These eternal soldiers are led by ancient generals reborn into unyielding service, making Veron's defences nearly impregnable. Regular drills and magical enhancements keep the army well-prepared for any external threats.

#### Ecosystems of Water and Greenery
The magical rivers of Veron not only provide water but also act as the lifeline of the city's ecosystems. These waters, rich with life-giving magic, have created an abundant biodiversity within the city limits and contribute to the health of the surrounding desert, turning potential wastelands into fertile grounds at the city's fringes.

#### Interaction with the Surrounding Desert
While the Umbral Plains offer a stark contrast to the lushness of Veron, they are not barren of life or interest. The desert is rich with minerals and ancient relics buried beneath its sands, frequently explored by Veron's scholars and adventurers. These expeditions often seek to uncover lost magics and ancient histories, further enriching Veron's extensive archives and knowledge base, and occasionally bringing back artefacts that enhance the city's magical reservoirs or serve as curiosities in the city's many museums.

### Geopolitical Description of Veron

#### Isolationist Policies and Diplomatic Relations
Veron, under the rule of [[Vorenus|King Vorenus]], maintains a largely isolationist stance, reflecting its sovereign's inherent mistrust of the external world. The city's interactions with outside nations are minimal and highly controlled. Trade agreements are rare, typically reserved for a select few external entities that have proven their worth and reliability over many decades. These relationships are governed by strict protocols that prioritise the security and self-sufficiency of Veron above all else.

The city-state does not maintain traditional embassies; instead, it employs a network of envoys who serve as both spies and diplomats. These envoys discreetly monitor political shifts and gather intelligence, ensuring Veron remains well-prepared to defend its interests and sovereignty. Such measures reflect the King's strategic, cautious approach to external relations, designed to minimise risk and avoid unnecessary entanglements.

#### Petitions for Residency
Veron's reputation as a lush oasis of magic and advanced culture attracts many who wish to reside within its walls. However, the process to gain residency is rigorous and designed to deter all but the most dedicated and suitable candidates. Potential residents must undergo extensive screening for their background, intentions, and potential contributions to the city. The criteria are stringent, with a focus on skills in magic, scholarship, or arts that align with Veron's cultural and intellectual ethos.

Petitioners often spend years providing evidence of their worthiness, undergoing interviews and tests to assess their compatibility with Veron’s values and needs. The screening process also includes magical assessments to ensure no ill intentions or hidden agendas threaten the city’s safety or secrecy.

#### Trade and Economic Policies 
Given its self-sufficient nature, Veron engages in limited trade, primarily exporting magical artefacts, enchanted flora, and scholarly works that are highly prized across the continent. In return, it imports rare materials, books, and artefacts that further its research and magical prowess. This selective trading policy ensures that the city remains an epicentre of arcane knowledge and magical innovation while protecting its resources from external exploitation.

Trade caravans and merchant ships allowed into Veron are subject to thorough inspections, and their crews are closely monitored during their stay. Such measures, while seen as draconian by some, have ensured that Veron remains untouched by the chaos and conflict that often plague other regions.

#### Military Strategy and Defence 
Veron’s military strategy is as much about deterrence as it is about defence. The city’s formidable undead army serves as a powerful deterrent to potential aggressors, backed by mystical defences woven into the city’s architecture. The strategic use of the city’s environment and magical constructs further fortifies Veron, making any thoughts of invasion unfeasible for would-be conquerors.

Moreover, the city’s isolationist stance and the mystique surrounding its magical prowess add to its impenetrability, creating an aura of invincibility. Few outsiders know the true extent of Veron’s military capabilities, and King Vorenus intends to keep it that way, shrouding his city in secrecy to safeguard its people and preserve its sanctity.
