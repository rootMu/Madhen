## Overview

**The Verdant Blades** is a renowned adventurers' guild based in [[Veron]], with its headquarters located in the [[Bazaar of the Bold]]. Known for their bravery, camaraderie, and dedication to the pursuit of knowledge and treasure, the Verdant Blades are a respected and influential force in the city. The guild specialises in expeditions into the [[Sunken Vault]] and other dangerous locations, often taking on missions that others deem too perilous. Their emblem, a sword entwined with ivy, symbolises their connection to nature and their unwavering resolve in the face of danger.

## History

The Verdant Blades were founded several decades ago by a group of veteran adventurers who sought to create a community where like-minded individuals could share resources, knowledge, and support. The guild quickly grew in reputation due to the success of their early expeditions into the Sunken Vault, where they uncovered numerous artefacts and treasures. Over time, the Verdant Blades became known not only for their skill and bravery but also for their commitment to preserving the natural world and protecting the people of Veron.

## Structure and Leadership

The Verdant Blades operate under a loose hierarchy, with experienced members taking on leadership roles to guide and mentor newer recruits. The guild is currently led by [[Talia Windrunner]], a seasoned adventurer known for her strategic mind and leadership abilities. Under her guidance, the Verdant Blades have continued to thrive, taking on increasingly challenging missions and expanding their influence within Veron.

### Leadership Roles

- **Guild Leader:** [[Talia Windrunner]] - Oversees all guild activities, makes strategic decisions, and represents the guild in dealings with other factions.
- **Expedition Coordinator:** [[Galen Stormrider]] - Plans and organises expeditions, ensuring that each mission is properly staffed and equipped.
- **Recruitment Officer:** [[Elara Quickstrike]] - Scouts and recruits new members, assessing their skills and ensuring they are a good fit for the guild.

## Membership

Membership in the Verdant Blades is open to adventurers of all backgrounds, though recruits must pass an initial skills assessment and interview conducted by [[Talia Windrunner]] or [[Elara Quickstrike]]. Once accepted, members gain access to a range of resources, including training, mission assignments, and the use of the guild’s facilities at the [[Verdant Blades Guildhall]]. The guild places a strong emphasis on mutual support, with more experienced members often mentoring newcomers.

### Member Benefits

- **Access to Missions:** Members can take on a variety of missions, ranging from exploration and treasure hunting to protection and retrieval tasks.
- **Training:** The guild offers combat and survival training to help members hone their skills.
- **Equipment and Resources:** Members receive discounts on gear and supplies, and have access to the guild’s inventory of magical items and artefacts.
- **Community:** The guild fosters a strong sense of camaraderie, with regular gatherings and events that bring members together.

## Values and Ethics

The Verdant Blades are guided by a code of conduct that emphasises honour, bravery, and respect for nature. Members are expected to conduct themselves with integrity and to support their fellow adventurers. The guild also places a high value on the preservation of natural and magical environments, often taking on missions that involve protecting endangered areas or creatures.

### Code of Conduct

- **Honour:** Members must act with integrity, upholding the guild’s reputation.
- **Bravery:** Adventurers are expected to face danger with courage and determination.
- **Respect for Nature:** The guild encourages the preservation of natural and magical environments, and members are urged to avoid unnecessary harm to the world around them.
- **Camaraderie:** The Verdant Blades is a community where members support each other, sharing knowledge, resources, and encouragement.

## Notable Achievements

- **The Expedition to Threnaliath:** One of the guild’s most famous missions, where they uncovered a lost artefact from the [[Lost Dungeon of Threnaliath]] and safely returned it to Veron, earning the guild significant acclaim.
- **The Defence of the Verdant Glade:** The guild successfully defended a sacred grove from a band of marauding bandits, preserving its ancient magical properties.
- **The Mapping of the Lower Vaults:** The Verdant Blades were the first to chart a significant portion of the lower levels of the Sunken Vault, providing invaluable information for future expeditions.

## Affiliations

The Verdant Blades maintain positive relationships with several other factions and groups within Veron, including:

- **[[Enchanter’s Conclave]]:** The guild often collaborates with the Conclave for the identification and certification of artefacts recovered during missions.
- **Brightshield Mercenaries:** The guild has a mutual respect with the mercenaries, occasionally working together on particularly dangerous missions.
- **Mossveil Remedies:** Members frequently visit this provision stall for potions and supplies before embarking on their adventures.

## Location

The Verdant Blades are headquartered in the [[Verdant Blades Guildhall]] within the [[Bazaar of the Bold]], making it easily accessible to both members and prospective recruits.
