## Overview

The **Brightshield Mercenaries** are a renowned and disciplined company of elite fighters based in [[Veron]]. Founded and led by the formidable warrior **[[Lirael Brightshield]]**, the mercenaries are known for their honour, integrity, and effectiveness in battle. The Brightshield Mercenaries have earned a sterling reputation across the region for taking on some of the most dangerous and challenging contracts, always delivering results with professionalism and unwavering dedication. Their headquarters, located at [[Brightshield’s Armoury]], also serves as a fully-equipped base of operations where the mercenaries train, plan, and prepare for their missions.

## Structure and Organisation

The Brightshield Mercenaries are structured with a clear chain of command, ensuring discipline and order within the ranks. The company is divided into several units, each specialising in different aspects of combat, from heavy infantry to scouting and reconnaissance. This specialisation allows the Brightshield Mercenaries to take on a wide variety of contracts, tailored to the specific needs of their clients.

### Leadership

- **Commander:** [[Lirael Brightshield]]
  - **Role:** As the founder and leader of the Brightshield Mercenaries, Lirael oversees all operations, from training and recruitment to mission planning and execution. Her strategic mind and unwavering commitment to honour guide the mercenaries in every mission they undertake.

### Key Units

- **Vanguard:** The front-line fighters of the Brightshield Mercenaries, consisting of heavily armoured warriors skilled in both offence and defence. They are the first into battle and are known for their resilience and strength.
- **Shadowstrike:** A unit of agile and stealthy operatives who excel in scouting, reconnaissance, and sabotage. They are often sent on covert missions where discretion and speed are paramount.
- **Skywatch:** A group of highly skilled archers and ranged combatants who provide cover and support from a distance. Their precision and ability to control the battlefield from afar are unmatched.
- **Ironclad:** The defensive unit, composed of shield-bearers and defensive tacticians who protect key positions and fortify battle lines. They are known for their unbreakable formations and ability to hold the line under even the most intense pressure.

## Headquarters

The Brightshield Mercenaries are headquartered at **[[Brightshield’s Armoury]]** in Veron. The armoury serves as both a place of business and the central hub for the company’s operations. Here, the mercenaries train, plan missions, and maintain their equipment. The facility includes a fully-equipped training ground, barracks for the mercenaries, and a war room where Lirael and her officers strategise and review contracts. The proximity to Brightshield’s Armoury ensures that the mercenaries always have access to the highest quality weapons and armour, tailored to their specific needs.

## Code of Conduct

The Brightshield Mercenaries operate under a strict code of conduct established by Lirael Brightshield herself. This code emphasises honour, integrity, and loyalty. All members of the company are expected to uphold these values, both on and off the battlefield. Contracts are accepted based on their alignment with the company’s principles—mercenaries are forbidden from taking on jobs that involve dishonourable actions or harm to innocents. This code has not only earned the Brightshield Mercenaries respect but also a loyal clientele who trust them to act with professionalism and ethics.

## Notable Contracts

- **Battle of the Broken Pass:** A critical battle where the Brightshield Mercenaries were hired to defend a strategic mountain pass against overwhelming odds. Their success in holding the pass earned them widespread acclaim and solidified their reputation as one of the most formidable mercenary companies in the region.
- **Operation Nightfall:** A covert mission to infiltrate and dismantle a dangerous criminal syndicate operating in Veron. The mission required precision, discretion, and a coordinated effort from several units within the company, all of which were executed flawlessly.
- **The Blackridge Campaign:** A prolonged military campaign where the Brightshield Mercenaries were contracted to support a coalition of forces in reclaiming territory from a well-entrenched enemy. The campaign tested the company’s endurance and strategic capabilities, resulting in a hard-won victory.

## Relationships and Allies

- **Adventurers' Guild:** The Brightshield Mercenaries maintain a strong relationship with the [[Adventurers' Guild]] in Veron. The two organisations often collaborate on large-scale operations that require both adventurers and mercenaries to work together, pooling their resources and expertise.
- **Veron’s City Guard:** While there is some tension between the Brightshield Mercenaries and the city guard, particularly with certain figures such as [[Captain Gaius Valen]], there is also a mutual respect for each other's capabilities. The mercenaries are sometimes called upon to assist the city guard during major crises.
- **Brightshield’s Armoury:** The mercenaries’ close ties to [[Brightshield’s Armoury]] ensure that they are always equipped with the best gear. This relationship allows for custom equipment tailored to the specific needs of the mercenaries, giving them an edge in battle.

## Recruitment and Training

Recruitment into the Brightshield Mercenaries is highly selective. Prospective members must demonstrate not only their combat skills but also their adherence to the company’s strict code of conduct. Once accepted, new recruits undergo rigorous training under the supervision of experienced veterans. This training is designed to hone their skills, instil discipline, and prepare them for the diverse challenges they will face as part of the company.

## Personality Traits of the Mercenaries

- **Honourable:** The mercenaries are known for their adherence to their code of conduct, ensuring they act with integrity and respect in all their dealings.
- **Disciplined:** The rigorous training and structured organisation of the company instil a strong sense of discipline in every member.
- **Loyal:** Members of the Brightshield Mercenaries are fiercely loyal to each other and to Lirael Brightshield, creating a strong bond of trust within the company.
