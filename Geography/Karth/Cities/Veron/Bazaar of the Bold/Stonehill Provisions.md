## Overview

**Stonehill Provisions** is a well-known provision stall located in the bustling [[Bazaar of the Bold]]. Owned and operated by [[Davrick Stonehill]], this stall is a favourite among adventurers preparing to delve into the [[Sunken Vault]]. With a reputation for reliability and fair pricing, Stonehill Provisions offers a wide range of essential supplies that every adventurer needs before embarking on their journey.

## Layout and Atmosphere

The stall is neatly organised, with shelves lined with various supplies ranging from food and water to ropes and bedrolls. The front counter is made of sturdy wood, behind which Davrick Stonehill stands, ready to assist customers with a friendly smile. Hanging from the canopy above are bundles of rope, lanterns, and other gear, showcasing the practicality and readiness of the stall. The air around the stall often carries the earthy scent of leather and the faint aroma of dried meats, contributing to the atmosphere of preparedness that Stonehill Provisions is known for.
