## Overview

**The Enchanter’s Conclave** is a critical government-operated institution in [[Veron]], dedicated to the identification, certification, and safeguarding of magical artefacts, particularly those retrieved from the [[Sunken Vault]]. Located within the [[Bazaar of the Bold]], the Conclave serves as the primary authority on all matters related to arcane items within the city, ensuring that dangerous or cursed artefacts do not pose a threat to the populace. The Conclave’s enchanters are highly skilled in detecting, analysing, and neutralising magical anomalies, making it an essential component of Veron’s governance and security.

## Role and Function

The Enchanter’s Conclave is responsible for inspecting and certifying all magical items brought into the city, especially those extracted from the Sunken Vault. Given the unpredictable and often dangerous nature of the artefacts found within the Vault, the Conclave plays a vital role in maintaining public safety. Their work involves:

- **Identification and Analysis:** Each artefact is meticulously examined to determine its properties, origin, and potential dangers. This process ensures that cursed or harmful items are identified before they can be sold or used within Veron.
- **Certification:** Once an artefact has been deemed safe, it is certified by the Conclave. This certification is mandatory for any magical item to be legally sold or traded within the city.
- **Containment and Neutralisation:** If an artefact is found to be cursed or dangerous, the Conclave’s enchanters will work to either neutralise its harmful effects or, if necessary, contain it to prevent any risk to the public.

## Layout and Atmosphere

The Enchanter’s Conclave is housed in a stately building within the [[Bazaar of the Bold]], easily recognisable by its tall, arched windows and the arcane symbols carved into its stone façade. Inside, the Conclave is a blend of scholarly atmosphere and high-security facility. The main hall is lined with shelves filled with ancient tomes and magical artefacts awaiting inspection, while several secure rooms are designated for the careful examination of particularly volatile items.

The air is thick with the scent of old parchment and the faint hum of magical wards that protect the building from external threats. Enchanters, clad in robes adorned with the city’s insignia, move with purpose as they attend to their tasks, their eyes sharp and their minds focused on the delicate work of handling powerful magic.

## Key Personnel

### Master Enchanter

- **[[Theron Eldryn|Master Enchanter Theron Eldryn]]** - The head of the Conclave, Theron Eldryn is a seasoned enchanter with decades of experience in arcane studies. He oversees all operations within the Conclave and is particularly skilled in detecting curses and hidden enchantments. Theron’s expertise is unmatched, and his approval is the final word on whether an artefact is safe for use or sale.

### Senior Enchanters

- **[[Lysandra Ravenshadow|Mage Lysandra Ravenshadow]]** - An identification specialist, Lysandra is responsible for the initial analysis of artefacts. Her keen eye and growing expertise make her a valuable asset to the Conclave.
- **[[Darius Thoureux|Scribe Darius Thoureux]]** - Tasked with the documentation and certification of artefacts, Darius ensures that all records are meticulously kept and that each item is properly catalogued.

### Supporting Staff

- **Apprentices:** A group of junior enchanters and scribes who assist in the daily operations of the Conclave, often tasked with preparing artefacts for examination or maintaining the Conclave’s extensive archives.
- **Security Wardens:** A specialised group of guards trained in both martial and arcane combat, responsible for the security of the Conclave and the containment of particularly dangerous artefacts.

## Services

### Artefact Identification

- **Basic Identification** - 50 gp
  - A standard service to determine the basic properties of an artefact, ensuring it is safe for use or sale.
- **Advanced Identification** - 150 gp
  - A more thorough examination that reveals all properties, including any curses, hidden effects, or potential dangers.
- **Discretionary Identification** - 250 gp
  - A service provided under strict confidentiality, ensuring that the nature of the artefact and the identity of its owner remain undisclosed.

### Certification

- **Official Certification** - 100 gp
  - Upon successful identification, artefacts are issued an official certificate of safety by the Conclave, allowing them to be legally sold or traded within Veron.
- **Re-Certification** - 50 gp
  - For artefacts that have undergone changes or repairs, this service re-certifies their safety and legality.

### Containment and Neutralisation

- **Containment** - Varies by artefact
  - Dangerous artefacts are placed in containment to prevent them from causing harm. This may involve the use of specialised wards, enchanted containers, or even permanent sealing.
- **Curse Removal** - Varies by artefact
  - The Conclave offers curse removal services for artefacts that have been identified as cursed. This process is delicate and requires significant expertise, hence the variable pricing.
  
### Enchanted Item Replacement Service

- **Enchanted Item Replacement** - Sponsored by the City of Veron
    - If an artefact retrieved from the [[Sunken Vault]] is identified as cursed or deemed too dangerous for safe use, the Conclave offers a unique service to its owner. At the expense of the City of [[Veron]], the Conclave will create and enchant a replacement item with similar properties, but without the harmful effects. This replacement service is optional; however, any artefacts deemed too dangerous by the Conclave will not be allowed to circulate within [[Veron]]. Instead, these dangerous items will be stored in the Conclave’s **[[Stasis Vault]]**—a heavily secured facility designed to neutralise and contain such artefacts.
- **Stasis Vault Storage** - Sponsored by the City of Veron
    - Artefacts deemed too dangerous are securely stored within the [[Stasis Vault]]. This facility is protected by powerful wards and guarded by the **Veron Guard**. Owners of such artefacts may choose to remove them from the city; however, the item must be transported by a specialised skeleton escort to ensure it does not pose a threat during transit. The skeleton transport will carry the item safely out of Veron, at which point it will be returned to its owner once clear of the city’s boundaries.

## Reputation and Authority

The Enchanter’s Conclave holds a significant position within Veron, both as a guardian of public safety and as a centre of arcane knowledge. Their strict regulations and thorough inspections have prevented countless disasters, earning them the trust and respect of the city’s citizens. However, their authority also means that they are often approached by those seeking to bypass the legal channels, leading to an ongoing effort to maintain the integrity of their operations.

## Affiliations

- **[[Verdant Blades]]:** The Conclave frequently works with the [[Verdant Blades]] to identify and certify artefacts recovered from the Sunken Vault, ensuring that the guild’s activities do not inadvertently endanger the city.
- **[[The Silver Sigil]]:** Occasionally collaborates with [[Korrin Blackthistle]] for the identification of rare and particularly enigmatic artefacts, although this relationship is more professional than friendly.
- **The Government of Veron:** As a government-operated institution, the Conclave works closely with Veron’s ruling body to maintain public safety and uphold the laws regarding magical items.

## Notable Achievements

- **The Sealing of the Infernal Blade:** The Conclave successfully identified and contained an ancient blade imbued with infernal energy that had the potential to corrupt its wielder and those around them.
- **The Cleansing of the Shadow Amulet:** The Conclave managed to remove a powerful curse from an amulet found within the [[Sunken Vault]], preventing what could have been a catastrophic spread of shadow magic in Veron.
- **The Containment of the Wailing Skull:** An artefact known for its ability to drive those who hear it to madness was safely contained by the Conclave, ensuring it could no longer harm anyone in Veron.
