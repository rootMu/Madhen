## Overview

**The Lantern’s Light** is a unique and enchanting stall in the [[Bazaar of the Bold]] in [[Veron]], known for its array of magical lanterns, each imbued with different effects and properties. Run by the talented enchanter [[Maren Thistlebloom]], the stall is a favourite among adventurers, scholars, and collectors alike. The lanterns, each a work of art in its own right, offer more than just illumination—they provide powerful magical effects tailored to the needs of their owners.

## Layout and Atmosphere

The Lantern’s Light is a small, charming stall draped in rich fabrics and lit by a multitude of glowing lanterns, each casting a different hue. The air is filled with the soft hum of magical energy, and the flickering lights create an almost otherworldly atmosphere. Lanterns of various shapes and sizes hang from the stall’s canopy, their lights dancing across the polished wooden counter where Maren Thistlebloom welcomes her customers with a warm smile.

Each lantern is displayed prominently, with a small sign indicating its name, magical properties, and price. The stall is carefully organised, with sections dedicated to different types of magic—protection, illumination, communication, and more. Customers often find themselves mesmerised by the variety of colours and the subtle, enchanting glow that fills the space.

## Products and Services

### Magical Lanterns

- **Lantern of Azure Clarity** - 300 gp
  - Emits a calming blue light that clears the mind and enhances concentration. While within its light, the user gains advantage on Intelligence checks and saving throws against charm effects.

- **Lantern of Ruby Protection** - 500 gp
  - Glows with a warm red light that provides a protective aura. While within its light, the user gains +2 to AC and advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects.

- **Lantern of Emerald Veil** - 400 gp
  - Shines with a green light that bends the shadows around the user, granting them increased stealth. While within its light, the user gains advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks and can become invisible for up to 1 minute once per day.

- **Lantern of Amber Vigilance** - 350 gp
  - Casts a bright amber light that sharpens the senses. While within its light, the user gains advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks and cannot be surprised.

- **Lantern of Sapphire Communication** - 450 gp
  - Glows with a deep blue light that enhances communication. While within its light, the user can cast the Sending spell once per day and gains advantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks.

### Custom Lanterns and Enchantments

- **Custom Lantern Creation** - Price varies
  - Maren offers custom lanterns tailored to the specific needs of her clients. Customers can choose the type of magic they want imbued in the lantern, as well as the colour and design. These custom orders are crafted with care and precision, ensuring that the lantern meets the exact specifications of the customer.

- **Lantern Enchantment** - Price varies
  - Customers can bring their own lanterns to Maren for enchantment. She offers a variety of enchantments, including those that enhance the light’s properties, add protective effects, or grant unique abilities.

## Notable Customers

- **[[Elandra Mossveil]]** - Often visits the Lantern’s Light to purchase enchanted lanterns for her herbalism work, using their magical properties to enhance her potions and remedies.
- **[[Thaddeus Grim]]** - A frequent customer, Thaddeus buys lanterns to create the perfect ambiance for his performances, often selecting those with calming or inspiring effects.
- **[[The Sirocco Lanterns]]** - A renowned team of five monks, known for their discipline, agility, and unique use of magical lanterns in combat and exploration. Each monk carries a different coloured lantern from The Lantern’s Light, attached to a long pole on their back, creating a striking and recognisable appearance. The lanterns not only aid them in their adventures but have also become symbolic of their group’s unity and strength. The Sirocco Lanterns are one of the most famous adventuring teams in Veron, respected for their prowess and their commitment to peace and balance.

## Proprietor

### Maren Thistlebloom

**Maren Thistlebloom** is the kind-hearted and talented enchanter who runs The Lantern’s Light. With a deep love for both magic and craftsmanship, Maren has dedicated her life to creating beautiful, functional, and magical lanterns that bring light and magic into the lives of her customers.

## Location

The Lantern’s Light is located within the [[Bazaar of the Bold]], its glow easily recognisable from a distance. The stall’s magical ambiance and Maren’s reputation ensure a steady stream of customers, many of whom return time and again for her unique and enchanting creations.
