## Overview

**Stonefist’s Forge** is one of the most respected armour and weaponry stalls in the bustling [[Bazaar of the Bold]]. Owned and operated by [[Borak Stonefist]], a former adventurer turned master armorer, this forge is known for producing some of the most durable and effective gear available, especially for those preparing to explore the treacherous depths of the [[Sunken Vault]]. Adventurers rely on Borak’s expertise and craftsmanship to ensure their gear can withstand the perils they will face.

## Layout and Atmosphere

The forge is a hive of activity, with the clang of hammer on anvil echoing through the stall as Borak works. The stall is lined with racks displaying a variety of weapons and armour, each piece meticulously crafted to meet Borak’s exacting standards. The air around the forge is filled with the scent of hot metal and the steady heat from the forge itself. Borak, always seen in his leather apron and smithing goggles, moves with practiced precision, ensuring that each piece of equipment meets his rigorous standards before it reaches his customers.
