## Overview

**The Silver Sigil** is a mysterious and shadowy shop located on the outskirts of the bustling [[Bazaar of the Bold]]. Owned and operated by [[Korrin Blackthistle]], this shop specialises in rare and powerful magical relics and artefacts, many of which have dark or forbidden origins. The Silver Sigil is known for its secrecy and the high prices it commands, but the items sold here are of unparalleled potency, making it a frequent stop for those seeking an edge in the [[Sunken Vault]].

## Layout and Atmosphere

The shop is dimly lit, with walls lined with shelves and glass cases displaying a variety of relics and artefacts, each carefully arranged to catch the eye of discerning adventurers. The air inside is thick with the scent of aged parchment and arcane incense, adding to the shop’s enigmatic atmosphere. A heavy velvet curtain separates the main area from a back room where Korrin conducts private dealings. Korrin himself is often found behind a polished ebony counter, his piercing yellow eyes watching over the shop with a mixture of suspicion and curiosity.

## Inventory

### Magical Relics

- **Ring of Water Walking** - 500 gp
  - Allows the wearer to walk across the surface of water as if it were solid ground.
- **Cloak of the Fern** - 350 gp
  - Grants the wearer advantage on Stealth checks when moving through areas with heavy plant growth.
- **Stone of Detection** - 400 gp
  - Vibrates when within 30 feet of hidden traps or secret doors, increasing in intensity as it nears the source.

### Artefacts

- **Amulet of Proof against Detection and Location** - 1,200 gp
  - This amulet makes the wearer immune to magical tracking and prevents them from being detected by divination magic.
- **Bag of Holding** - 500 gp
  - A bag that can hold up to 500 pounds of material, not exceeding a volume of 64 cubic feet.
- **Wand of Magic Missiles** - 1,000 gp
  - A wand that allows the user to cast Magic Missile at will.

### Identification Services

- **Basic Identification** - 50 gp
  - Provides information on the basic properties of an artefact, ensuring it is safe for use.
- **Advanced Identification** - 150 gp
  - A thorough examination that reveals all properties, including curses and hidden effects.
- **Discretionary Identification** - 250 gp
  - An identification service conducted with the utmost discretion, ensuring that the nature of the artefact and the identity of its owner remain confidential.

## Services

- **Private Appointments** - Varies by request
  - Korrin offers private consultations and dealings for those seeking specific relics or needing discreet services. These appointments are conducted in the back room of the shop, away from prying eyes.
- **Commissioned Artefact Hunts** - Varies by request
  - For a significant fee, Korrin will either acquire or commission the retrieval of a specific artefact, utilising his extensive network of contacts and knowledge of ancient ruins and dungeons.

## Notable Interactions

### Owner

- **[[Korrin Blackthistle]]** - The enigmatic and shrewd proprietor of The Silver Sigil, known for his expertise in magical relics and his cautious, secretive nature.

### Customers

- **[[Lirael Brightshield]]** - Occasionally trades rare materials or artefacts with Korrin, though their relationship is marked by professional respect rather than friendship.
- **[[Thaddeus Grim]]** - Sometimes exchanges information or relics with Korrin, with their dealings often shrouded in mystery.

## Location

The Silver Sigil is located on the outskirts of the [[Bazaar of the Bold]], a place known for its secrecy and the allure of the powerful relics within. Adventurers seeking powerful magical items or discreet services often find their way to this shadowy shop.
