## Overview

The **Stasis Vault** is a heavily fortified, magical facility located deep beneath the [[Enchanter’s Conclave]] in [[Veron]]. Designed as a secure containment area for cursed, dangerous, or otherwise volatile artefacts retrieved from the [[Sunken Vault]] and other perilous locations, the Stasis Vault plays a crucial role in ensuring the safety of Veron’s citizens. This vault is where items deemed too hazardous to circulate within the city are stored, neutralised, and protected from falling into the wrong hands.

## Purpose and Function

The primary function of the Stasis Vault is to contain and neutralise artefacts that have been identified as too dangerous for public use. The vault is equipped with powerful magical wards and physical defences to ensure that no harmful magic or entity can escape. Only a select few within the [[Enchanter’s Conclave]] have access to the Stasis Vault, and its location and contents are closely guarded secrets.

### Key Functions

- **Containment:** The vault is designed to hold artefacts that have been deemed unsafe, preventing them from causing harm or being misused. These items are stored in individual stasis chambers, each tailored to the specific nature of the artefact it contains.
- **Neutralisation:** In cases where an artefact poses an ongoing threat, the Enchanters work to neutralise its dangerous properties while it is held within the vault. This process can take time and may involve complex rituals or the use of other powerful artefacts.
- **Secure Storage:** The vault ensures that dangerous artefacts are kept out of circulation and away from those who might seek to exploit them. The Stasis Vault is protected by both arcane and physical means, making it one of the most secure locations in Veron.

## Layout and Security

The Stasis Vault is located in a series of deep, interlinked chambers beneath the Enchanter’s Conclave. The vault is divided into multiple sections, each containing stasis chambers designed to suppress and contain different types of magical energy or curses.

### Security Features

- **Magical Wards:** The vault is surrounded by layers of powerful wards designed to suppress magical energies and prevent the activation of artefacts. These wards are maintained and reinforced regularly by the Enchanters.
- **Arcane Locking Mechanisms:** Each chamber within the vault is sealed with an arcane lock that can only be opened by authorised personnel using a specific magical key. These locks are designed to be impervious to traditional lockpicking and brute force.
- **Veron Guard:** A contingent of the [[Veron Guard]], specially trained in both martial and arcane combat, is stationed at the entrance to the Stasis Vault. Their presence ensures that no unauthorised individuals can gain access.
- **Skeleton Transport:** In the rare event that an artefact must be removed from the vault, it is done so via a specialised skeleton transport. These enchanted constructs are tasked with carrying the item out of the city, ensuring that it does not pose a threat during transit.

## Access and Protocols

Access to the Stasis Vault is strictly controlled. Only senior members of the Enchanter’s Conclave, such as the [[Theron Eldryn|Master Enchanter Theron Eldryn]], have direct access to the vault. Protocols for placing an artefact in the vault involve multiple layers of verification and require approval from both the Conclave’s leadership and Veron’s governing authorities.

### Artefact Retrieval

- **Owner Retrieval Process:** If the owner of a stored artefact wishes to remove it from Veron, they must submit a formal request to the Conclave. Upon approval, the item will be retrieved by the Conclave and securely transported out of the city by the skeleton transport service. The artefact will only be returned to the owner once it is safely outside the city’s boundaries.
- **Emergency Containment:** In cases where an artefact becomes unstable or presents an immediate danger, the vault is equipped with emergency protocols to contain and neutralise the threat. These protocols are activated automatically by the vault’s wards and are overseen by the Master Enchanter.

## Reputation and Importance

The Stasis Vault is considered one of Veron’s most vital institutions, crucial for maintaining the city’s safety and stability. Its existence ensures that even the most dangerous relics and artefacts do not threaten the lives of Veron’s citizens. The vault’s reputation for security is unmatched, and its operations are a testament to the city’s commitment to responsible magical governance.

## Notable Containments

- **The Infernal Blade:** An ancient weapon imbued with infernal energy, capable of corrupting its wielder. The blade is stored in a specially warded chamber to prevent its malevolent influence from spreading.
- **The Wailing Skull:** A cursed artefact that induces madness in those who hear its cries. The skull is kept in an isolated chamber, its cries muted by powerful silencing wards.
- **The Black Mirror of Threnaliath:** A relic from the [[Lost Dungeon of Threnaliath]], this mirror is said to trap the souls of those who gaze into it. The mirror is contained within a stasis field that prevents it from being activated.
