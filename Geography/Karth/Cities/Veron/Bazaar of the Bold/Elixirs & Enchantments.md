## Overview

**Elixirs & Enchantments** is a bustling magic and alchemy stall located in the [[Bazaar of the Bold]] in [[Veron]]. Specialising in a wide range of potions, elixirs, and enchanted items, this stall is a favourite among adventurers, mages, and anyone in need of a magical boost or a potent remedy. The stall is run by the talented and knowledgeable **[[Sariel Dawnglow]]**, a half-elf with a deep understanding of both alchemy and enchantment. Sariel’s expertise ensures that every potion is perfectly brewed and every enchanted item is infused with just the right amount of magical energy.

## Layout and Atmosphere

Elixirs & Enchantments is a vibrant and colourful stall, with shelves lined with bottles of various shapes and sizes, each filled with liquids of different hues. The air is thick with the scent of herbs, spices, and magical ingredients, creating an atmosphere that is both inviting and mysterious. Hanging from the awning are small enchanted trinkets that glow softly, adding to the magical ambiance. Sariel’s workspace is a cluttered but organised area at the back of the stall, where she can often be seen mixing potions or carefully enchanting items. The stall is always busy, with customers browsing the selection of potions or consulting with Sariel about custom orders.

## Products and Services

### Potions and Elixirs

- **Potion of Healing** - 50 gp
  - A staple for any adventurer, this potion heals wounds and restores vitality. (Heals 2d4 + 2 hit points)
- **Elixir of Strength** - 150 gp
  - A powerful elixir that temporarily enhances physical strength, perfect for warriors heading into battle. (Grants +2 to Strength for 1 hour)
- **Potion of Invisibility** - 200 gp
  - A rare and sought-after potion that grants the drinker the ability to become invisible for a short period. (Lasts for 1 hour or until the user attacks or casts a spell)
- **Elixir of Night Vision** - 100 gp
  - A handy elixir that allows the drinker to see in complete darkness, making it ideal for nighttime explorations. (Grants darkvision for 8 hours)

### Enchanted Items

- **Amulet of Protection** - 300 gp
  - A simple yet effective amulet that enhances the wearer’s resistance to harm. (Grants +1 to AC)
- **Ring of Spell Storing** - 500 gp
  - A magical ring that can store up to 3 levels worth of spells, allowing the wearer to cast them later. (Can store up to 3 spell levels, such as one 3rd-level spell or three 1st-level spells)
- **Cloak of Shadows** - 400 gp
  - A dark, enchanted cloak that helps the wearer blend into the shadows, making them harder to detect. (Grants advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks)

### Custom Potions and Enchantments

- **Custom Potion Brewing** - Price varies
  - Sariel offers custom potion brewing services, allowing customers to request specific potions tailored to their needs. Whether it’s a rare antidote or a powerful combat elixir, Sariel’s potions are known for their effectiveness and precision.
- **Custom Enchantment Services** - Price varies
  - In addition to her standard wares, Sariel also provides custom enchantment services. Customers can bring their own items to be enchanted, or request a specific enchantment on an item purchased from the stall. Sariel’s enchantments are crafted with care, ensuring that the magical effects are both potent and stable.

## Notable Customers

- **[[Korrin Blackthistle]]** - Frequently visits Elixirs & Enchantments to purchase rare ingredients and custom potions for his magical experiments and missions.
- **[[Talia Windrunner]]** - Regularly buys potions and enchanted items to aid her in leading the [[Verdant Blades]], trusting Sariel’s expertise to provide exactly what she needs.
- **[[The Sirocco Lanterns]]** - Often commission Sariel for custom elixirs that enhance their natural abilities, ensuring they are always prepared for their dangerous missions.

## Proprietor

### Sariel Dawnglow

**Sariel Dawnglow** is the bright and knowledgeable half-elf who runs Elixirs & Enchantments. With a passion for both alchemy and enchantment, Sariel has dedicated her life to mastering the magical arts. Her stall is a reflection of her expertise, offering a wide range of potions, elixirs, and enchanted items that cater to the diverse needs of Veron’s adventurers and mages.

- **Appearance:** Sariel is a slender half-elf with long, golden hair that falls in soft waves down her back. Her bright blue eyes are filled with curiosity and warmth, and she often wears robes of deep green, symbolising her connection to nature and the alchemical arts. Her fingers are often stained with the remnants of various ingredients, a testament to her constant work with potions and magical items.
- **Personality:** Sariel is friendly and approachable, always eager to help her customers find the perfect potion or enchantment. She is deeply knowledgeable about her craft and enjoys sharing her insights with others. Sariel is also meticulous and precise, ensuring that every potion is brewed to perfection and every enchantment is carefully crafted. Her passion for magic is evident in everything she does, making her stall one of the most popular in the Bazaar.

## Location

Elixirs & Enchantments is located within the [[Bazaar of the Bold]], where it draws a steady stream of customers from all walks of life. The stall’s vibrant atmosphere and Sariel’s reputation for quality ensure that it is always busy, with adventurers and mages alike seeking out her potions and enchanted items.
