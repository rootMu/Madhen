## Overview

**The Iron Edge** is a reputable armour and weaponry stall located in the bustling [[Bazaar of the Bold]] in [[Veron]]. Run by the skilled blacksmith [[Harlan Blackwood]], the Iron Edge is known for its high-quality weapons and armour, crafted with precision and care. Adventurers and mercenaries alike frequent the stall to acquire gear that is both durable and effective, ensuring they are well-prepared for the dangers that await them, particularly in the [[Sunken Vault]].

## Layout and Atmosphere

The Iron Edge is a large, open-air stall filled with the clang of hammer on anvil and the scent of hot metal and oil. Weapons and armour of all kinds are displayed on sturdy racks and tables, each piece meticulously crafted and polished to a high sheen. The front of the stall features a large, reinforced wooden counter where customers can discuss their needs with Harlan or one of his assistants. Behind the counter, a forge is constantly alight, where Harlan can be seen working on custom orders or repairing damaged gear.

The atmosphere at the Iron Edge is one of focus and industriousness. Harlan runs a tight ship, ensuring that every piece sold meets his exacting standards. Customers appreciate the straightforward and no-nonsense approach of the shop, knowing that they are getting weapons and armour they can rely on in the heat of battle.

- **Battle Axe of Cleaving** - 200 gp
  - A heavy, brutal axe known for its ability to deal devastating blows. (1d8 slashing damage)

### Armour

- **Ironclad Plate Armour** - 1,200 gp
  - Full plate armour custom-fitted for maximum protection, featuring reinforced joints and a polished finish. (AC 18)
- **Chainmail of Resilience** - 500 gp
  - A suit of chainmail offering excellent protection while maintaining flexibility for agile movements. (AC 16)
- **Leather Armour of the Scout** - 250 gp
  - Light, durable armour designed to enhance stealth and mobility, perfect for rogues and scouts. (AC 11 + Dex modifier)

### Special Orders and Customisation

- **Custom Weapons and Armour** - Price varies
  - Harlan offers made-to-order weapons and armour tailored to the specific needs of the customer. This includes enhancements such as increased durability, personalised engravings, or special materials.
- **Enchantments** - Price varies
  - In collaboration with the [[Enchanter’s Conclave]], Harlan offers weapon and armour enchantment services, allowing customers to imbue their gear with magical properties. These enchantments range from elemental damage to increased defensive capabilities.

### Repairs and Maintenance

- **Weapon Sharpening** - 5 gp per weapon
  - Quick and reliable sharpening services to ensure weapons are battle-ready.
- **Armour Repair** - 10 gp per repair
  - Expert repairs for damaged armour, restoring it to its full protective capacity.
- **Full Maintenance Package** - 25 gp
  - Includes weapon sharpening, armour repair, and a thorough inspection to ensure all gear is in top condition.

## Notable Customers

- **[[Davrick Stonehill]]** - A frequent visitor who often recommends the Iron Edge to adventurers in need of reliable gear.
- **[[Korrin Blackthistle]]** - Occasionally commissions special orders from Harlan, particularly for enchanted or rare materials.

## Proprietor

### Harlan Blackwood

**[[Harlan Blackwood]]** is a master blacksmith with years of experience crafting weapons and armour. Known for his skill and attention to detail, Harlan takes great pride in his work and maintains a strict standard of quality at the Iron Edge. His reputation for creating durable and effective gear has earned him the loyalty of many repeat customers.

## Location

The Iron Edge is located within the [[Bazaar of the Bold]], making it easily accessible to adventurers preparing for their expeditions into the [[Sunken Vault]]. The stall’s prominent location and Harlan’s reputation ensure a steady stream of customers.
