## Overview

**The Arcane Cache** is a well-established magic shop located in the heart of the [[Bazaar of the Bold]] in [[Veron]]. Specialising in rare and powerful magical items, scrolls, and arcane artefacts, this shop is a go-to destination for adventurers, wizards, and collectors seeking the finest magical wares. The shop is run by the enigmatic and knowledgeable **[[Ilyana Stormwind]]**, an accomplished wizard with a deep understanding of arcane lore and enchantment. The Arcane Cache is renowned for its curated selection of high-quality magical items, each chosen for its potency and rarity.

## Layout and Atmosphere

The Arcane Cache is a spacious and elegantly designed shop, with shelves lined with glittering artefacts, carefully arranged scrolls, and shimmering potions. The air is filled with the faint hum of magical energy, and the soft glow of enchanted crystals illuminates the space. The walls are adorned with tapestries depicting scenes of ancient magic and mystical landscapes, adding to the shop's otherworldly ambiance. A large counter runs along the back of the shop, where Ilyana can often be found advising customers or conducting delicate enchantments. The atmosphere is one of quiet power and sophistication, with Ilyana’s presence adding to the sense of arcane mystery.

## Products and Services

### Magical Items

- **Wand of Fireballs** - 750 gp
  - A powerful wand that can unleash devastating fireballs upon command. (Contains 7 charges; each charge expends a fireball spell, with a DC 15 save)
- **Ring of Protection** - 500 gp
  - A simple yet potent ring that enhances the wearer’s defenses, offering protection from both physical and magical attacks. (Grants +1 to AC and saving throws)
- **Cloak of Displacement** - 1,000 gp
  - A rare and valuable cloak that causes the wearer’s image to shift and waver, making it difficult for enemies to land a hit. (Attackers have disadvantage on attack rolls against you)

### Scrolls and Tomes

- **Scroll of Teleportation** - 300 gp
  - A scroll inscribed with the teleportation spell, allowing the user to instantly travel to a known location.
- **Tome of Arcane Lore** - 400 gp
  - A book containing rare and ancient magical knowledge, valuable to any wizard seeking to expand their understanding of the arcane.
- **Scroll of Invisibility** - 150 gp
  - A scroll that allows the user to cast the invisibility spell, rendering them unseen for a short duration.

### Arcane Artefacts

- **Orb of Scrying** - 800 gp
  - A crystal orb used for scrying, allowing the user to see distant places and people as if they were present.
- **Amulet of the Planes** - 1,200 gp
  - A powerful amulet that allows the wearer to shift between different planes of existence. (Requires attunement; allows planar travel once per day)
- **Staff of Power** - 2,000 gp
  - A legendary staff imbued with potent magical energy, capable of casting powerful spells and enhancing the wielder’s magical abilities. (Grants +2 to spell attack rolls and saving throws)

### Custom Enchantments

- **Item Enchantment Services** - Price varies
  - Ilyana offers custom enchantment services, allowing customers to bring in their own items to be imbued with magical properties. Her enchantments are known for their potency and precision, making even the most mundane items powerful tools in the hands of a skilled user.

## Notable Customers

- **[[Korrin Blackthistle]]** - A frequent visitor to the Arcane Cache, Korrin often purchases rare magical items and scrolls to aid in his arcane research and missions.
- **[[Thaddeus Grim]]** - Occasionally seeks out rare and unusual magical items from Ilyana’s collection to enhance his performances and magical displays.
- **[[The Sirocco Lanterns]]** - Regularly consult Ilyana for advice on powerful magical items that can aid them in their missions, trusting her expertise to guide them in their choices.

## Proprietor

### Ilyana Stormwind

**Ilyana Stormwind** is the owner and operator of the Arcane Cache. A powerful wizard and skilled enchanter, Ilyana has dedicated her life to the pursuit of magical knowledge and the acquisition of rare and potent artefacts. Her deep understanding of arcane magic and her connections within the magical community have made the Arcane Cache one of the most respected magic shops in Veron.

## Location

The Arcane Cache is located within the [[Bazaar of the Bold]], where it draws a steady stream of customers from across Veron and beyond. The shop’s reputation for quality and the rare magical items it offers ensure that it is always busy, with Ilyana often personally attending to her customers’ needs.
