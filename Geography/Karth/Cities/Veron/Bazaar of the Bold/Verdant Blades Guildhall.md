## Overview

**The Verdant Blades Guildhall** is a small but well-appointed headquarters for the **[[Verdant Blades]]**, a prominent adventurers' guild based in [[Veron]]. Located within the bustling [[Bazaar of the Bold]], the guildhall serves as a central hub for members to gather, plan expeditions, and receive assignments. Managed by the capable and charismatic [[Talia Windrunner]], the guildhall is a key part of the adventuring community in Veron, offering resources, support, and camaraderie to its members.

## Layout and Atmosphere

The guildhall is a modest yet comfortable building, with its walls adorned with banners and trophies from past expeditions. A large table dominates the main room, surrounded by sturdy chairs where members of the Verdant Blades gather to discuss their plans and missions. A notice board near the entrance is filled with job postings, mission details, and announcements from the guild leadership. The atmosphere is one of camaraderie and shared purpose, with a constant hum of activity as members come and go, preparing for their next adventure.

## Services and Activities

### Recruitment

- **Guild Membership Application** - 10 gp
  - Prospective members can apply to join the Verdant Blades. This includes an interview and a skills assessment by [[Talia Windrunner]] or another senior guild member.

### Mission Assignments

- **Expedition Planning** - 50 gp
  - Detailed planning sessions with [[Galen Stormrider]], the Expedition Coordinator, to map out strategy, gather a team, and prepare for an upcoming mission into the [[Sunken Vault]].
- **Mission Contracts** - Varies by contract
  - Specific missions assigned to guild members, with rewards ranging from 50 gp to 500 gp depending on the complexity and danger involved.

### Training and Support

- **Combat Training Session** - 25 gp per hour
  - One-on-one training with a seasoned guild member in melee or ranged combat techniques, ensuring members are well-prepared for the challenges of the Vault.
- **Gear Consultation** - Free with membership
  - [[Lirael Brightshield]] offers advice on the best gear load-outs for specific challenges or environments within the Sunken Vault, helping members optimise their equipment for their missions.

### Member Benefits

- **Verdant Blades Cloak** - 75 gp
  - A cloak bearing the emblem of the Verdant Blades, offering minor protection (+1 to AC) and serving as a badge of membership.
- **Guild-Issued Healing Potions** - 40 gp each
  - Discounted potions available exclusively to members, useful for quick recovery during missions.
- **Meeting Halls** - Free with membership
  - Members can use private meeting rooms within the guildhall to plan their expeditions or rest before and after missions.

## Notable Members

### Guild Leadership

- **[[Talia Windrunner]]** - The charismatic and skilled Guild Representative and Manager, known for her leadership and tactical acumen.
- **[[Galen Stormrider]]** - The experienced Expedition Coordinator who assists members in planning and executing their missions.

### Frequent Visitors

- **[[Elara Quickstrike]]** - The Recruitment Officer who scouts and recruits new talent for the [[Verdant Blades]].
- **[[Thaddeus Grim]]** - A bard who often performs at guild events, commemorating the deeds of guild members with his stirring ballads.

## Location

The Verdant Blades Guildhall is situated within the [[Bazaar of the Bold]], making it easily accessible to adventurers and guild members. Its location at the heart of the Bazaar reflects the guild’s importance within Veron’s adventuring community.

## Atmosphere

The guildhall is a place of constant activity, where adventurers gather to share stories, prepare for their missions, and support one another. The walls are lined with weapons, maps, and artefacts from past expeditions, serving as a reminder of the guild’s storied history and the dangers that lie ahead. The warm glow of lanterns and the smell of well-worn leather and steel create an atmosphere of readiness and anticipation.
