## Overview

The **Bazaar of the Bold** is a vibrant and bustling marketplace located just before the entrance to the [[Sunken Vault]] beneath the [[Living Waters|Living Waters District]] of [[Veron]]. Serving as the last stop for adventurers preparing to venture into the dangerous depths of the Vault, the Bazaar is a gathering place for merchants, seasoned adventurers, and guild representatives, all catering to the needs of those who seek fortune and glory below.

## Layout and Structure

The Bazaar is set within a large, open cavern-like space, repurposed from an ancient underground complex. Stalls, shops, and meeting areas are arranged in a circular pattern around a central plaza, creating an organised yet lively atmosphere. The natural rock walls are adorned with hanging lanterns and banners from various guilds, casting a warm, flickering light over the entire area.

### Central Plaza

At the heart of the Bazaar is the **Central Plaza**, a wide, open space where adventurers gather to discuss plans, join expeditions, or rest before entering the Vault. The plaza is dominated by a large stone fountain, intricately carved with scenes from Veron’s history. Around the plaza, benches and low stone tables provide places for adventurers to sit, eat, and trade stories.

### The Market Stalls

Surrounding the Central Plaza are rows of market stalls, each managed by a different merchant. These stalls are constructed from wood and stone, with sturdy canopies made from canvas or leather. The market stalls are organised by type, making it easy for adventurers to find what they need.

#### Provision Stalls
1. **[[Mossveil Remedies]]** - Run by [[Elandra Mossveil]]
   - Specialises in potions, antidotes, and rare herbs, including underwater breathing potions and salves for burns and wounds.
   
2. **[[Stonehill Provisions]]** - Run by [[Davrick Stonehill]]
   - Offers basic supplies such as food, water, and camping gear tailored for dungeon exploration.
   
3. **[[The Lantern’s Light]]** - Run by [[Maren Thistlebloom]]
   - Sells torches, lanterns, and other lighting equipment, including magical lanterns that function underwater.

#### Armour and Weaponry Stalls
1. **[[Stonefist’s Forge]]** - Run by [[Borak Stonefist]]
   - Specialises in custom armour and weapons, including gear specifically designed for the challenges of the Sunken Vault.
   
2. **[[The Iron Edge]]** - Run by [[Harlan Blackwood]]
   - Offers a range of swords, axes, and shields, with a focus on durable, reliable equipment for extended expeditions.
   
3. **[[The Bowyer’s Nook]]** - Run by [[Lyra Silverstring]]
   - Provides bows, crossbows, and a variety of arrows, including enchanted arrows designed to pierce magical defences.

#### Magic and Alchemy Stalls
1. **[[Elixirs & Enchantments]]** - Run by [[Sariel Dawnglow]]
   - Offers a variety of potions, scrolls, and minor enchanted items, including protective charms and potions of strength.
   
2. **[[The Arcane Cache]]** - Run by [[Ilyana Stormwind]]
   - Specialises in scrolls and wands, with a selection of spells tailored for dungeon exploration and combat.

3. **[[The Hidden Path]]** - Run by [[Faelen Quickstep]]
   - Specialises in secret maps and information about lesser-known paths and hidden areas within the Vault.

### Permanent Shops

Beyond the market stalls, along the outer edges of the Bazaar, are several permanent shops. These structures are built directly into the rock walls, with small entrances leading into more spacious interiors. Each shop has a unique facade, reflecting the merchant’s trade.

1. **[[Enchanter’s Conclave|The Enchanter’s Conclave]]** - *Government Office*
   - Staffed by a team of enchanters and mages, this office is responsible for identifying and cataloging any artefacts brought out of the Sunken Vault. They ensure that no cursed or dangerous items are brought into the city. Adventurers can also request identification services or seek advice on magical items found within the Vault.

2. **[[Brightshield’s Armoury]]** - Run by [[Lirael Brightshield]]
   - A full-service armoury offering high-quality weapons, armour, and shields, as well as repair services. Lirael also provides consultation on gear load-outs for specific Vault expeditions.

3. **[[The Silver Sigil]]** - Run by [[Korrin Blackthistle]]
   - A shadowy shop dealing in rare relics and artefacts, often those with mysterious or forbidden origins. The shop is known for its secrecy and the high prices it commands.

4. **[[Verdant Blades Guildhall|The Verdant Blades Guildhall]]** - Run by [[Talia Windrunner]]
   - A small but well-appointed guildhall where members of the Verdant Blades can gather, plan, and receive assignments. The guildhall also serves as a recruitment center for new members.

### Guild Representatives and Hirelings

Scattered throughout the Bazaar are representatives from various adventurers' guilds and mercenary companies. These individuals often set up small, temporary tents or meeting areas marked by their respective guild banners. They recruit adventurers, offer contracts, and arrange for hirelings to accompany expeditions.

- **[[Verdant Blades|The Verdant Blades]]** - Represented by [[Talia Windrunner]]
  - A well-known adventurers' guild that organises expeditions into the Sunken Vault and other dangerous locations. Talia oversees recruitment and mission assignments.
  
- **[[Brightshield Mercenaries]]** - Led by [[Lirael Brightshield]]
  - A respected mercenary company offering skilled adventurers for hire. They are known for their reliability and effectiveness in the field.
  
- **[[Seekers of Knowledge]]** - Represented by [[Theron Valmaris|Archivist Theron]]
  - A guild of scholars and mages who seek out rare artefacts and knowledge from within the Vault. They often hire adventurers to retrieve specific items or to escort their own members on dangerous research missions.

### Meeting Halls

Tucked into alcoves and quieter corners of the Bazaar are a few **Meeting Halls**—small, enclosed spaces where adventurers can hold private discussions, finalise plans, or rest before their journey. These halls are often rented out by guilds or mercenary groups and are equipped with basic furniture and storage for gear.

## Atmosphere and Visuals

The Bazaar is a lively, colourful place, filled with the sounds of haggling, laughter, and the clinking of weapons and armour. The smell of cooking food mingles with the earthy scent of the underground, and the air is filled with the soft glow of enchanted lanterns. Banners and flags from various guilds and factions hang from the walls, adding splashes of color to the otherwise muted tones of the stone and wood structures.

**Water Features:**
Small streams run through channels carved into the stone floor, fed by the waterfalls that cascade down from the Living Waters District above. These streams feed into the central fountain, which serves as both a decorative piece and a source of fresh water for those in the Bazaar. The constant presence of water provides a refreshing coolness to the air, making the space feel alive and vibrant despite being underground.

**Lighting:**
Enchanted lanterns hang from the wooden beams and stone arches, casting a warm, inviting glow over the entire area. These lanterns are crafted to resemble ancient designs, with intricate patterns etched into the glass that create dancing shadows along the walls. The lighting in the Bazaar is designed to be both functional and atmospheric, with brighter lights illuminating the stalls and shops, while softer, more ambient light fills the central plaza and meeting halls.

**Sounds:**
The Bazaar is rarely quiet. The sounds of merchants calling out to potential customers, the clinking of coin as deals are made, and the chatter of adventurers planning their next move create a constant hum of activity. The central fountain adds a soothing backdrop of splashing water, which helps to soften the louder noises and lends a sense of calm amidst the bustling marketplace.

**Vegetation:**
Despite being underground, the Bazaar is home to various patches of moss, small ferns, and creeping vines that have taken root in the cracks of the stone walls. These plants are tended to by a few dedicated merchants and give the space an organic, almost mystical feel. The presence of greenery adds to the overall atmosphere, making the Bazaar feel like a hidden sanctuary beneath the city.
