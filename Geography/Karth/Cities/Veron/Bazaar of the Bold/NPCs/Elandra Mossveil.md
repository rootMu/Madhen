## Overview

**Elandra Mossveil** is the owner and herbalist of [[Mossveil Remedies]], a well-known provision stall in the [[Bazaar of the Bold]]. A half-elf with deep green eyes and hair streaked with grey, Elandra is renowned for her knowledge of herbs and potions. She has spent years studying the flora of Veron and the [[Sunken Vault]], crafting potions that are highly sought after by adventurers.

## Role

Elandra provides adventurers with potions, antidotes, and rare herbs essential for their journeys. Her expertise is not just in brewing potions but also in understanding the unique properties of the plants she uses, many of which are sourced from dangerous locations.

## Appearance and Personality

- **Appearance:** Middle-aged half-elf with deep green eyes, grey-streaked hair, and simple robes adorned with pouches of herbs.
- **Personality:** Calm, wise, and somewhat mysterious. Elandra is known for offering sage advice to those who seek her wares, speaking in a soothing tone that puts her customers at ease.

## Relationships

- **Friend:** [[Borak Stonefist]] - They share a mutual respect for each other’s crafts and often exchange goods or advice.
- **Contact:** [[Korrin Blackthistle]] - Occasionally trades rare herbs for magical items or ingredients.

## Location

Elandra can always be found at [[Mossveil Remedies]] in the [[Bazaar of the Bold]].
