## Overview

**Ilyana Stormwind** is the enigmatic and powerful wizard who runs [[The Arcane Cache]] in the [[Bazaar of the Bold]] in [[Veron]]. With a reputation for her deep knowledge of arcane magic and her ability to source and enchant some of the rarest magical items in the realm, Ilyana is a respected figure in both the magical and adventuring communities. Before establishing herself in the Bazaar of the Bold, Ilyana ran a prestigious magic shop in the Arcane Quarter, a more secluded and exclusive area of Veron. Seeking to reach a broader audience and cater to the growing needs of Veron’s adventurers, she relocated to the Bazaar. Ilyana now rents out her former shop and laboratory to up-and-coming wizards, while she continues her work in the bustling heart of the city.

## Appearance and Personality

- **Appearance:** Ilyana is a tall, striking woman with long, flowing black hair that cascades in waves around her shoulders. Her piercing grey eyes seem to see through to the very soul, and her regal bearing commands respect. She typically dresses in elegant robes of deep blue or violet, often adorned with arcane symbols and intricate embroidery. Her jewellery, much like her shop, is both tasteful and imbued with subtle magical properties.

- **Personality:** Ilyana is known for her calm, composed demeanour and her authoritative presence. She speaks with confidence and precision, always choosing her words carefully. Ilyana is deeply knowledgeable about all aspects of magic and enjoys discussing arcane theory with those who share her passion. Despite her reserved nature, she is deeply dedicated to her craft and her customers, ensuring that every item she sells or enchants is of the highest quality. Ilyana is also a shrewd businesswoman, able to negotiate deals with both customers and suppliers to maintain the prestige of the Arcane Cache.

## Skills and Expertise

- **Master Wizard:** Ilyana is a powerful and highly skilled wizard, with a deep understanding of both practical and theoretical magic. Her mastery of the arcane allows her to cast complex spells and enchant items with potent magical properties.
- **Expert Enchanter:** Ilyana’s skill in enchanting is unparalleled. She can imbue mundane items with magical properties that enhance their functionality and power. Her enchantments are known for their precision and reliability, making her items highly sought after.
- **Artefact Acquisition:** Ilyana has a vast network of contacts within the magical community, allowing her to source rare and powerful artefacts. Her shop, The Arcane Cache, is renowned for its collection of these items, each carefully chosen for its quality and potency.

## History

Before establishing herself in the [[Bazaar of the Bold]], Ilyana Stormwind was the proprietor of a prestigious magic shop located in the Arcane Quarter of Veron. This exclusive district was known for its concentration of powerful wizards, arcane scholars, and magical institutions. Ilyana’s shop was a cornerstone of the community, frequented by those seeking the most potent spells and rarest artefacts. However, as Veron grew and the needs of its adventurers expanded, Ilyana recognised an opportunity to reach a broader audience. She decided to relocate her operations to the more accessible and bustling Bazaar of the Bold, where she could offer her expertise to a wider range of customers.

After her move, Ilyana rented out her former shop and laboratory in the Arcane Quarter to up-and-coming wizards and enchanters, providing them with a space to develop their skills and contribute to Veron’s magical community. This move not only expanded her influence but also cemented her reputation as a key figure in the city’s magical circles.

## Relationships

- **Customer:** [[Korrin Blackthistle]] - Korrin is a frequent visitor to the Arcane Cache, relying on Ilyana’s expertise to acquire rare magical items and scrolls for his research and missions. The two share a professional relationship built on mutual respect for each other’s abilities.
- **Customer:** [[Thaddeus Grim]] - Thaddeus occasionally seeks out rare and unusual magical items from Ilyana’s collection to enhance his performances and magical displays. Their interactions are often lively and full of arcane banter.
- **Ally:** [[Sariel Dawnglow]] - Ilyana maintains a friendly and cooperative relationship with Sariel, often exchanging rare ingredients and discussing the latest advancements in magic and alchemy. Their partnership has resulted in several powerful enchanted items that are highly sought after in Veron.

## Location

Ilyana Stormwind can be found at [[The Arcane Cache]] in the [[Bazaar of the Bold]], where she oversees the day-to-day operations of her shop and personally attends to customers seeking the finest magical items. Her former shop and laboratory in the Arcane Quarter are now rented out to promising wizards, continuing to contribute to Veron’s magical community while she focuses on her work in the Bazaar.
