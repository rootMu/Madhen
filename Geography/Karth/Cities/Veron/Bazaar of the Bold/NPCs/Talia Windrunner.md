## Overview

**Talia Windrunner** is the representative and manager of the [[Verdant Blades Guildhall]] in the [[Bazaar of the Bold]]. A charismatic and athletic human woman with short, windswept auburn hair, Talia is a seasoned adventurer known for her leadership skills and sharp tactical mind.

## Role

Talia manages the Verdant Blades Guildhall, overseeing recruitment, mission assignments, and expedition planning. She is responsible for organising guild activities and ensuring that members are well-prepared for their journeys into the [[Sunken Vault]].

## Appearance and Personality

- **Appearance:** Athletic human woman with short auburn hair and a confident demeanour, often seen wearing a mix of leather and cloth armour adorned with the Verdant Blades emblem.
- **Personality:** Outgoing and charismatic, Talia is quick to engage with others and is always on the lookout for promising recruits. She is known for her sharp wit and strong leadership abilities.

## Relationships

- **Friend:** [[Lirael Brightshield]] - They work closely together, coordinating their efforts to prepare adventurers for the Vault.
- **Contact:** [[Elandra Mossveil]] - Occasionally purchases potions and supplies for guild members.

## Location

Talia can be found at the [[Verdant Blades Guildhall]] in the [[Bazaar of the Bold]].
