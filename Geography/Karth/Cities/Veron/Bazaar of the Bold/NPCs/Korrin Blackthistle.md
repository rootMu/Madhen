## Overview

**Korrin Blackthistle** is the enigmatic owner of [[The Silver Sigil]], a shop dealing in rare and powerful magical relics and artefacts located in the [[Bazaar of the Bold]]. A half-orc with a lean build and piercing yellow eyes, Korrin is known for his shrewd business sense and the secrecy that surrounds his dealings.

## Role

Korrin specialises in selling magical relics and artefacts, many of which have dark or forbidden origins. He also provides identification services for artefacts of unknown origin, offering his insight into their nature and potential uses.

## Appearance and Personality

- **Appearance:** Lean half-orc with dark grey-green skin and piercing yellow eyes, always dressed in dark, finely tailored clothing.
- **Personality:** Quiet and enigmatic, Korrin rarely engages in small talk. He is a shrewd businessman but fair in his dealings, with a reputation for honesty despite the secretive nature of his shop.

## Relationships

- **Rival:** [[Borak Stonefist]] - There’s a friendly rivalry between them, particularly when it comes to crafting or acquiring unique items.
- **Contact:** [[Lirael Brightshield]] - Occasionally trades artefacts for rare materials or information.

## Location

Korrin can be found at [[The Silver Sigil]] in the [[Bazaar of the Bold]].
