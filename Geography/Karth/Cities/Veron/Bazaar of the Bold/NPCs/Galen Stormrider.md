## Overview

**Galen Stormrider** is the **Expedition Coordinator** for the [[Verdant Blades]] and a key figure within the guild. A seasoned adventurer with years of experience in leading dangerous expeditions, Galen is known for his tactical expertise and calm demeanour. He is responsible for planning and organising the guild’s missions, ensuring that each expedition is properly staffed, equipped, and prepared for the challenges ahead.

## Role

As Expedition Coordinator, Galen’s primary role is to oversee the logistics and strategy of the Verdant Blades' missions, particularly those into the treacherous [[Sunken Vault]]. He works closely with guild members to assemble balanced teams, provides tactical advice, and ensures that each mission is planned with precision. Galen’s knowledge of the Vault and other dangerous locations is extensive, making him an invaluable resource for adventurers looking to succeed in their quests.

## Appearance and Personality

- **Appearance:** Galen is a tall, broad-shouldered human man with short-cropped grey hair and a few visible battle scars on his arms and face. His piercing blue eyes reflect a lifetime of experience and a deep understanding of the dangers that adventurers face. He typically wears sturdy leather armour, even within the guildhall, and carries a longsword at his side—a reminder of his days in the field.
- **Personality:** Galen is calm, methodical, and deeply committed to the success and safety of his fellow guild members. He is known for his tactical mind and his ability to remain cool under pressure, qualities that have earned him the respect and trust of those he leads. Though he can be stern, especially when it comes to mission preparation, Galen is also approachable and always willing to offer advice to less experienced adventurers.

## Relationships

- **Colleague:** [[Talia Windrunner]] - Works closely with Talia to coordinate guild activities and ensure that missions run smoothly. Their relationship is built on mutual respect and shared goals.
- **Mentor:** [[Elara Quickstrike]] - Often provides guidance and support to Elara, especially when it comes to assessing the skills of new recruits and matching them with appropriate missions.
- **Ally:** [[Lirael Brightshield]] - Occasionally consults with Lirael on gear recommendations and strategies for specific missions, valuing her expertise in combat and equipment.

## Location

Galen can be found at the [[Verdant Blades Guildhall]] in the [[Bazaar of the Bold]], where he spends most of his time planning missions, training guild members, and preparing for upcoming expeditions.

## Inventory and Services

### Services

- **Expedition Planning** - 50 gp per session
  - Detailed planning sessions where Galen assists adventurers in mapping out strategy, selecting team members, and ensuring that all necessary preparations are made for a successful mission.
- **Tactical Training** - 25 gp per hour
  - One-on-one or group training sessions focused on combat tactics, dungeon navigation, and survival skills, aimed at improving members’ chances of success in the field.
- **Mission Briefings** - Free with membership
  - Before each major expedition, Galen conducts thorough briefings, providing essential information about the mission objectives, potential threats, and tactical considerations.

## Notable Achievements

- **Mapping the Lower Vaults:** Galen played a crucial role in the Verdant Blades' efforts to chart a significant portion of the lower levels of the [[Sunken Vault]], a feat that has greatly benefited future expeditions.
- **Rescue of the Verdant Company:** Led a daring rescue mission to retrieve a stranded team of adventurers from deep within the Sunken Vault, navigating treacherous conditions and bringing all members back safely.
- **The Siege of the Stoneclaw Fortress:** A key strategist in the successful assault on the Stoneclaw Fortress, Galen’s tactical brilliance turned the tide of battle and secured a vital victory for the Verdant Blades.

## Personality Traits

- **Strategic Mind:** Galen is highly analytical and always thinks several steps ahead, making him a formidable planner and leader.
- **Mentor:** He takes pride in mentoring younger or less experienced adventurers, sharing his knowledge to help them grow and succeed.
- **Steady Under Pressure:** Galen remains calm in even the most dire situations, providing stability and reassurance to those around him.

## Affiliations

- **Verdant Blades:** As Expedition Coordinator, Galen is deeply connected to the guild’s activities and success. He is a trusted leader within the Verdant Blades and is dedicated to advancing the guild’s reputation and effectiveness.
- **Brightshield Mercenaries:** Has collaborated with the Brightshield Mercenaries on several joint operations, particularly in high-risk missions where additional manpower was needed.
