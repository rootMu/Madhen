## Overview

**Sariel Dawnglow** is a talented half-elf alchemist and enchanter who currently runs the popular stall [[Elixirs & Enchantments]] in the [[Bazaar of the Bold]] in [[Veron]]. Before moving to the bustling Bazaar, Sariel was the proprietor of a prominent shop in [[Alchemist's Row]] within the [[Solace Quarter]], where she built a strong reputation for her exceptional potions and enchanted items. After deciding to shift her focus and be closer to the adventurers who needed her services most, Sariel relocated to the Bazaar. She now rents out her old shop and laboratory in [[Alchemist's Row,]] while continuing her work in the Bazaar of the Bold, where she has become a trusted figure among adventurers, mages, and others seeking arcane remedies and enhancements.

## Appearance and Personality

- **Appearance:** Sariel is a slender half-elf with long, flowing golden hair that cascades down her back in soft waves. Her bright blue eyes reflect both warmth and a keen intelligence, always alert to the needs of her customers. She typically dresses in robes of deep green, often adorned with subtle patterns of leaves and vines, symbolising her connection to nature and the alchemical arts. Her fingers are often stained with the remnants of various magical ingredients, a testament to her constant work with potions and enchantments.

- **Personality:** Sariel is known for her friendly and approachable demeanour, making her a beloved figure in both the Solace Quarter and the Bazaar of the Bold. She is always eager to assist her customers, whether they are seasoned adventurers or curious novices. Sariel is deeply knowledgeable about her craft and enjoys sharing her insights with others, often taking the time to explain the properties of her potions and enchanted items. Despite her gentle nature, Sariel is meticulous and precise, ensuring that every creation she produces is of the highest quality. Her passion for magic and helping others is evident in everything she does.

## Skills and Expertise

- **Master Alchemist:** Sariel is highly skilled in the art of alchemy, capable of brewing a wide range of potions and elixirs that can heal, enhance, or even grant temporary magical abilities. Her potions are known for their potency and reliability, making them highly sought after by adventurers and mages alike.
- **Expert Enchanter:** In addition to her alchemical skills, Sariel is also a gifted enchanter. She can imbue mundane items with magical properties, creating enchanted items that provide various benefits to their users. Whether it’s a ring that stores spells or an amulet that offers protection, Sariel’s enchanted items are crafted with care and precision.
- **Custom Crafting:** Sariel offers custom potion brewing and enchantment services, allowing customers to request specific potions or enchantments tailored to their needs. Her ability to understand her customers’ desires and create exactly what they need has earned her a loyal clientele.

## History

Sariel Dawnglow originally ran a highly successful alchemy and enchantment shop on [[Alchemist's Row]] in the [[Solace Quarter]], a district known for its concentration of magical businesses and skilled artisans. Her shop became a staple of the community, attracting customers from all over Veron. However, as her reputation grew, Sariel decided to relocate her operations to the [[Bazaar of the Bold]], where she could be closer to the adventurers who frequently sought her potions and enchanted items. Since moving to the Bazaar, she has continued to thrive, while renting out her former shop and laboratory in the [[Solace Quarter]] to aspiring alchemists and enchanters looking to make their mark.

## Relationships

- **Customer:** [[Korrin Blackthistle]] - Korrin frequently visits Sariel’s stall to purchase rare ingredients and custom potions for his magical experiments and missions. They share a mutual respect for each other’s expertise.
- **Customer:** [[Talia Windrunner]] - Talia regularly buys potions and enchanted items from Sariel to aid her in leading the [[Verdant Blades]]. Sariel’s knowledge and precision have made her an invaluable ally to Talia.
- **Customer:** [[The Sirocco Lanterns]] - The Sirocco Lanterns often commission Sariel for custom elixirs that enhance their natural abilities, ensuring they are always prepared for their dangerous missions.

## Location

Sariel Dawnglow can be found at [[Elixirs & Enchantments]] in the [[Bazaar of the Bold]], where she spends her days crafting potions, enchanting items, and helping her customers find the perfect magical remedy or enhancement. Her former shop and laboratory in the [[Solace Quarter|Solace Quarter’s]] [[Alchemist's Row]] are now rented out, continuing to serve the community while Sariel focuses on her new venture in the Bazaar.

## Notable Creations

- **Elixir of Strength:** A powerful elixir that enhances physical strength, perfect for warriors preparing for battle.
- **Potion of Invisibility:** A rare and sought-after potion that grants the drinker the ability to become invisible for a short period.
- **Ring of Spell Storing:** A magical ring that can store up to 3 levels worth of spells, allowing the wearer to cast them later.

## Personality Traits

- **Compassionate:** Sariel is deeply caring and always seeks to understand and meet the needs of her customers, often going out of her way to ensure their satisfaction.
- **Curious:** Her love for magic and alchemy drives her to constantly experiment and improve her craft, always searching for new ways to help her customers.
- **Meticulous:** Sariel pays close attention to detail in all her work, ensuring that every potion, elixir, and enchanted item she creates meets her exacting standards.
