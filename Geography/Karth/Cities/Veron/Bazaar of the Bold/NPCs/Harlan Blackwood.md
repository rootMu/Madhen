## Overview

**Harlan Blackwood** is the master blacksmith and proprietor of [[The Iron Edge]], a renowned armour and weaponry stall in the [[Bazaar of the Bold]] in [[Veron]]. Known for his exceptional craftsmanship and attention to detail, Harlan has earned a reputation as one of the most reliable and skilled blacksmiths in the region. Adventurers and mercenaries alike seek out Harlan’s services, trusting in the durability and effectiveness of the weapons and armour he forges.

## Appearance and Personality

- **Appearance:** Harlan is a tall, broad-shouldered man in his late forties, with a strong, muscular build honed by years of hard labour at the forge. He has a thick, well-kept beard that is flecked with grey, and his hair, though often covered by a leather cap, is similarly streaked with grey. His hands are calloused and scarred from years of working with hot metal, and his arms are often dusted with soot and sweat. He is usually seen wearing a heavy leather apron over simple, sturdy clothes that are practical for the intense work of blacksmithing.
  
- **Personality:** Harlan is straightforward and no-nonsense, with little patience for idle chatter or frivolity. He values hard work, honesty, and the pursuit of excellence in his craft. While he may come across as gruff or distant, Harlan is deeply committed to his customers, ensuring that every piece of equipment that leaves his forge meets his exacting standards. He has a dry sense of humour and, despite his serious demeanor, takes quiet pride in his work and the role he plays in equipping Veron’s adventurers.

## Skills and Expertise

- **Master Blacksmith:** Harlan’s expertise in blacksmithing is unmatched, with a particular focus on creating weapons and armour that are both functional and durable. He is capable of working with a variety of materials, including iron, steel, and even more exotic metals.
- **Custom Weapon and Armour Crafting:** Harlan excels at custom orders, crafting pieces tailored to the specific needs of his clients. His attention to detail ensures that each item is perfectly balanced and fitted for its user.
- **Enchantment Collaboration:** Although not a mage himself, Harlan collaborates closely with the [[Enchanter’s Conclave]] to offer enchanted weapons and armour. His ability to seamlessly integrate enchantments into his work makes him a sought-after blacksmith for those requiring magical gear.

## Relationships

- **Colleague:** [[Davrick Stonehill]] - Harlan has a professional relationship with Davrick, often referring customers to each other. They share a mutual respect for each other’s crafts.
- **Customer:** [[Korrin Blackthistle]] - Occasionally commissions special orders from Harlan, particularly for enchanted or rare materials. Though their dealings are mostly professional, they share a cautious respect for each other's expertise.
- **Ally:** [[Borak Stonefist]] - Borak and Harlan share a bond as master craftsmen, often exchanging techniques and insights about their respective trades. They are both well-known figures in the Bazaar and maintain a friendly, if competitive, relationship.

## Location

Harlan can be found at [[The Iron Edge]] in the [[Bazaar of the Bold]], where he spends most of his time working the forge, crafting weapons and armour, and attending to customers. The stall’s prominent location within the Bazaar ensures a steady stream of business, and Harlan’s reputation draws clients from across Veron and beyond.

## Notable Works

- **Ironclad Plate Armour:** A custom set of plate armour crafted for a prominent knight of Veron, known for its exceptional durability and flawless craftsmanship.
- **Battle Axe of Cleaving:** A powerful weapon designed for a veteran warrior, renowned for its perfect balance and devastating effectiveness in combat.
- **Enchanted Longsword:** Crafted in collaboration with the [[Enchanter’s Conclave]], this longsword was imbued with a fire enchantment, becoming a prized possession of its owner.

## Personality Traits

- **Perfectionist:** Harlan is dedicated to his craft and refuses to sell any item that does not meet his high standards. This perfectionism has earned him a loyal customer base but also means he can be exacting in his work.
- **Honest:** He is straightforward with his customers, providing honest advice and opinions on the best equipment for their needs.
