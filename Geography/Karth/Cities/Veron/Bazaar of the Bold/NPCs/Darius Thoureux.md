# Scribe Darius Thoureux

## Overview

**Scribe Darius Thoureux**, is responsible for documentation and certification at [[Enchanter’s Conclave]] within the [[Bazaar of the Bold]]. A meticulous and organised individual, Darius ensures that all artefacts identified by the Conclave are properly documented and that their certifications are accurate and up-to-date.

## Role

Darius handles the administrative side of the Conclave’s operations, maintaining detailed records of all identified artefacts and issuing official certifications. His role is crucial in ensuring that artefacts can be legally sold or used within Veron, and he takes great pride in the accuracy and thoroughness of his work.

## Appearance and Personality

- **Appearance:** A middle-aged human man with neatly combed brown hair and spectacles perched on the bridge of his nose. He is always seen wearing well-pressed robes, often with ink stains on his fingers from his work.
- **Personality:** Darius is precise and methodical, with a keen eye for detail. He is known for his dry sense of humour and his insistence on perfection in all things. While he may seem stern, he is deeply committed to his work and takes great satisfaction in a job well done.

## Relationships

- **Colleague:** [[Lysandra Ravenshadow | Mage Lysandra Ravenshadow]] - Works closely with Lysandra to ensure that all artefacts are accurately documented and certified.
- **Supervisor:** [[Theron Eldryn | Master Enchanter Theron Eldryn]] - Darius respects Theron’s expertise and often consults with him on the more complex artefacts.

## Location

Darius can be found at [[Enchanter’s Conclave]] in the [[Bazaar of the Bold]].
