## Overview

**Borak Stonefist** is the owner and master armorer of [[Stonefist’s Forge]] in the [[Bazaar of the Bold]]. A grizzled dwarf with a thick beard braided with small trinkets from his adventuring days, Borak is a skilled craftsman who specializes in creating and repairing armor and weapons, particularly those designed for the unique challenges of the [[Sunken Vault]].

## Role

Borak offers custom armor and weapons, as well as reliable repair services to adventurers. His forge is known for producing some of the most durable and effective gear available, tailored to withstand the perils of the Vault.

## Appearance and Personality

- **Appearance:** Muscular dwarf with a thick, braided beard, often seen wearing a leather apron and smithing goggles.
- **Personality:** Gruff but fair, Borak is practical and no-nonsense. He enjoys regaling customers with stories from his adventuring days, though he rarely speaks of why he retired.

## Relationships

- **Friend:** [[Elandra Mossveil]] - Often exchanges goods and advice with her.
- **Rival:** [[Korrin Blackthistle]] - Though they respect each other’s skills, there’s a friendly rivalry between them, particularly when it comes to crafting unique items.

## Location

Borak can be found at [[Stonefist’s Forge]] in the [[Bazaar of the Bold]].
