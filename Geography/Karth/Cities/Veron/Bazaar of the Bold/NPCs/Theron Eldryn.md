## Overview

**Master Enchanter Theron Eldryn** is the head enchanter at [[Enchanter’s Conclave]], the government-run office within the [[Bazaar of the Bold]]. Theron is an elderly human with a commanding presence and decades of experience in the arcane arts. He oversees the identification and certification of artefacts brought out of the [[Sunken Vault]], ensuring they are safe for use within Veron.

## Role

Theron leads the team of enchanters at the Conclave, providing expert analysis of magical items and artefacts. He is also responsible for certifying these items, a service required for their legal sale or use within the city. Theron’s deep understanding of ancient magics makes him particularly adept at detecting curses and hidden enchantments.

## Appearance and Personality

- **Appearance:** An elderly human with silver hair and a long, neatly trimmed beard. He is always dressed in ornate robes that denote his rank and expertise, with intricate patterns of gold thread woven into the fabric.
- **Personality:** Stern and meticulous, Theron is highly respected for his knowledge and experience. He is thorough in his work and has little patience for those who waste his time. Despite his stern exterior, he is deeply committed to ensuring the safety of Veron and its citizens.

## Relationships

- **Colleague:** [[Lysandra Ravenshadow]] - They work closely together, with Theron often guiding Lysandra in the more complex aspects of artefact identification.
- **Contact:** [[Korrin Blackthistle]] - Occasionally consults with Korrin regarding particularly rare or mysterious artefacts.

## Location

Theron can be found at [[Enchanter’s Conclave]] in the [[Bazaar of the Bold]].
