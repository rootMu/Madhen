## Overview

**Davrick Stonehill** is the proprietor of [[Stonehill Provisions]], a well-stocked provision stall in the [[Bazaar of the Bold]]. Known for his friendly demeanour and reliable goods, Davrick is a trusted source of essential supplies for adventurers preparing to delve into the [[Sunken Vault]]. He has a deep knowledge of the needs of adventurers and takes pride in offering quality items at fair prices.

## Role

Davrick provides a wide range of basic supplies, from food and water to camping gear and tools. His stall is one of the first stops for many adventurers entering the Bazaar, ensuring they have everything they need for their journey. In addition to selling provisions, Davrick is known to offer helpful advice to less experienced adventurers, drawing from his own youthful exploits.

## Appearance and Personality

- **Appearance:** A sturdy, middle-aged human man with a broad build, short brown hair, and a neatly trimmed beard. He often wears simple, practical clothing suitable for the demands of his trade, with an apron that holds various tools and supplies.
- **Personality:** Davrick is warm, welcoming, and always ready with a smile. He has a reputation for honesty and fairness, which has earned him the loyalty of many repeat customers. Though no longer an adventurer himself, Davrick has a wealth of knowledge about the basics of exploration and survival, which he freely shares with those in need.

## Relationships

- **Friend:** [[Borak Stonefist]] - Davrick and Borak often exchange goods and stories, with Borak occasionally crafting special tools or items for Davrick to sell.
- **Ally:** [[Elandra Mossveil]] - They maintain a friendly business relationship, often referring customers to each other when their needs overlap.

## Location

Davrick can be found at [[Stonehill Provisions]] in the [[Bazaar Of The Bold]].
