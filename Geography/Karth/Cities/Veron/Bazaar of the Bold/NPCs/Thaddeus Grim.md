## Overview

**Thaddeus Grim** is a bard and chronicler well-known in the [[Bazaar of the Bold]]. With a deep, resonant voice and a flair for the dramatic, Thaddeus captivates audiences with his tales of adventure, danger, and the mysteries of the [[Sunken Vault]]. Though he is a performer by trade, Thaddeus’s stories are often more than mere entertainment—they are a source of valuable lore and information for those brave enough to venture into the Vault.

## Role

Thaddeus plays a dual role in the Bazaar: entertainer and informer. His performances are sought after by adventurers and common folk alike, but those who listen carefully can often discern useful information and warnings hidden within his tales. Thaddeus also offers personalised ballads and stories, memorialising the deeds of adventurers who seek to leave their mark on history.

## Appearance and Personality

- **Appearance:** A tall, slender human man with long, dark hair and a well-groomed goatee. He typically wears a dark cloak and carries a lute on his back, his sharp and expressive eyes capable of captivating an audience with just a glance.
- **Personality:** Thaddeus is charismatic and theatrical, always ready to spin a tale or sing a song. Despite his outwardly jovial nature, there is an air of melancholy about him, as though he carries a heavy burden from his past. He is generous with his stories but can be guarded about his own history.

## Relationships

- **Friend:** [[Elandra Mossveil]] - Thaddeus often visits Elandra for herbal remedies and potions to maintain his voice and health.
- **Contact:** [[Korrin Blackthistle]] - Occasionally shares information with Korrin in exchange for rare artefacts or magical items.
- **Patron:** [[Verdant Blades Guildhall]] - Thaddeus is sometimes hired by the Verdant Blades to perform at their events, commemorating their exploits.

## Location

Thaddeus can usually be found performing or socialising at various stalls and shops within the [[Bazaar of the Bold]].

## Inventory and Services

### Services

- **Personalised Ballads** - 50 gp
  - Thaddeus will compose a ballad commemorating a particular adventure or heroic deed.
- **Lore and Tales** - 10 gp per session
  - Detailed stories about the history of the Sunken Vault, including insights and potential warnings.
- **Performance** - 5 gp per song/story
  - A single performance, often a song or a short story, provided to entertain or inspire.

### Notable Performances

- **The Fall of Threnaliath** - A dramatic retelling of the conquest of the Lost Dungeon of [[Threnaliath]] by King Vorenus, filled with suspense and heroism.
- **The Lament of the Vault** - A haunting ballad that speaks of the many adventurers lost to the depths of the Sunken Vault, and the treasures they left behind.
- **The Song of the Verdant Blades** - A rousing anthem celebrating the deeds of the [[Verdant Blades]], a favourite among guild members.
