## Overview

**Elara Quickstrike** is the **Recruitment Officer** for the [[Verdant Blades]], responsible for scouting and recruiting new talent for the guild. A nimble and sharp-witted elf, Elara has a keen eye for potential and is adept at assessing the skills of adventurers seeking to join the Verdant Blades. Her swift movements and quick thinking have earned her the nickname “Quickstrike,” a reflection of her abilities both in and out of combat.

## Role

As Recruitment Officer, Elara’s primary responsibility is to identify and recruit skilled adventurers to join the Verdant Blades. She conducts interviews, oversees trials, and ensures that new recruits are properly integrated into the guild. Elara is known for her discerning judgment and high standards, ensuring that only the most capable and reliable adventurers become members of the Verdant Blades.

## Appearance and Personality

- **Appearance:** Elara is a lithe elf with sharp features, emerald green eyes, and long, braided blonde hair. She often wears light, flexible armour that allows her to move swiftly and silently, and she carries a pair of finely crafted daggers at her side. Her agile build and graceful movements reflect her expertise in stealth and close combat.
- **Personality:** Elara is quick-witted and observant, with a knack for reading people. She is confident in her abilities and has a no-nonsense attitude when it comes to recruitment. Despite her serious demeanour, she is fiercely loyal to the Verdant Blades and takes great pride in finding the best new talent. Her sharp tongue and keen mind make her a formidable presence within the guild.

## Relationships

- **Mentor:** [[Galen Stormrider]] - Elara looks up to Galen as a mentor and often seeks his guidance when assessing new recruits or preparing for missions.
- **Colleague:** [[Talia Windrunner]] - Works closely with Talia to ensure the guild continues to attract skilled and reliable members. Their relationship is built on mutual respect and shared dedication to the guild’s success.
- **Contact:** [[Borak Stonefist]] - Occasionally consults with Borak to provide recruits with recommendations on their equipment needs, ensuring they are properly outfitted for their first missions.

## Location

Elara can be found at the [[Verdant Blades Guildhall]] in the [[Bazaar of the Bold]], where she spends her time interviewing potential recruits, overseeing training sessions, and coordinating with other guild members.

## Inventory and Services

### Services

- **Skills Assessment** - 20 gp per assessment
  - Elara conducts a thorough evaluation of an adventurer’s skills, providing feedback and advice on areas of strength and improvement.
- **Recruitment Trials** - Varies by recruit
  - Potential members undergo a series of trials designed by Elara to test their abilities, including combat, strategy, and survival skills.
- **Talent Scouting** - Free with membership
  - Elara is always on the lookout for promising new talent, often scouting the Bazaar and surrounding areas for adventurers who show potential.

## Notable Achievements

- **The Scouting of the Silver Path:** Elara led a team of scouts through a dangerous and uncharted section of the Sunken Vault, successfully mapping the area and discovering several hidden chambers filled with valuable artefacts.
- **Recruitment of the Nightblades:** Elara played a key role in recruiting a group of elite adventurers known as the [[Nightblades]], who have since become one of the Verdant Blades’ most effective teams.
- **[[The Trial of the Ironwood Glade]]:** Elara designed and oversaw a challenging recruitment trial in the Ironwood Glade, where prospective members had to survive and navigate a perilous forest filled with magical traps and creatures.

## Personality Traits

- **Keen Observer:** Elara has a sharp eye for detail, allowing her to quickly assess a person’s strengths and weaknesses.
- **Strategic Mind:** She is always thinking ahead, planning for contingencies and ensuring that recruits are well-prepared for any situation.
- **Loyal and Protective:** Elara is deeply committed to the Verdant Blades and takes her responsibility for the safety and success of the guild’s members very seriously.

## Affiliations

- **Verdant Blades:** As Recruitment Officer, Elara is integral to the growth and strength of the guild. Her efforts ensure that the Verdant Blades continue to thrive and maintain their reputation as one of Veron’s premier adventuring guilds.
- **Mossveil Remedies:** Frequently visits this provision stall to acquire potions and supplies for recruits, ensuring they are well-prepared for their missions.
