## Overview

**Lirael Brightshield** is a formidable warrior and the leader of the renowned [[Brightshield Mercenaries]], a company of elite fighters known for their discipline, honour, and effectiveness in battle. Based in [[Veron]], Lirael is also the proprietor of [[Brightshield’s Armoury]], which doubles as the headquarters for the mercenary company. Under her leadership, the Brightshield Mercenaries have become one of the most sought-after forces for hire in the region, known for taking on the most dangerous and challenging contracts. Lirael’s commitment to both her mercenaries and her business has cemented her as a key figure in Veron’s military and economic circles.

## Appearance and Personality

- **Appearance:** Lirael is a tall and imposing woman with a strong, muscular build. Her long blonde hair is usually tied back in a braid, and her piercing blue eyes reflect both determination and intelligence. She wears a set of finely crafted plate armour, emblazoned with the Brightshield crest—a shining shield with a radiant sunburst behind it. Despite her formidable appearance, Lirael carries herself with a calm and measured demeanour, always in control of her emotions.

- **Personality:** Lirael is a natural leader, known for her decisive and fair approach to command. She is fiercely loyal to her mercenaries and expects the same level of dedication from them. Lirael values honour and integrity, and she has earned the respect of her peers and subordinates alike for her unwavering commitment to these principles. Despite her stern exterior, Lirael is deeply compassionate, particularly towards those under her command. She takes their well-being seriously and ensures that they are always prepared and supported in their missions.

## Involvement with the Brightshield Mercenaries

Lirael Brightshield founded the [[Brightshield Mercenaries]] after leaving a prestigious position in Veron’s city guard. Frustrated by the bureaucracy and corruption she witnessed, Lirael decided to create a company that operated with the honour and discipline she believed was lacking in the official forces. Drawing on her extensive military experience, Lirael recruited only the most skilled and trustworthy warriors, training them to uphold the strict code of conduct she had established.

Under Lirael’s leadership, the Brightshield Mercenaries quickly gained a reputation for their reliability and effectiveness. They have taken on contracts ranging from protecting trade caravans to aiding in full-scale battles, always delivering results with professionalism and honour. Lirael personally oversees every contract, ensuring that her mercenaries are well-equipped and well-informed before they set out on a mission. Her tactical acumen and strategic thinking have made her a key figure in Veron’s military circles, and she is often sought after for her advice on matters of defence and warfare.

## Proprietor of Brightshield’s Armoury

In addition to leading the mercenary company, Lirael is also the proprietor of [[Brightshield’s Armoury]], a well-respected establishment in Veron that supplies high-quality weapons, armour, and adventuring gear. The armoury also serves as the headquarters for the Brightshield Mercenaries, providing a secure and well-equipped base of operations for the company. Lirael takes great pride in the craftsmanship of the items sold at the armoury, ensuring that only the best equipment is available to her mercenaries and customers alike. The dual role of running the armoury and leading the mercenaries allows Lirael to maintain tight control over the quality and readiness of her forces.

## Relationships

- **Ally:** [[Thorian Stoneheart]] - The Guildmaster of the [[Adventurers' Guild]], Thorian and Lirael share a mutual respect for each other’s leadership and commitment to their respective organisations. They occasionally collaborate on large-scale operations that require both the adventurers and the mercenaries to work together.
- **Mentor:** [[Jarek Ironfist]] - The head trainer at the Adventurers' Guild, Jarek was once Lirael’s mentor during her early years in the city guard. Their bond remains strong, with Jarek often consulting Lirael on training techniques and combat tactics.
- **Rival:** [[Captain Gaius Valen]] - A high-ranking officer in Veron’s city guard, Gaius and Lirael have a complicated history. While they respect each other’s abilities, they often clash over their differing views on how to handle threats to the city.

## Location

Lirael Brightshield can often be found at [[Brightshield’s Armoury]], located in Veron. The armoury serves as the headquarters for the [[Brightshield Mercenaries]], where Lirael manages contracts, oversees the training of her mercenaries, and ensures the quality of the equipment sold in her shop. When she is not at the armoury, Lirael frequently visits the [[Adventurers' Guild]] to maintain her ties with the city’s other warriors and strategists.

## Notable Achievements

- **Founding the Brightshield Mercenaries:** Lirael’s most significant achievement is the creation and successful leadership of the Brightshield Mercenaries, turning them into a respected and powerful force in the region.
- **Battle of the Broken Pass:** Lirael led her mercenaries in a critical battle at the Broken Pass, where they held off a much larger enemy force, earning the Brightshield Mercenaries their reputation as one of the most formidable fighting forces in Veron.
- **Operation Nightfall:** A covert mission to infiltrate and dismantle a dangerous criminal syndicate operating in Veron. Lirael’s strategic planning and leadership were key to the operation’s success.

## Personality Traits

- **Honourable:** Lirael holds herself and her mercenaries to the highest standards of honour, ensuring that they conduct themselves with integrity on and off the battlefield.
- **Tactical:** Her strategic mind and ability to anticipate enemy movements make her an exceptional leader in combat situations.
- **Compassionate:** Despite her tough exterior, Lirael cares deeply for her mercenaries and the people of Veron, always prioritising their safety and well-being.
