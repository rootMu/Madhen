## Overview

**Mage Lysandra Ravenshadow** is an identification specialist at [[Enchanter’s Conclave]] within the [[Bazaar of the Bold]]. A young but talented mage, Lysandra is responsible for analysing artefacts brought out of the [[Sunken Vault]], identifying their magical properties, and detecting any curses or dangerous enchantments.

## Role

Lysandra plays a key role in the identification process at the Conclave, providing detailed reports on the magical items and artefacts that pass through the office. Her keen eye for detail and growing expertise make her a valuable asset to the team, particularly in the identification of complex or ancient magic.

## Appearance and Personality

- **Appearance:** A young human woman with striking blue eyes and shoulder-length black hair, often seen wearing simple but elegant robes. She carries a staff adorned with a small crystal that glows faintly when she uses her magic.
- **Personality:** Lysandra is diligent and eager to prove herself. She is passionate about her work and has a natural curiosity about the artefacts she examines. Though relatively new to her role, she is determined to learn all she can from [[Theron Eldryn | Master Enchanter Theron Eldryn]].

## Relationships

- **Mentor:** [[Theron Eldryn | Master Enchanter Theron Eldryn]] - Lysandra greatly respects Theron and often seeks his guidance in more challenging identification tasks.
- **Colleague:** [[Darius Thoureux]] - Works closely with Darius to ensure accurate documentation and certification of artefacts.

## Location

Lysandra can be found at [[Enchanter’s Conclave]] in the [[Bazaar of the Bold]].
