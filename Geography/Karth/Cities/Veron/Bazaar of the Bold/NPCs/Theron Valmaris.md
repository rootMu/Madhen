## Overview

**Archivist Theron Valmaris** is the leader and representative of the [[Seekers of Knowledge]], a prestigious guild of scholars, mages, and historians based in [[Veron]]. With decades of experience in the study of ancient texts and artefacts, Theron is known for his vast knowledge, meticulous nature, and unwavering dedication to the pursuit of knowledge. As the driving force behind the Seekers of Knowledge, Theron has played a crucial role in many of the guild’s most significant discoveries and expeditions.

## Appearance and Personality

- **Appearance:** Theron Valmaris is an older man with a dignified bearing. He has a tall, lean frame, and his posture reflects both his scholarly discipline and the weight of years spent in study. His hair, once a deep chestnut, is now mostly grey, neatly tied back in a short ponytail. His eyes, a sharp grey-blue, seem to hold the wisdom of countless years of study and experience. Theron typically dresses in fine robes of dark blue and silver, adorned with symbols of knowledge and arcane power. He carries with him a staff of polished wood, intricately carved with runes of protection and knowledge, a symbol of his status within the guild.

- **Personality:** Theron is known for his calm and deliberate manner. He approaches every task with methodical precision, ensuring that nothing is overlooked. His dedication to the pursuit of knowledge is matched only by his sense of responsibility to preserve and protect the artefacts and texts that the Seekers of Knowledge recover. While he can be stern and demanding, especially when it comes to maintaining the high standards of the guild, Theron is also deeply compassionate and cares deeply for the well-being of his colleagues and the preservation of knowledge for future generations.

## Role in The Seekers of Knowledge

As the **Archivist** and leader of the [[Seekers of Knowledge]], Theron Valmaris is responsible for overseeing all guild operations. This includes planning and coordinating research expeditions, managing the guild’s vast collection of artefacts and texts, and ensuring that the guild’s activities align with its mission to uncover and preserve ancient knowledge. Theron’s extensive knowledge and experience make him the final authority on which artefacts and texts are of significant value and how they should be studied and preserved.

### Key Responsibilities

- **Expedition Planning:** Theron is deeply involved in the planning of all major research expeditions undertaken by the guild. He determines the objectives, selects the team members, and often personally oversees the most critical missions.
- **Artefact Preservation:** Theron ensures that all artefacts recovered by the guild are properly documented, studied, and preserved. He works closely with the guild’s scholars and mages to unlock the secrets of these ancient items.
- **Guild Representation:** As the face of the [[Seekers of Knowledge]], Theron represents the guild in Veron’s scholarly and magical circles. He liaises with other organisations, such as the [[Adventurers Guild]] and [[Enchanter’s Conclave|The Enchanter’s Conclave]], to further the guild’s goals.

## Relationships

- **Colleague:** [[Kael Durnan|Librarian Kael Durnan]] - Kael is a trusted colleague who manages the library and map room within the [[Adventurers Guild]]. Theron and Kael often collaborate on research projects and the preservation of rare texts.
- **Ally:** [[Theron Eldryn|Master Enchanter Theron Eldryn]] - Another Theron within Veron, Eldryn is the Master Enchanter at [[Enchanter’s Conclave|The Enchanter’s Conclave]]. The two Therons share a professional respect and often consult each other on matters of magical artefacts.
- **Mentor:** Theron has mentored many young scholars and mages within the Seekers of Knowledge, passing on his vast knowledge and ensuring that the guild’s mission continues for generations to come.

## Notable Achievements

- **Discovery of the Lost Tomes of Threnaliath:** Theron led the expedition into the [[Sunken Vault]] that resulted in the discovery of the Lost Tomes of Threnaliath. These ancient texts have become some of the most valuable and well-studied items in the guild’s collection.
- **The Preservation of the Azurite Amulet:** Under Theron’s guidance, the guild recovered and preserved the Azurite Amulet, a powerful and historically significant artefact. His careful handling of the amulet ensured that its magical properties were fully understood and documented.
- **The Restoration of the Crystal of Eldara:** Theron played a key role in the restoration of the Crystal of Eldara, a complex and delicate process that required his deep understanding of both ancient magic and modern restoration techniques.

## Personality Traits

- **Meticulous:** Theron approaches every task with methodical precision, ensuring that all details are accounted for and nothing is overlooked.
- **Wise:** His extensive knowledge and experience have made him a wellspring of wisdom, both in matters of scholarship and in the practicalities of leadership.
- **Compassionate:** Despite his stern exterior, Theron cares deeply about the preservation of knowledge and the well-being of his colleagues and students.

## Location

Theron Valmaris can typically be found at the headquarters of the [[Seekers of Knowledge]] in Veron, where he oversees the guild’s operations and conducts his research. He often spends his days in the grand library, surrounded by ancient texts and artefacts, diligently working to unlock the secrets of the past.
