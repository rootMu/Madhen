## Overview

**Mossveil Remedies** is a well-known provision stall in the [[Bazaar of the Bold]], specialising in potions, antidotes, and rare herbs. The stall is run by [[Elandra Mossveil]], a knowledgeable herbalist with a deep understanding of the flora found both in Veron and within the [[Sunken Vault]]. Her potions are highly sought after by adventurers preparing for the dangers of the Vault.

## Services Offered

- **Potions:** Healing potions, underwater breathing potions, and potions of strength.
- **Antidotes:** Remedies for poisons and venomous bites.
- **Rare Herbs:** Special herbs used in potion-making or for specific effects, such as enhancing night vision or resistance to cold.

## Inventory

### Potions
- **Potion of Healing** - 50 gp
  - A basic healing potion that restores 2d4+2 hit points.
- **Potion of Greater Healing** - 150 gp
  - A more potent healing potion that restores 4d4+4 hit points.
- **Potion of Water Breathing** - 100 gp
  - Allows the drinker to breathe underwater for 1 hour.
- **Potion of Strength** - 200 gp
  - Increases the drinker's Strength score by 2 for 1 hour.
- **Potion of Night Vision** - 75 gp
  - Grants darkvision out to 60 feet for 8 hours.

### Antidotes
- **Antitoxin** - 25 gp
  - Grants advantage on saving throws against poison for 1 hour.
- **Venom Antidote** - 40 gp
  - Cures poison from snake or spider bites, effective immediately.

### Rare Herbs
- **Silverleaf** - 10 gp per bundle
  - A rare herb used to brew potions that enhance magical abilities.
- **Frostcap Mushroom** - 25 gp per mushroom
  - A cold-resistant mushroom that is a key ingredient in potions of cold resistance.
- **Firebloom** - 30 gp per flower
  - A bright red flower that can be used in the creation of potions of fire resistance.

## Employees

- **[[Elandra Mossveil]]** - Owner and Herbalist
