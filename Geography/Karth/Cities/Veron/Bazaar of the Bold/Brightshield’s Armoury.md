## Overview

**Brightshield’s Armoury** is a premier weaponry and armour shop located in the [[Bazaar of the Bold]] in [[Veron]], renowned for its high-quality equipment and strong ties to the [[Brightshield Mercenaries]]. Owned and operated by the formidable warrior **[[Lirael Brightshield]]**, the armoury is not just a place to purchase weapons and armour; it also serves as the headquarters for the Brightshield Mercenaries. The armoury is known for its exceptional craftsmanship and the bespoke gear tailored to the needs of its clients, whether they are seasoned mercenaries, adventurers, or members of Veron’s city guard.

## Layout and Atmosphere

Brightshield’s Armoury is a spacious and well-organised establishment, with every inch of the shop designed to showcase the finest weapons, armour, and adventuring gear. The front of the shop features a wide display of swords, shields, and armour sets, each piece meticulously crafted and maintained. The walls are adorned with the Brightshield crest—a shining shield with a radiant sunburst behind it—symbolising the armoury’s commitment to quality and honour.

In the back of the shop lies the workshop, where skilled artisans and blacksmiths work tirelessly to produce and maintain the armoury’s inventory. The workshop is equipped with forges, anvils, and racks of tools, creating an environment of constant productivity and craftsmanship. Adjacent to the workshop is the war room, a strategic planning area used by Lirael and her officers to plan missions and review contracts for the [[Brightshield Mercenaries]].

## Special Services

### Mercenary Supplies

As the headquarters of the [[Brightshield Mercenaries]], the armoury is equipped to supply the company with all the gear and provisions they need for their missions. This includes not only weapons and armour but also rations, medical supplies, and specialised equipment tailored to the requirements of each contract.

### Strategic Planning

The war room within Brightshield’s Armoury is used by Lirael and her officers to plan and coordinate mercenary operations. This space is equipped with detailed maps, tactical models, and communication tools, allowing the mercenaries to prepare thoroughly before embarking on a mission. The armoury’s proximity to the planning area ensures that any adjustments to gear or supplies can be made swiftly and efficiently.

- **[[Elara Quickstrike]]** - A regular customer, Elara prefers the lightweight and stealthy armour crafted by Brightshield’s artisans, perfect for her style of combat.
- **[[Korrin Blackthistle]]** - Occasionally purchases custom weapons from the armoury, valuing the craftsmanship and precision that goes into each piece.

## Proprietor

### Lirael Brightshield

As the owner and operator of Brightshield’s Armoury, **[[Lirael Brightshield]]** oversees all aspects of the business, from crafting and sales to the strategic operations of the [[Brightshield Mercenaries]]. Lirael’s hands-on approach ensures that every piece of equipment sold in the armoury meets the highest standards of quality and reliability. Her dual role as both a business owner and a military leader allows her to maintain tight control over the readiness and effectiveness of her mercenaries, making Brightshield’s Armoury not just a shop, but a vital part of Veron’s defence network.

## Location

Brightshield’s Armoury is strategically located in the [[Bazaar of the Bold]] in Veron, making it easily accessible to both the city’s residents and the [[Brightshield Mercenaries]]. The shop’s central location within the Bazaar ensures a steady stream of customers, ranging from adventurers and soldiers to nobles seeking custom-made weapons and armour.
