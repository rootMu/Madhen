## Overview

**The Hidden Path** is a discreet and well-guarded shop located in a shadowy corner of the [[Bazaar of the Bold]] in [[Veron]]. Specialising in items and equipment designed for stealth, infiltration, and subterfuge, this shop is a haven for rogues, spies, and those who prefer to operate from the shadows. The shop is run by **[[Faelen Quickstep]]**, a nimble and cunning halfling with a reputation for acquiring and crafting some of the most effective tools of the trade. Whether you’re in need of a smoke bomb, lockpicks, or a cloak that can render you invisible, The Hidden Path has everything a shadowy operator could desire.

## Layout and Atmosphere

The Hidden Path is tucked away in a less-travelled section of the Bazaar, its entrance partially concealed by hanging drapery and subtle illusions. Inside, the shop is dimly lit, with shelves and display cases filled with an array of items meant for those who prefer the cover of darkness. The walls are adorned with maps, scrolls, and diagrams, some of which depict elaborate heists and escape routes. A low counter at the back serves as Faelen’s workspace, where he can often be seen tinkering with small mechanical devices or brewing up new alchemical concoctions. The atmosphere is one of secrecy and intrigue, perfectly suited to the shop’s clientele.

## Products and Services

### Tools of the Trade

- **Set of Masterwork Lockpicks** - 100 gp
  - A finely crafted set of lockpicks, capable of opening even the most complex locks with ease. (Grants advantage on Dexterity checks to pick locks)
- **Smoke Bomb** - 50 gp
  - A small, spherical bomb that releases a thick cloud of smoke upon detonation, perfect for creating a quick escape. (Covers a 20-foot radius in thick smoke, obscuring vision)
- **Grappling Hook and Rope** - 75 gp
  - A sturdy grappling hook with a 50-foot length of silk rope, designed for scaling walls and making daring escapes.

### Stealth and Infiltration Gear

- **Cloak of Shadows** - 400 gp
  - An enchanted cloak that renders the wearer nearly invisible in dim light or darkness. (Grants advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks in low-light conditions)
- **Boots of Silent Step** - 300 gp
  - Light, flexible boots that muffle the wearer’s footsteps, making it easier to move unnoticed. (Grants advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks)
- **Ring of Disguise** - 500 gp
  - A magical ring that allows the wearer to cast *Disguise Self* at will, perfect for those who need to change their appearance on the fly.

### Information and Espionage

- **Maps of Secret Tunnels** - 150 gp
  - Detailed maps of hidden passages and escape routes throughout Veron and beyond, invaluable for planning heists or evading pursuit.
- **Poisoner’s Kit** - 100 gp
  - A complete set of tools and ingredients for crafting various poisons, from paralytics to deadly toxins. (Includes basic instructions for safe handling and use)
- **Encrypted Messages** - Price varies
  - Faelen offers a message encryption service, ensuring that sensitive information remains secure during transit. He also deciphers encrypted messages for those in need.

### Custom Orders

- **Custom Trap Design** - Price varies
  - Faelen is an expert in designing traps for all purposes, from simple snares to elaborate mechanisms. Customers can commission traps tailored to their specific needs, whether for protecting valuables or ensnaring enemies.
- **Personalised Gear** - Price varies
  - The Hidden Path offers custom modifications to existing equipment, such as reinforcing lockpicks or adding additional enchantments to cloaks and boots. Faelen’s expertise ensures that each item is perfectly suited to its user.

## Proprietor

### Faelen Quickstep

**Faelen Quickstep** is the cunning and resourceful halfling who owns and operates The Hidden Path. With a background steeped in the art of stealth and a reputation for being able to acquire just about anything, Faelen is well-connected within Veron’s underworld. His shop is a reflection of his skills and his network, offering everything a rogue, spy, or adventurer might need to complete their missions discreetly.

## Location

The Hidden Path is located within the [[Bazaar of the Bold]], tucked away in a shadowy corner that’s easy to miss if you don’t know where to look. The shop’s location reflects its purpose—catering to those who value secrecy and discretion above all else. Faelen’s reputation ensures that those who need his services will find him, no matter how well-hidden his shop may be.
