## Overview

**The Nightblades** are an elite team within the [[Verdant Blades]] guild, known for their exceptional skills in stealth, subterfuge, and assassination. Composed entirely of rogue archetypes, the Nightblades are often called upon for the most dangerous and delicate missions, where discretion and precision are paramount. Their reputation as shadowy operatives has earned them both fear and respect among adventurers and enemies alike. What sets the Nightblades apart is their unique blend of roguish talents, including spell casting, assassination, and deception. Among their ranks is a particularly enigmatic member—a jester known as **[[Rictus]]**, who uses trickery, illusion, and deadly humour to confound and eliminate foes.

## Structure and Membership

The Nightblades are a small, tightly-knit unit within the [[Verdant Blades]]. Each member has honed their skills in a specific rogue archetype, making them specialists in their respective fields. Their operations are often shrouded in secrecy, with only a few within the guild knowing the full extent of their missions. 

### Notable Members

- **Shade the Silent** - A master assassin who excels in eliminating targets without leaving a trace. Shade’s skills in stealth and poison make her one of the deadliest members of the Nightblades.
- **Whisper** - A Shadowblade who uses shadow magic to manipulate the battlefield and deceive enemies. Whisper is known for her ability to cast spells that enhance her stealth and confound her opponents. 
- **Rictus** - A Jester who is as deadly as he is entertaining. Rictus’s unique class blends roguish agility with illusion magic and psychological manipulation. His jests are laced with deadly intent, and he often confuses and disorients enemies before delivering the final blow.

## Mission Profile

The Nightblades are typically assigned to missions that require stealth, precision, and the element of surprise. They are experts in infiltration, sabotage, and assassination, often operating behind enemy lines or in environments where open combat would be too risky. Their skills make them ideal for:

- **High-Value Target Elimination:** Removing key figures or threats that pose a danger to the Verdant Blades or Veron.
- **Infiltration and Espionage:** Gathering intelligence from hostile or secure locations without being detected.
- **Sabotage Operations:** Disabling enemy assets, machinery, or plans through covert actions.

## Reputation

The Nightblades are both feared and respected throughout [[Veron]] and beyond. Their reputation for efficiency and secrecy means that they are often the first choice for missions where failure is not an option. Within the [[Verdant Blades]], they are regarded with a mix of admiration and wariness, as even their fellow guild members understand that the Nightblades operate by their own rules.

## Affiliations

- **Verdant Blades:** As an elite team within the [[Verdant Blades]], the Nightblades play a crucial role in the guild’s operations. They are trusted with the most sensitive and dangerous missions, often working directly with guild leadership to ensure the success of their objectives.
- **The Silver Sigil:** The Nightblades occasionally collaborate with [[Korrin Blackthistle]] to acquire rare magical items or information needed for their missions, often trading favours or artefacts in exchange for Korrin’s assistance.

## Notable Achievements

- **The Assassination of Lord Morvin:** The Nightblades were tasked with eliminating a corrupt noble who had been secretly undermining Veron’s stability. The mission was completed without a trace, and Lord Morvin’s downfall was attributed to “mysterious circumstances.”
- **The Theft of the Shadowstone:** In a daring heist, the Nightblades infiltrated a heavily guarded fortress to retrieve the Shadowstone, a powerful artefact that had fallen into the wrong hands. The operation was so flawlessly executed that the theft was not discovered until days later.
- **The Sabotage of the Ironclaw Foundry:** The Nightblades successfully infiltrated and sabotaged the Ironclaw Foundry, a key asset of an enemy faction, crippling their ability to produce weapons and armour.
