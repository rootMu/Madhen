## Overview

**The Seekers of Knowledge** is a distinguished guild of scholars, mages, and historians based in [[Veron]], dedicated to the pursuit of rare artefacts and ancient knowledge, particularly from within the [[Sunken Vault]]. The guild is represented by **[[Theron Valmaris|Archivist Theron]]**, a seasoned scholar known for his vast knowledge and meticulous approach to research. The Seekers of Knowledge are respected throughout Veron for their dedication to uncovering and preserving the secrets of the past, often venturing into dangerous and unexplored regions in their quest for enlightenment.

## Mission and Activities

The primary mission of The Seekers of Knowledge is to uncover, study, and preserve rare artefacts, ancient texts, and forgotten lore. The guild believes that understanding the past is key to mastering the present and shaping the future. To achieve their goals, they often hire adventurers to retrieve specific items or to escort their own members on perilous research missions into places like the [[Sunken Vault]], where many of their most valuable discoveries have been made.

The guild’s activities include:

- **Artefact Retrieval:** The Seekers frequently commission adventurers to delve into dangerous dungeons and ruins to retrieve artefacts of historical or magical significance. These missions are often well-compensated, given the risks involved.
- **Research Expeditions:** The guild organises and leads expeditions into ancient ruins, forgotten libraries, and other places of interest. These expeditions are usually led by experienced members of the guild and are often accompanied by hired adventurers for protection.
- **Knowledge Preservation:** Once artefacts and texts are retrieved, they are studied and preserved within the guild’s archives. The Seekers of Knowledge take great care in documenting and protecting these discoveries, ensuring that the knowledge is not lost to time.

## Headquarters

The Seekers of Knowledge are headquartered in a grand and ancient library located within Veron. The library serves as both a research facility and a secure storage area for the artefacts and texts the guild has collected over the years. The building itself is a marvel of architecture, with towering bookshelves, intricate stained glass windows, and hidden vaults where the most valuable and dangerous items are kept. The atmosphere within the headquarters is one of quiet study and reverence for the knowledge contained within its walls.

## Key Figure

### Archivist Theron

- **Role**: Leader and Representative of The Seekers of Knowledge
- **Description**: **[[Archivist Theron]]** is an elder scholar with decades of experience in the study of ancient texts and artefacts. He is known for his sharp mind and unwavering dedication to the pursuit of knowledge. Theron has a deep understanding of the magical and historical significance of the items the guild seeks, and he is often the one who decides which artefacts are worth pursuing. His careful and deliberate manner makes him a respected leader within the guild, and his guidance is invaluable on research expeditions.
- **Responsibilities**: Theron oversees all guild operations, including the planning of expeditions, the hiring of adventurers, and the preservation of artefacts. He also serves as the guild’s representative in Veron, liaising with other organisations and individuals who share the guild’s interests.

## Relationships

- **Adventurers' Guild**: The Seekers of Knowledge maintain a strong relationship with the [[Adventurers' Guild]], often hiring its members for dangerous retrieval missions. The two organisations share a mutual respect, recognising the value of each other’s expertise in their respective fields.
- **The Enchanter’s Conclave**: The Seekers frequently consult with [[The Enchanter’s Conclave]] to identify and understand the magical properties of the artefacts they recover. This collaboration ensures that dangerous items are properly handled and that their magical significance is fully understood.
- **Brightshield Mercenaries**: On particularly dangerous missions, the Seekers sometimes hire the [[Brightshield Mercenaries]] to provide additional protection. The mercenaries' disciplined and professional approach makes them ideal partners for the guild’s more perilous undertakings.

## Notable Achievements

- **Discovery of the Lost Tomes of Threnaliath**: The Seekers of Knowledge led an expedition into the [[Sunken Vault]] that resulted in the discovery of the Lost Tomes of Threnaliath, a collection of ancient texts that have since become one of the guild’s most prized possessions.
- **The Preservation of the Azurite Amulet**: An artefact of immense power and historical significance, the Azurite Amulet was recovered from a forgotten temple by the Seekers. Its preservation and study have provided valuable insights into the magical practices of a long-lost civilisation.
- **The Restoration of the Crystal of Eldara**: The guild successfully restored the Crystal of Eldara, a powerful magical artefact that was believed to be lost forever. The restoration process was a monumental task that required the combined efforts of the guild’s best scholars and mages.

## Membership and Recruitment

Membership in The Seekers of Knowledge is highly selective. Prospective members must demonstrate a deep commitment to the pursuit of knowledge and a willingness to face the dangers that come with it. Scholars, mages, and historians who wish to join the guild must prove their expertise in their field, often by presenting a significant discovery or by contributing to the guild’s research in a meaningful way. Once accepted, members gain access to the guild’s vast resources and are expected to contribute to ongoing research and expeditions.

## Personality Traits of Members

- **Curious**: Members of the Seekers of Knowledge possess an insatiable curiosity, always eager to uncover new information and explore uncharted territories.
- **Dedicated**: The guild’s members are deeply committed to their work, often spending years on a single research project or expedition.
- **Meticulous**: The Seekers are known for their attention to detail and their methodical approach to research, ensuring that every artefact and piece of knowledge is carefully documented and preserved.
