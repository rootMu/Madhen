Veron, often referred to as the Necropolis, is a marvel of mystical and ancient architecture. It is nestled in a valley surrounded by sloping cliffs that descend into the valley. The city is characterised by its towering spires and arching gateways made from obsidian and dark granites, which absorb light during the day and emit a soft, ethereal glow at night.


#### Major Districts

**[[Enclave of Eternity]]**
Central district with King Vorenus’ majestic palace.

- **[[Palace of Vorenus]]**: The majestic palace of King [[Vorenus]], featuring grand halls adorned with magical tapestries that depict the city's history.
- **[[Arcane Senate]]**: A council of powerful mages who advise the king on magical and political matters.
- **[[Eternal Library]]**: A vast repository of ancient texts and magical tomes. The library is open to scholars and mages seeking knowledge.
- **[[Plaza of Ancestors]]**: A central plaza with statues of past heroes. It is a place of reflection and remembrance.

**[[Obsidian Quarter]]**
Known for its towering spires and arching gateways.

- **[[Necropolis]]**: The grand cemetery where the city's honoured dead are interred. It is a place of reverence and mystery, often visited by necromancers and historians.
- **[[Undying Market]]**: A bustling market where magical artefacts, potions, and enchanted items are traded. The market is known for its rare and exotic goods.
- **[[Obsidian Forge]]**: A renowned smithy where weapons and armour are crafted from rare materials. The forge is operated by master blacksmiths who are skilled in both traditional and magical crafting.
- **[[Hall of Reflection]]**: A serene hall where citizens come to meditate and connect with their ancestors. It is a place of spiritual significance.

**[[Artisans' Haven]]**
District dedicated to artistic pursuits and crafts.

- **[[Artisan's Guild]]**: A collective of artists, craftsmen, and sculptors. The guild hall is filled with beautiful creations and offers workshops for aspiring artists.
- **[[Culinary Guild]]**: Known for its exquisite dishes and culinary experiments. The guild hosts regular feasts and cooking competitions.
- **[[Enchanters' Guild]]**: A place where magical items are crafted and enchanted. The guild also offers services to imbue ordinary items with magical properties.
- **[[Grand Theatre]]**: A majestic theatre that hosts plays, musical performances, and other cultural events. It is a centre of entertainment and artistic expression.

**[[Living Waters District]]**
Thrives with lush greenery and vibrant ecosystems.

- **[[Botanical Gardens]]**: Lush gardens filled with rare and magical plants. The gardens are a popular spot for relaxation and study of botany.
- **[[Waterways]]**: Canals that run through the district, providing a serene mode of transportation. Gondolas are available for hire.
- **[[Fountain of Vitality]]**: A mystical fountain said to have healing properties. It is a popular destination for those seeking rejuvenation.
- **[[Greenhouse of Wonders]]**: A large greenhouse that houses exotic plants from all over the world. It is also a research centre for magical flora.

**[[Solace Quarter]]**
Serene eastern district with proximity to enchanted forests.

- **[[Solace Grove]]**: A tranquil grove known for its ancient trees and peaceful atmosphere. It is a place of retreat and meditation.
- **[[Moonlit Residences]]**: Luxurious homes illuminated by magical moonlight. The residences are known for their beauty and serenity.
- **[[Woodland Retreat]]**: A secluded area on the edge of the enchanted forests. It offers rustic cabins and a quiet escape from city life.
- **[[Sylvan School]]**: A school dedicated to the study of nature magic and druidic practices. It attracts students from all over the realm.

**[[Luminescent Quarter]]**
Vibrant western district across the river.

- **[[Luminescent Plaza]]**: A vibrant plaza illuminated by bioluminescent plants and magical lights. It is a lively area with street performances and markets.
- **[[Scholar's Rest]]**: An inn that caters to visiting scholars and mages. It offers comfortable lodgings and a well-stocked library.
- **[[Artisan's Alley]]**: A street lined with workshops and boutiques of various craftsmen. It is the perfect place to find unique handcrafted items.
- **[[Lumina Theatre]]**: A smaller, more intimate theatre known for experimental and avant-garde performances.

**[[Aurelian Quarter]]**
Northern district intersecting with the Solace Quarter.

- **[[Aurelian Academy]]**: A prestigious academy for the study of advanced magic. It offers courses in various magical disciplines and is known for its rigorous curriculum.
- **[[Alchemist's Row]]**: A street dedicated to alchemy shops and laboratories. It is the place to find rare ingredients and potions.
- **[[Astral Observatory]]**: An observatory that offers a stunning view of the night sky. It is also a centre for astronomical research.
- **[[Enchanted Bazaar]]**: A market known for its wide array of magical goods and curiosities. It attracts traders and buyers from distant lands.

#### Enchanted Rivers and Bridges
A circular enchanted river surrounds the [[Enclave of Eternity]], with the following bridges connecting various districts:
- **Shadowgate Bridge**: Connects the [[Obsidian Quarter]] to the [[Enclave of Eternity]].
- **Craftsman's Crossing**: Connects the [[Artisans' Haven]] to the [[Enclave of Eternity]].
- **Verdant Bridge**: Connects the [[Living Waters District]] to the [[Enclave of Eternity]].
- **Sunrise Bridge**: Connects the [[Aurelian Quarter]] to the [[Enclave of Eternity]].

#### Surrounding Areas
- **[[Verdant Outskirts]]**: Lush farmland and vibrant ecosystems sustained by the enchanted rivers. It provides food and resources for the city.
- **[[Food Jungle]]**: An agricultural area producing a variety of crops and herbs. It is known for its fertile soil and abundant harvests.
- **[[Whispering Meadows]]**: A residential suburb known for its serene atmosphere and proximity to enchanted forests. It is a popular place for those seeking a quiet life.

#### Governmental and Natural Features
- **Governmental Buildings**: Includes the [[Arcane Senate]], [[Trade Hall]], [[Immigration Office]], [[Strategic Command]], [[Maintenance Guild]], [[Council Chambers]], [[Judicial Court]], [[City Treasury]], [[Public Works Office]], [[Enclave Guard Headquarters]], [[Diplomatic Hall]], [[Registry Office]], and [[Archives of Veron]]. #### Cultural and Social Aspects
- **Undead Workforce**: Perform menial jobs and labour work, ensuring the city's smooth operation.
- **Living Citizens**: Engage in artistic pursuits, intellectual activities, and magical research.
- **Cultural Festivities**:
  - **Festival of Eternal Waters**: Celebrates the city's rebirth and the cycle of life and death.
  - **Dance of Renewal**: A dance performed by both living and undead, symbolising unity and eternal bonds.
