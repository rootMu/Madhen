## Overview

The **Sunken Vault** is the last accessible portion of the once-great Lost Dungeon of [[Threnaliath]], a vast and ancient underground complex that lies beneath the city of [[Veron]]. This dungeon was conquered by [[Vorenus|King Vorenus]] and subsequently raised to form the foundation of his grand palace. The city of Veron was constructed around this palace, sealing off most of the original dungeon with powerful magic to prevent its dangers from spilling out into the city above. The Sunken Vault, however, remains open, serving as a proving ground and treasure trove for adventurers from across the realm.

## Connection to Threnaliath

The Lost Dungeon of [[Threnaliath]] was a sprawling labyrinth of traps, monsters, and ancient secrets, once believed to be impenetrable. It was within this dungeon that Vorenus made his name, defeating its guardian and claiming its treasures. After raising the dungeon to form the base of his palace, most of Threnaliath was magically sealed off, deemed too dangerous to remain open to the public. The Sunken Vault is the only part of Threnaliath that remains accessible, though it is heavily monitored and regulated by the Veron Guard.

## Regenerative Nature

The Sunken Vault is classified as a [[Regenerative Dungeon]], known for its unique ability to renew itself over time. Unlike other parts of Threnaliath, which have been permanently sealed, the Vault continues to spawn new traps, monsters, and treasures. Adventurers find that the layout shifts, with dangers resetting after a short period. This regenerative quality makes the Sunken Vault a popular destination for those seeking both challenge and fortune.

## Difficulty and Exploration

The Vault serves as a training and testing ground for adventurers of all levels. The dungeon grows progressively more dangerous as one descends deeper into its depths. While the upper levels are relatively well-mapped and explored, the lower levels remain largely uncharted, filled with perilous traps and ancient guardians that have yet to be defeated. Even the most seasoned adventurers must proceed with caution, as the Vault’s ever-changing nature ensures that no two expeditions are ever the same.

## Security Measures

Given its history and the dangers it holds, the Sunken Vault is the only part of Threnaliath that remains accessible without direct permission from [[Vorenus|King Vorenus]]. However, its entrance is heavily guarded by the [[Veron Guard]], a contingent of skeletal warriors bound by magic to protect the city from the Vault’s dangers. These guardians ensure that nothing dangerous escapes and that only those adequately prepared are permitted entry.

## The Sunken Vault Today

Today, the Sunken Vault stands as a reminder of the power and mystery of the Lost Dungeon of [[Threnaliath]]. Adventurers, scholars, and treasure hunters alike are drawn to the Vault, eager to uncover its secrets and claim the powerful artefacts that lie within. Despite the risks, the Vault remains a vital part of Veron’s adventuring culture, offering both great riches and great perils to those who dare to enter its depths.

## The Bazaar of the Bold 
Before entering the Sunken Vault, adventurers pass through the [[Bazaar of the Bold]], a bustling marketplace where they can prepare for their journey into the depths. The Bazaar is filled with vendors selling essential supplies, rare magical items, and detailed maps of the upper levels of the Vault. It also serves as a gathering place where solo adventurers can join groups, hire mercenaries, or consult with experienced veterans before venturing further into the dungeon.
