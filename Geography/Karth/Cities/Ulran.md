#### **Overview**
Referred to as the Northern Gem, Ulran is the northernmost city of [[Ardenshire]], perched on the coast at the mouth of the [[Silverfin Estuary]]. It serves as a bustling hub for the local fishing and trading industries. As a crucial stopover for the seafaring [[Krial]] people, Ulran thrives on its vibrant maritime economy, with docks perennially busy with fishermen and traders.

#### **Geography**
Ulran is strategically located at the base of the expansive [[Silverfin Estuary]], which opens into the [[Azurean Deep]]. The city straddles the border between [[Crownshield]] and the [[Windswept Moors]], with a sheer cliff of 100-200 meters marking the edge of the two regions. A carved footpath along the west coast of Ulran allows farmers from the moors to travel up to the city, creating a vital connection between these regions.

#### **Economy**
Ulran's economy is primarily based on fishing and maritime trade. The city's docks are constantly bustling with activity as fishermen bring in their daily catches, and traders prepare goods for export. The fear of [[Krial]] pirates keeps most ships close to land, but Ulran remains a pivotal point for coastal trade. The city's architecture is crafted to withstand coastal storms, featuring robust timber buildings and protective sea walls.

#### **Notable Locations**

- **The Silverfin Docks**: The heart of Ulran's fishing industry, where boats come and go with the tides, and the air is filled with the scent of the sea.
- **Stormshield Market**: A vibrant market located near the docks, offering fresh seafood, maritime supplies, and various local goods. It is a bustling hub of commerce and social interaction.
- **The Sea Wall**: An impressive defensive structure that protects the city from the harsh coastal storms. It also serves as a popular promenade for residents and visitors alike.
- **The Cliffside Path**: A carved footpath that allows access between Ulran and the [[Windswept Moors]]. This path is essential for the transportation of goods and travel between the two regions.

#### **Guilds and Organisations**

- **The Fishermen's Guild**: This guild oversees the fishing industry in Ulran, ensuring sustainable practices and fair trade for all its members.
- **The Mariners' Association**: A group dedicated to the safety and well-being of those who work at sea, providing support and resources for sailors and their families.
- **The Traders' Consortium**: An organisation that regulates trade within the city, promoting economic growth and maintaining trade routes.

#### **Festivals and Traditions**

- **[[The Silverfin Festival]]**: An annual celebration that highlights Ulran’s connection to the sea. The festival features boat races, fishing competitions, seafood feasts, and cultural performances.
- **[[Tide's Blessing]]**: A traditional ceremony held at the beginning of each fishing season, where offerings are made to ensure bountiful catches and safe voyages.
- **[[The Harvest Moon Fair]]**: A joint celebration with the farmers from the Windswept Moors, featuring agricultural produce, livestock shows, and craft markets.

#### **Culture and Lifestyle**

Ulran's residents have a deep connection to the sea, which is reflected in their daily lives and cultural practices. The city's festivals and traditions celebrate this maritime heritage, and the close-knit community values hard work, resilience, and cooperation. Ulran's architecture, with its sturdy timber buildings and sea walls, showcases the city's ability to withstand the challenges posed by its coastal location.

#### **Notable Figures**

- **Captain Alaric Tidewalker**: A legendary fisherman known for his unmatched skill at sea and his leadership within the Fishermen's Guild. His daring exploits are the stuff of local legend.
- **Marina Seabreeze**: A renowned trader whose keen business acumen has helped establish Ulran as a vital trading hub. Her work within the Traders' Consortium has fostered economic growth and stability.
- **Elda Stormshield**: An engineer and architect responsible for designing the city's sea walls and other defensive structures. Her innovative designs have protected Ulran from countless storms.
- **[[Liam Blackthorn || Liam "The Fool" Blackthorn]]**: A living legend who now tends goats in the [[Windswept Moors]], unbeknownst to most. Liam was the best friend and closest confidant of [[King Anthranar Serbius III]]. Known for his pranks and loyalty, he was instrumental in convincing the Krial merchants to ferry Anthranar and his champions across the [[Arkadian Expanse]] to [[Ruban]] in 1211 ES. Liam's cunning saved Anthranar and two champions during the siege of [[Arnette]] where he presumedly died.

#### **Historical Figures**

- **Thorin Saltbreaker**: The founder of the Fishermen’s Guild, who established the practices and standards that still guide Ulran's fishing industry today.
- **Galen Tideborn**: A famous navigator and mapmaker whose charts of the Silverfin Estuary and surrounding waters are still used by sailors and traders.
- **Elara Cliffwalker**: The architect who carved the Cliffside Path, creating a vital connection between Ulran and the Windswept Moors.

#### **Conclusion**

Ulran stands as a testament to the strength and resilience of its people. Its strategic location at the mouth of the [[Silverfin Estuary]] and its thriving fishing and trading industries make it a vital part of [[Ardenshire]]. From its bustling docks to its vibrant festivals, Ulran celebrates its maritime heritage while looking forward to a prosperous future.
