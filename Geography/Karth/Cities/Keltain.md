#### **Overview**
Keltain serves as the primary gateway to [[Ardenshire]] for those not arriving via [[Bridor]] on the east coast. Renowned for its thriving textile industry, this city is the heart of weaving and fabric production in the region. Artisans in Keltain create everything from durable woollens to fine silks, making it a vital commercial hub known for its high-quality products and bustling markets.

#### **Geography**
Strategically positioned, Keltain is nestled between the fertile plains of [[Goldenfields]] and the southern borders of [[Silvervale]] and [[Verdanthold]]. Its location provides easy access to raw materials, which are essential for its extensive textile industry. The city benefits from the rich agricultural output of [[Goldenfields]] and the proximity to the resources of [[Verdanthold]].

#### **Economy**
Keltain's economy is dominated by its textile industry. The city is home to numerous weaving guilds, dye houses, and tailoring shops. The high demand for Keltain's fabrics has made it a wealthy city, with trade routes extending across the continent. Markets are constantly buzzing with traders and visitors, eager to purchase the city's renowned textiles.

#### **Notable Locations**

- **[[The Weaver's Guildhall]]**: The heart of Keltain's textile industry, this grand building is where master weavers gather to share techniques, trade secrets, and conduct business. It also serves as a training ground for aspiring weavers.
- **[[Silkspun Market]]**: This vibrant market is the centre of trade in Keltain, offering a dazzling array of fabrics, clothing, and accessories. It's a bustling hub where merchants from across Karth come to trade.
- **[[The Dye Works]]**: Known for producing the finest dyes in Ardenshire, this complex of workshops and mills is where raw materials are transformed into vibrant colours used in Keltain’s fabrics.
- **[[Spindle Park]]**: A lush, green space in the centre of the city where artisans and citizens alike come to relax and showcase their latest creations. It’s also a popular spot for outdoor festivals.
- **[[Tailor’s Row]]**: A street lined with the finest tailoring shops in the city, where custom garments are made to order. Each shop is known for its unique style and craftsmanship.

#### **Guilds and Organisations**

- **The Weaver's Guild**: The most prestigious guild in Keltain, overseeing the quality and production of textiles. Membership is highly sought after, and only the best weavers are accepted.
- **The Dyers' Collective**: A powerful organisation that controls the dyeing processes and ensures the highest quality of colours. They also experiment with new dyeing techniques and materials.
- **The Tailors’ Association**: This guild represents the interests of tailors in Keltain, ensuring fair trade practices and promoting the city’s tailoring industry.
- **The Silk Guild**: Specialising in the production and trade of silk, this guild is known for its luxurious fabrics and exclusive trade agreements.

#### **Festivals and Traditions**

- **[[The Festival of Threads]]**: Held annually, this festival celebrates Keltain’s rich textile heritage. The city comes alive with parades, showcases of the latest fashions, weaving competitions, and markets filled with rare fabrics.
- **[[Dye Day]]**: A vibrant festival where the streets are filled with colourful displays and demonstrations of dyeing techniques. Citizens dress in their brightest clothes, and the city is adorned with banners and decorations.
- **[[Spindle Fair]]**: A biannual event where weavers and tailors from across [[Karth]] gather to trade techniques, tools, and fabrics. It includes workshops, demonstrations, and competitions.

#### **Clothing and Fashion**

Keltain is known for its innovative and high-quality clothing. The fashion here blends practicality with elegance, featuring durable yet beautiful fabrics. Traditional Keltain garments often include intricate embroidery and unique dye patterns. The city is a trendsetter in [[Ardenshire]], with its designs influencing fashion across the region.

#### **Notable Figures**

- **[[Mira Silkenweave]]**: A master weaver and the current head of the Weaver’s Guild. Known for her innovative techniques and stunning fabrics, she has elevated Keltain’s textile industry to new heights.
- **[[Garrick Threadeater]]**: A renowned dyer whose vibrant and long-lasting dyes are sought after throughout Karth. His workshop is a centre for experimentation and discovery in the dyeing arts.
- **[[Elara Softstitch]]**: A famous tailor whose designs are worn by nobility and adventurers alike. Her shop on Tailor’s Row is always busy with orders for custom garments.
- **[[Tamsin Brightloom]]**: A historian and author who has documented the rich history of Keltain’s textile industry. Her works are essential reading for anyone interested in the city’s cultural heritage.

#### **Historical Figures**

- **Aldwin Weaverborn**: The founder of the Weaver’s Guild, who established the standards and practices that still guide Keltain’s textile industry today.
- **Liora Dyehand**: A legendary dyer who discovered several of the vibrant natural dyes still used in Keltain’s fabrics. Her techniques revolutionised the industry.
- **Corwin Tailorson**: An influential tailor who introduced many of the styles and techniques that define Keltain fashion. His legacy lives on in the Tailor’s Association.

#### **Conclusion**

Keltain is a city that weaves together tradition, innovation, and community. Its textile industry is the lifeblood of the city, shaping its economy, culture, and identity. From its bustling markets to its vibrant festivals, Keltain stands as a testament to the craftsmanship and entrepreneurial spirit of Ardenshire.
