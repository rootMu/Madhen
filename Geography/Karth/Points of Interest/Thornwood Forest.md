#### **Overview**  
Thornwood Forest, located in the northern part of [[Harbinger's Coast]], is an ancient old-growth forest renowned for its rich biodiversity, mystical aura, and historical significance. The forest serves as a vital resource for the region, providing timber, medicinal herbs, and a haven for wildlife. It is also home to several unique settlements that thrive within its verdant embrace.

#### **Geography**  
Thornwood Forest covers a vast area in the northern reaches of Harbinger's Coast. The forest is characterised by towering trees, some of which are centuries old, dense underbrush, and numerous streams and rivers that originate within its depths. The [[Silverstream River]] flows from this forest, contributing to the fertility and prosperity of the surrounding lands.

#### **Economy**
The economy of Thornwood Forest is primarily based on sustainable forestry, herbalism, and hunting. The forest's abundant resources support various trades and crafts, making it an integral part of [[Harbinger's Coast||Harbinger's Coast's]] overall economic structure. Timber, medicinal herbs, and game are the main products harvested from the forest, each contributing to the livelihoods of its inhabitants.

#### **Settlements**
Thornwood Forest is home to several unique settlements, each with its distinct character and connection to the forest.

- **[[Thornhollow]]**: The largest settlement within Thornwood Forest, Thornhollow is renowned for its timber and herbal trades. The village is built around a massive, ancient oak tree believed to be imbued with magical properties. Thornhollow's residents are skilled woodworkers and herbalists, crafting exquisite wooden items and potent medicinal remedies.

- **[[Leafshade]]**: A smaller village deep within the forest, Leafshade is known for its tranquility and natural beauty. The village is a haven for artists and scholars who seek inspiration from the serene environment. Leafshade is also home to a renowned apothecary that creates highly sought-after potions and salves from forest herbs.

- **[[Elderwatch]]**: Positioned at the northern edge of the forest, Elderwatch serves as a protective outpost and a centre for forest rangers. The settlement is responsible for monitoring the forest and ensuring that its resources are used sustainably. Elderwatch is also a hub for hunters and trappers, providing a steady supply of game to the region.

- **[[Silvergrove]]**: Located near the source of the Silverstream River, Silvergrove is a picturesque village surrounded by lush greenery and sparkling streams. The villagers are known for their expertise in fishing and agriculture, making the most of the fertile soil and abundant water. Silvergrove is also famous for its annual Silverstream Festival, celebrating the river's bounty with music, dance, and feasting.

- **[[Greenhearth]]**: Situated in a clearing within Thornwood Forest, Greenhearth is a community focused on sustainable living and environmental harmony. The village is self-sufficient, with residents practising permaculture and eco-friendly crafts. Greenhearth's inhabitants are deeply spiritual, often engaging in rituals and ceremonies to honour the forest and its spirits.

#### **Natural Resources**
Thornwood Forest is rich in natural resources that sustain both its inhabitants and the wider region. The forest provides valuable timber, medicinal herbs, and game, all harvested with a focus on sustainability and environmental stewardship.

#### **Ecosystem**
Thornwood Forest boasts a diverse and thriving ecosystem, teeming with a variety of life forms.

**Flora:**
- **Ancient Trees**: The forest is home to towering ancient trees, including oaks, elms, and pines, some of which are believed to be centuries old.
- **Medicinal Herbs**: The forest floor is abundant with medicinal herbs and plants, utilised by local herbalists to create potent remedies and potions.
- **Wildflowers**: Throughout the forest, wildflowers bloom in vibrant colours, adding to the natural beauty of the landscape.

**Fauna:**
- **Mammals**: Thornwood Forest is home to various mammals such as deer, foxes, and boars. These animals play a crucial role in the forest's ecosystem and are often hunted for sustenance.
- **Birds**: The forest is alive with the songs of countless bird species, including owls, hawks, and songbirds. These birds contribute to the forest's vibrant biodiversity.
- **Insects**: A myriad of insects, including bees, butterflies, and beetles, can be found within the forest, pollinating plants and serving as a food source for other wildlife.

**Waterways:**
- **[[Silverstream River]]**: Originating from within the forest, this river is vital for the region's irrigation and supports a diverse array of aquatic life.
- **Streams and Brooks**: Numerous smaller streams and brooks crisscross the forest, providing water and sustaining the lush vegetation.

#### **Culture and Community**
The residents of Thornwood Forest are deeply connected to their natural surroundings. The community values sustainability, environmental stewardship, and harmonious living with nature. Seasonal festivals and rituals are an integral part of life in Thornwood, celebrating the forest's bounty and the changing seasons.

#### **Notable Geographical Features**

- **[[Thornwood Grove]]**: The heart of the forest, where the largest and oldest trees are found, including the ancient oak around which Thornhollow is built.
- **[[Silverstream River]]**: A major river flowing from Thornwood Forest, providing water for the shire’s farms and a scenic route for leisurely activities.
- **[[Sacred Glade]]**: A hidden clearing known for its spiritual significance and the site of many local rituals and ceremonies.

#### **Conclusion**
Thornwood Forest is a vital part of [[Harbinger's Coast]], offering rich resources and a serene, mystical environment. It is a place where the past and present intertwine, and where nature and community thrive together. The forest stands as a testament to the resilience and harmony of its inhabitants, embodying the spirit of sustainability and natural beauty.
