#### **Overview**
Neridia, once a thriving island city, now lies in ruins beneath the waves of [[Mirrormere]] due to seismic activity. This sunken refuge is home to various aquatic races, each occupying different districts of the former city. Despite being a hub of conflict and uneasy alliances, Neridia serves as a vital sanctuary for those who dwell in its submerged remains.

#### **Geography**
Neridia is located in the depths of the Mirrormere. The ruins of the ancient city are spread across the seabed, with crumbling structures and coral-covered remnants forming the current landscape. The once-grand architecture is now a blend of natural underwater beauty and the remnants of craftsmanship.

#### **Economy**
While the city itself is in ruins, the economy of Neridia thrives on the treasures and resources found within its depths. Adventurers and treasure hunters frequently explore the sunken ruins in search of valuable artefacts, rare underwater herbs, and enchanted pearls. Trade, though dangerous, occurs between the different factions and with surface dwellers brave enough to venture into the depths.

#### **Notable Locations**

- **Coral Throne**: The symbolic seat of governance where representatives from each faction meet to discuss matters of importance. This alliance is tenuous, with conflicts often breaking out despite efforts to maintain peace.
- **Kelp Market**: A makeshift market where traders from different races come to exchange goods. The market is a chaotic blend of bartering and negotiations, reflecting the fragile peace among the factions.
- **Pearl Gardens**: An area where the Ondine cultivate the finest pearls, guarded fiercely to protect their valuable resource.
- **Gills District**: The primary settlement for the Axalor, featuring homes made from natural rock formations and bioluminescent algae.
- **Triton Stronghold**: The fortified section of the ruins occupied by the Triton warriors, serving as both a home and a defensive position.
- **Sahuagin Pits**: A rugged and isolated area dominated by the Sahuagin, known for its harsh conditions and frequent internal conflicts.

#### **Festivals and Traditions**

- **Festival of the Tides**: An annual celebration held when the tides are at their highest. It is a rare occasion where the factions come together to celebrate their shared heritage, featuring parades and underwater dance performances.
- **Pearl Moon Festival**: A significant event for the Ondine, celebrated under the full moon’s reflection. It involves the harvesting of pearls and various cultural ceremonies.
- **Great Hunt**: A traditional Sahuagin event where warriors hunt large sea creatures to prove their strength and skill.

#### **Aquatic Races of Neridia**

1. **Merfolk**
   - The Merfolk of Neridia are one of the most prevalent races in the Mirrormere. With the upper body of a human and the lower body of a fish, they are known for their enchanting voices and deep connection to the magic of the ocean. In the ruins of Neridia, they often serve as diplomats and mediators, striving to maintain the fragile peace among the factions.

2. **Tritons**
   - Tritons are the noble protectors of the Mirrormere. They possess a humanoid form with fish-like features, including gills, fins, and scaled skin. In Neridia, Tritons occupy the fortified areas and are vigilant guardians against external threats, their sense of duty and honour making them respected and sometimes feared by other races.

3. **Kuo-Toa**
   - The Kuo-Toa are erratic and secretive fish-like humanoids who inhabit the deeper, shadowy parts of Neridia's ruins. Known for their strange rituals and the ability to create gods through sheer belief, they are often hostile to outsiders. Their bizarre culture and unpredictable behaviour make them one of the more enigmatic and dangerous factions within Neridia.

4. **Sahuagin**
   - The Sahuagin, or "sea devils," are aggressive and territorial. With their humanoid form and fish-like features, including sharp teeth and fins, they control the harsher, more rugged areas of Neridia. Their predatory nature and frequent raids on other factions keep the balance of power in a constant state of tension.

5. **[[Ondine]]**
   - Ondine are bioluminescent humanoids with delicate fins. They inhabit the more serene and less contested regions of Neridia, using their ability to blend into their surroundings for both defence and trade. Ondine are often seen as the keepers of knowledge and tradition, their glowing markings a common sight in the underwater city's calmer districts.

6. **[[Ambystan]]**
   - The Ambystan, an axolotl-like beastkin race, live in the nutrient-rich and lush areas of Neridia. Their distinctive external gills and regenerative abilities make them resilient inhabitants of the Mirrormere. Known for their cheerful and curious nature, the Axalor are often found exploring the ruins and cultivating underwater gardens, contributing to the fragile ecosystem of the sunken city.

#### **Challenges and Opportunities**
Living in Neridia presents both challenges and opportunities. The diverse population often leads to conflicts, but the city’s ruins hold invaluable resources and artefacts. The constant threat of factional warfare makes Neridia a perilous yet intriguing destination for adventurers and traders alike.

#### **Conclusion**
Neridia, though in ruins, remains a city of intrigue and opportunity beneath the waves of the Mirrormere. It stands as a testament to the resilience of its inhabitants and the complex web of alliances and conflicts that define life in this underwater refuge.
