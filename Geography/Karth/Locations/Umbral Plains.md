#### Landscape
The Umbral Plains, located east of the [[Arphscar]] within [[The Hinterlands]] are characterised by a vast, arid desert that stretches across the region, presenting a harsh and unforgiving environment for travellers. The landscape is dominated by rolling sand dunes, rocky outcrops, and scattered oases that provide rare but vital water sources. The intense heat and shifting sands create a challenging terrain that demands resilience and preparation from those who venture through it.

In the middle, near [[Veron]], the desert gives way to more hospitable areas where oases become more frequent, offering lush pockets of vegetation and water. These oases are crucial for survival and serve as resting points for travellers and merchants.

### Major Locations

#### **[[Trent]]**
Trent is the northern gateway to the Umbral Plains, known for its bustling markets and strong trade connections. It serves as the starting point for many journeys into the desert.

#### **[[Veron]]**
Veron lies in the south-central part of the Umbral Plains, near the edge of the desert. It is renowned for its strategic location near several oases, making it a vital hub for travellers and traders.

#### **[[Oasisvale]]**
Oasisvale is located on the banks of the Oasis of Serah, this thriving oasis town is known as the Jewel of the Umbral Plains.

### Notable Places

#### **[[Obsidian Canyon]]**
Obsidian Canyon is a striking geological formation within the Umbral Plains, known for its sharp, black volcanic glass that glistens under the sun. It provides both a natural wonder and a hazardous terrain for adventurers.

#### **[[The Shimmering Dunes]]**
The Shimmering Dunes are an area where the sand has a unique reflective quality, creating mirages and optical illusions that can disorient even the most experienced travellers.

#### **[[Oasis of Serah]]**
One of the largest and most famous oases near [[Veron]], the Oasis of Serah offers a lush respite from the harsh desert. It is a vital stop for replenishing supplies and resting. 

#### **[[The Whispering Sands]]**
An eerie section of the desert where the wind creates haunting, whispering sounds as it moves the sand. This area is often avoided by superstitious travellers.

#### **[[The Glass Flats]]**
A region where lightning storms have turned the sand into smooth, glassy surfaces. While beautiful, the Glass Flats are dangerous to traverse due to sharp edges and intense heat.

#### **[[The Dune Sea]]**
An expansive area of towering dunes that seem endless. The Dune Sea is known for its ever-shifting sands that can easily mislead and trap the unwary.

### Travel and Survival Tips

1. **Carry Sufficient Water**: The desert heat can quickly lead to dehydration. Ensure you have enough water for the journey.
2. **Travel by Night**: To avoid the extreme daytime temperatures, consider traveling during the cooler night hours.
3. **Use Landmarks**: Natural features like the [[Obsidian Canyon]] and the [[Shimmering Dunes]] can help with navigation.
4. **Rest at Oases**: Plan your route to include stops at oases like the [[Oasis of Serah]] for rest and replenishment.
