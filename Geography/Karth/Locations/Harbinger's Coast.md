#### **Overview**  
Harbinger's Coast lies at the southern edge of [[Ardenshire]], encompassing the expansive inland sea known as [[Mirrormere]]. This shire is noted for its serene waterfronts, abundant fishing spots, and the vast river system that flows south-west from Mirrormere through the central spine mountains into [[The Marshes]]. Its geographical positioning makes it a vital gateway for trade and transport between the inland regions and the southern territories.

#### **Geography** 
Harbinger's Coast is characterised by its tranquil lakesides, rolling plains, and fertile deltas. The shire is bordered to the north by [[Thornwood Forest]], an ancient old-growth forest which provides a rich ecological backdrop and valuable resources. From the [[Central Spine Mountains]], the [[Silverstream River]] and its numerous tributaries flow southward, eventually merging into a wide river system, before spreading out into the vast wetlands of [[The Marshes]].

#### **Economy**
The economy of Harbinger's Coast is diverse, with fishing, agriculture, and trade forming its backbone. The inland sea, [[Mirrormere]], is rich in freshwater fish, supporting a thriving fishing industry. Additionally, the fertile lands surrounding the river deltas are ideal for growing crops such as rice, vegetables, and fruit, contributing significantly to Ardenshire’s food supply. The strategic location along key trade routes also facilitates commerce, connecting Ardenshire with The Marshes and beyond.

#### **Settlements**
Harbinger's Coast is home to several important towns and villages that support its economic activities and serve as cultural hubs. Notable settlements include:

- **[[Lakeshire]]**: A bustling town on the shores of Mirrormere, known for its vibrant fish markets and boat-racing.
- **[[Riversmeet]]**: Located at the confluence of the [[Azureflow River]] and [[Greenbrook River]], this town is a key agricultural centre, renowned for its rice paddies and fruit orchards.
- **[[Delta's End]]**: Positioned near the marshy southern border, this village is crucial for trade with The Marshes, facilitating the exchange of goods and cultural interactions.

#### **Natural Resources**
Harbinger's Coast benefits from a wealth of natural resources. The inland sea, [[Mirrormere]], provides abundant fish and supports the local economy. The fertile soil of the river deltas is ideal for agriculture, yielding bountiful crops that sustain the shire and the wider region. Additionally, the old-growth forest to the north supplies valuable timber and medicinal herbs, adding to the shire’s natural wealth.

#### **Culture and Community**  
The communities of Harbinger's Coast are closely knit, with a culture deeply rooted in their natural surroundings. Seasonal festivals celebrate the bounty of the inland sea and the harvests from the fertile deltas. Fishermen's fairs, boat races, and agricultural festivals are common, reflecting the residents’ connection to the water and the land.

#### **Notable Geographical Features**

- **[[Mirrormere]]**: The expansive inland sea that forms the heart of Harbinger's Coast, known for its calm waters and abundant fish.
- **[[Central Spine Mountains]]**: The mountain range that runs through Harbinger's Coast, providing the source for the rivers that feed the fertile deltas and wetlands.
- **[[Silverstream River]]**: A major river flowing from Mirrormere through the central spine mountains, splitting into numerous tributaries before reaching The Marshes.
- **[[Azureflow River]]**: This river originates from the central spine mountains, flowing southward through the fertile lands of Harbinger's Coast.
- **[[Greenbrook River]]**: A major tributary of the Silverstream River, it flows from the northern old-growth forest, converging with Azureflow River at Riversmeet.
- **[[Delta Marshes]]**: A vast wetland area that marks the south-western boundary of the shire, serving as a vital ecological zone and trade route.
- **[[Thornwood Forest]]**: A dense forest north of Mirrormere, rich in biodiversity and resources, contributing to the shire’s natural wealth.

#### **Conclusion**  
Harbinger's Coast stands as a vital gateway of [[Ardenshire]], bridging the inland regions with the southern territories through its rivers and the inland sea. It is a shire where the rhythms of water and land define the way of life, fostering a community that thrives on fishing, agriculture, and trade. The landscapes of Harbinger's Coast tell a story of natural bounty, resilience, and the harmonious coexistence of its people with their environment.
