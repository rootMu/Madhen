### Description
The Celestial Daisy appears at first glance to be a simple, delicate flower. Its petals are a pure, luminous white with a subtle iridescent shimmer, catching the light in a way that gives them a faint, otherworldly glow. The center of the daisy is a deep, midnight blue, speckled with tiny, silver star-like patterns that seem to shift and twinkle as though alive. The stem is slender and green, but it is infused with a subtle golden hue that suggests a divine origin.

### Appearance
- Petals: Soft white with a radiant glow, shifting colours slightly depending on the angle and light, giving the impression of a miniature aurora.
- Center: Midnight blue, dotted with star-like specks that twinkle as if reflecting the night sky.
- Stem: Green with a golden sheen, smooth to the touch, and seemingly unbreakable.
 
### Mystical Properties
The true power of the Celestial Daisy is unknown, even to the most learned sages and scholars. It is said that this flower is not native to the material plane but was gifted by an unknown celestial being. The daisy’s powers are vast and varied, but they reveal themselves only under specific, mysterious circumstances. The following are the known effects, though much remains to be discovered:

#### Timeless Beauty:
The flower never wilts or fades. It remains in a state of perfect bloom indefinitely, regardless of environmental conditions.

#### Aura of Calm:
Holding or being near the Celestial Daisy induces a profound sense of peace and calm in all living beings. This effect can soothe anger, reduce anxiety, and even bring comfort to those in great distress.

#### Unknown Activation:
The daisy’s true powers are said to be activated under certain celestial events—such as a solar eclipse, a rare planetary alignment, or a night when the stars form a specific constellation. When these conditions are met, the daisy is rumoured to exhibit miraculous powers, the nature of which vary depending on the alignment of celestial forces.

#### Otherworldly Protection:
The flower is believed to grant its bearer protection from certain dangers. While the exact nature of this protection is unclear, those who carry the Celestial Daisy have been reported to narrowly avoid disasters or survive situations that should have been fatal.

#### Mysterious Healing:
Some accounts suggest that in times of great need, the flower can heal wounds, cure diseases, or even restore life to the recently deceased. However, these powers are unreliable and seem to be connected to the intentions and purity of the person in need.

### Celestial Connection
There are whispers that the daisy can act as a conduit to the celestial realms. During certain nights, meditating with the flower may allow one to communicate with celestial beings or receive visions of divine knowledge.

### Unpredictable Effects
The daisy’s powers are unpredictable and can vary drastically depending on the situation, the person holding it, and the celestial conditions at the time. It might grant immense power, or it could simply be a beautiful flower for someone who is unworthy or uninterested in its potential.

### Mystery and Lore
The Celestial Daisy has been the subject of numerous legends and myths. Some believe it to be the remnant of a divine garden, while others think it is a seed from a star that fell to the earth. Those who possess the flower often guard it closely, aware that its true potential is as vast as the cosmos but as elusive as a whisper on the wind.In all tales, one thing is certain: the Celestial Daisy is a rare and wondrous gift, its true capabilities known only to the stars that guide its mysterious power. Adventurers and scholars alike seek the flower not just for its beauty, but for the hope that it might one day reveal its secrets.
