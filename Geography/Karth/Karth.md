Karth is Eastern most continent of [[Madhen]] made up of three provinces, [[Ardenshire]], [[The Hinterlands]] and [[The Marshes]]

[[Ardenbridge]] is the Capital of Karth and holds dominion over [[Ardenshire]], [[The Hinterlands]] and [[The Marshes]] though both areas govern themselves they predominantly respect its authority.
