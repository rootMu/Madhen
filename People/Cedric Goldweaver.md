Lord Cedric Goldweaver, born in 789 ES, was a towering figure in the Kingdom of [[Karth]] and one of the [[Three Bridges#Founding Merchant Lords|founding members]] of [[Three Bridges]]. As the heir to the [[Goldweaver Trading Company]], Cedric was groomed from a young age to manage and expand the family's extensive trading empire. With piercing blue eyes, a well-groomed beard, and a regal demeanour, Cedric was known for his sharp wit and keen business acumen. 

#### Early Life and Education
Cedric spent his childhood in the lavish [[Goldweaver Manor]], learning the intricacies of trade and commerce from his father and grandfather. His education was comprehensive, encompassing not only the art of negotiation and trade but also combat and statecraft. Cedric quickly showed a natural talent for leadership and business, securing profitable deals and expanding the company's influence. 

#### Founding of Three Bridges
In 803 ES, Cedric played a pivotal role in founding the merchant town of [[Three Bridges]], a strategic hub for trade in [[Ardenshire]]. Over five years, Cedric oversaw the development of the town, reforming the roads leading north to the capital of [[Ardenbridge]], and ensuring the infrastructure supported the booming trade. 

### Marriage and Family
Cedric married three times throughout his life. 

- **[[Alice Fairmont|Lady Alice Fairmont]]** married in 810 ES.<br>They had two children before Alice's untimely death in 835 ES:
    - **Lord Charles Goldweaver (b. 812 ES, d. 889 ES)**
    - **Lady Eleanor Goldweaver (b. 814 ES, d. 877 ES)**

- **[[Beatrice Thornwood|Lady Beatrice Thornwood]]** married in 837 ES.<br>They had three children before their marriage ended in 855 ES:
    - **Lady Beatrice Goldweaver (b. 838 ES, d. 880 ES)**
    - **Lord Richard Goldweaver (b. 840 ES, d. 882 ES)**
    - **Lady Constance Goldweaver (b. 843 ES, d. 885 ES)**

- **[[Catherine Rivendale|Lady Catherine Rivendale]]** married in 860 ES. <br>They had four children: 
    - **Lady Alice Goldweaver (b. 851 ES, d. 892 ES)**
    - **Lady Matilda Goldweaver (b. 862 ES, d. 888 ES)**
    - **Lord John Goldweaver (b. 866 ES, d. 920 ES)**
    - **Lady Agatha Goldweaver (b. 869 ES, d. 930 ES)**
 
### Later Life and Legacy
Cedric continued to expand the [[Goldweaver Trading Company]] until his death in 868 ES. His legacy was carried on by his children and grandchildren, who continued to grow the family business and maintain its position as the King's appointed merchant. 

#### Descendants

Cedric's descendants continued to build upon his legacy, ensuring that the Goldweaver name remained synonymous with wealth, power, and influence in the Kingdom of Karth well into the future.
