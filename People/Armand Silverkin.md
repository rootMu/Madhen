**born:** 788 ES **died:** 860 ES

Lord Armand Silverkin, originating from the cosmopolitan regions of [[Ardenshire]], became one of the influential [[Three Bridges#Founding Merchant Lords|founding members]] of [[Three Bridges]]. Known for his expansive textile trade, Armand hailed from a long lineage of silk merchants whose operations spanned across the continent and into the exotic markets of distant lands. With his sharp eye for quality and innovative weaving techniques, he was celebrated for bringing luxurious fabrics to the heart of Madhen.

#### Early Life and Education
Armand was raised in the bustling trade city of [[Ardenshire]], where he was immersed in the family business from a young age. Educated both at home and abroad, he mastered the intricacies of international trade, languages, and the art of silk weaving, setting the stage for his later ventures in expanding the family’s textile empire.

#### Founding of Three Bridges
In 805 ES, recognising the strategic potential of a settlement at the nexus of major trade routes, Armand championed the development of [[Three Bridges]]. His vision was instrumental in establishing the city’s first Weavers' Guild, which became a vital aspect of the city's economy, attracting artisans from across the globe.

### Marriage and Family
Armand married [[Julia Wovenstrand|Lady Julia Wovenstrand]] in 812 ES. Their partnership not only strengthened their familial ties but also amalgamated their textile resources. Together, they nurtured a family deeply rooted in the values of commerce and cultural appreciation:
- **Lady Sylvia Silverkin (b. 815 ES, d. 878 ES)**
- **Lord Frederick Silverkin (b. 818 ES, d. 884 ES)**

### Later Life and Legacy
Continuing to influence global textile markets until his death in 860 ES, Armand’s legacy is marked by the exotic fabrics and vibrant markets he helped establish in Three Bridges. His pioneering efforts in textile innovation left an indelible mark on the city and its economic landscape.

#### Descendants
Armand Silverkin’s descendants continue to dominate the textile industry, maintaining the high standards of quality and luxury he set. The Silverkin name remains synonymous with the finest fabrics and artisanal mastery, echoing his profound impact on the trade.
