The King of [[Gilded Dominion]], his true name is shrouded in mystery, known only as the Slaver King.

#### Power and Longevity
He has maintained his rule for thousands of years, employing dark rituals and magical means to extend his life. These rituals involve the horrific sacrifice of infants in utero and the theft of life essence from slaves.

#### Support Staff
His longevity is further supported by a dedicated chronomancer, whose sole purpose is to enhance the king's lifespan. This chronomancer trains a single apprentice who will take over upon their passing, ensuring continuity in this dark art.

#### Influence
As the pinnacle of power in [[Gilded Dominion]], the Slaver King not only governs the political landscape but also oversees the operations and regulations of the slave trade out of [[Strakhaven]].
