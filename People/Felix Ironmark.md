**born:** 787 ES **died:** 861 ES

Lord Felix Ironmark, hailing from the rugged terrain of [[The Hinterlands]], became a prominent figure in the realm and one of the [[Three Bridges#Founding Merchant Lords|founding members]] of [[Three Bridges]]. Originating from a family of master smiths and metal traders who migrated from the distant continent of Ruban, Felix brought a rich heritage of metallurgy and mineral trade to the burgeoning trade centre. Known for his robust physique and steadfast determination, he was widely respected for his expertise in ores and innovative smelting techniques.

#### Early Life and Education
Raised amid the mining operations of [[The Hinterlands]], Felix's childhood was deeply embedded in the lore of metals and the earth. His education, guided by his father—a [[Corva|Corvan]] immigrant—blended rigorous craftsmanship with sophisticated trade strategies, equipping him to expand the family business significantly.

#### Founding of Three Bridges
In 802 ES, recognising the strategic potential of a permanent settlement at the convergence of major trade routes, Felix played an instrumental role in establishing [[Three Bridges]]. He founded the city's first foundry, which later became the cornerstone of the Metalworkers' Guild, a key factor in the city’s economic ascent.

### Marriage and Family
Felix married [[Miranda Coalheart|Lady Miranda Coalheart]] in 810 ES. This union merged two influential mining families, strengthening their collective business endeavour's. Together, they raised three children in the industrious atmosphere of [[Three Bridges]]:
- **Lord Garrick Ironmark (b. 813 ES, d. 879 ES)**
- **Lady Elara Ironmark (b. 816 ES, d. 882 ES)**
- **Lord Dorian Ironmark (b. 820 ES, d. 888 ES)**

### Later Life and Legacy
Continuing to innovate in metal production and trade until his death in 861 ES, Felix's initiatives significantly expanded the economic base of [[Three Bridges]]. His pioneering work introduced new metal alloys and techniques that had a lasting impact across multiple regions.

#### Descendants
Felix Ironmark’s descendants uphold his legacy of quality and innovation in metalworking. The Ironmark Foundry remains a cornerstone of [[Three Bridges]]’ industrial sector, with his family continuing to influence metal trades well beyond the city's borders.
