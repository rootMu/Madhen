**born:** 805 ES **died:** 868 ES

**Lady Catherine Rivendale** was [[Cedric Goldweaver|Lord Cedric Goldweaver's]] third wife. Their wedding took place in 860 ES at the scenic [[Lakeside Pavilion]] on the Inland Sea of [[Mirrormere]], celebrated with grandeur befitting their prominent status. Together, they had four children:

- **Lady Alice Goldweaver (born in 851 ES)**
- **Lord John Goldweaver (born 862 ES)**
- **Lady Agatha Goldweaver (born 865 ES)**
- **Lady Matilda Goldweaver (born 868 ES)**

Lady Alice Goldweaver was conceived during Lord Cedric's ongoing marriage to Lady Beatrice Thornwood. This circumstance is a delicate aspect of the family's history yet it is well documented and a known historical fact reflecting complexities within Lord Cedric's personal life during that time.
