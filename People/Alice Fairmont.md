**born:** 793 ES **died:** 835 ES

Lady Alice Fairmont was [[Cedric Goldweaver|Lord Cedric Goldweaver's]] first wife. They were wed in 810 ES at [[Goldweaver Manor]] the ancestral home of the Goldweaver family. Together, they had two children:

- **Lord Charles Goldweaver** (born 816 ES)
- **Lady Eleanor Goldweaver** (born 819 ES)
