**born:** 798 ES **died:** 860 ES

Lady Beatrice Thornwood was [[Cedric Goldweaver|Lord Cedric Goldweaver's]] second wife. They married in 837 ES at the Chapel of the Verdant Fields, a popular venue for high society weddings in [[Ardenshire]]. They had three children:

- **Lady Beatrice Goldweaver** (born 822 ES)
- **Lord Richard Goldweaver** (born 825 ES)
- **Lady Constance Goldweaver** (born 828 ES)
