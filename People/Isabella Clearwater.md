**born:** 790 ES **died:** 864 ES

Lady Isabella Clearwater, hailing from the serene lakeside villages of Verdanthold in central Ardenshire, rose to prominence as one of the [[Three Bridges#Founding Merchant Lords|founding members]] of [[Three Bridges]]. Renowned for her exquisite taste and sharp business acumen, Isabella was a leading figure in the trade of freshwater pearls and fine jewellery, which brought a touch of elegance and sophistication to the markets of Madhen.

#### Early Life and Education
Isabella was raised in a family known for their long-standing tradition in the jewellery trade, specifically focusing on pearls harvested from the lakes of Verdanthold. Her upbringing was marked by a deep appreciation for the finer things in life, coupled with a rigorous education in gemology and commerce. She travelled extensively across Karth and beyond, enhancing her understanding of the global gem market and establishing valuable contacts.

#### Founding of Three Bridges
In 807 ES, Isabella recognised the potential of a trading post at the junction of major rivers and played a pivotal role in the founding of [[Three Bridges]]. She was key in establishing the Jewelers' Market within the city, which quickly became a central hub for traders and artisans dealing in precious stones and metals.

### Marriage and Family
Isabella married [[Lucas Windale|Lord Lucas Windale]] in 814 ES. Their partnership was not only romantic but also a strategic alliance that combined their expertise in jewellery making and luxury goods trading. Together, they nurtured a family deeply involved in their trade:
- **Lord Marcus Clearwater (b. 817 ES, d. 881 ES)**
- **Lady Cecilia Clearwater (b. 820 ES, d. 889 ES)**

### Later Life and Legacy
Continuing to innovate in the luxury goods market until her death in 864 ES, Isabella's legacy is marked by the renowned Jewelers' Market in Three Bridges, which continues to attract elite buyers and artisans from across the continent. Her pioneering efforts in jewellery design and trade significantly shaped the city's economic and cultural landscape.

#### Descendants
Isabella Clearwater’s legacy of elegance and excellence in jewellery continues through her descendants, who maintain the high standards she set. The Clearwater family remains influential in the jewellery trade, ensuring that her contributions to the craft are remembered and revered.
