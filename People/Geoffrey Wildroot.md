**born:** 788 ES **died:** 862 ES

Lord Geoffrey Wildroot, originating from a small town near modern-day [[Woodhaven]] in [[Verdanthold]], a key shire of central Ardenshire, just south of the Everscram Forest, emerged as one of the [[Three Bridges#Founding Merchant Lords|founding members]] of [[Three Bridges]]. Geoffrey, known for his vast knowledge of exotic woods and fine carpentry, played a crucial role in establishing the Woodworkers' Guild within the city, greatly influencing its architectural and economic development.

#### Early Life and Education
Geoffrey was born into a family of skilled woodworkers and carpenters, where he learned the trade from his father. Growing up surrounded by the lush forests of Ardenshire provided him with a deep appreciation for the variety and qualities of wood available in his region. His education extended beyond the craft of woodworking, as he also studied the business of timber trade, which prepared him to expand his family's operations significantly.

#### Founding of Three Bridges
Recognising the strategic position of Three Bridges as a trade nexus, Geoffrey saw an opportunity to influence the city’s growth by introducing his expertise in woodcraft and trade. In 806 ES, he was instrumental in the construction of the first structures in Three Bridges, using wood sourced from the sustainable forests of Ardenshire. His contributions were not only functional but also aesthetically significant, helping to shape the city’s character.

### Marriage and Family
Geoffrey married [[Elise Greenwood|Lady Elise Greenwood]] in 811 ES, a union that fortified his ties to other prominent woodcraft families. Together, they raised a family who continued the tradition of woodworking:
- **Lord Harold Wildroot (b. 814 ES, d. 880 ES)**
- **Lady Marian Wildroot (b. 817 ES, d. 875 ES)**

### Later Life and Legacy
Geoffrey continued to innovate in woodcraft and trade until his death in 862 ES. His initiatives not only expanded the capabilities of Three Bridges in terms of construction and artisanal products but also ensured that the city became known for its superb craftsmanship in wood.

#### Descendants
Geoffrey Wildroot’s legacy lives on through his descendants, who continue to lead the Woodworkers' Guild. The Wildroot family maintains a reputation for excellence in woodworking, with their craftsmanship highly sought after in Ardenshire and beyond.
