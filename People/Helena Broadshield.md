Lady Helena Broadshield, born in 790 ES, emerged as a formidable figure in the Kingdom of **[[Karth]]** and was one of the **[[Three Bridges#Founding Merchant Lords|Founding Merchant Lords]]** of **[[Three Bridges]]**. As the scion of the renowned Broadshield armoury, Helena was raised to helm the family business, known across the continent for its superior weapons and armour. Distinguished by her steely gaze and commanding presence, she was revered for her strategic mind and unyielding resolve.

#### Early Life and Education

Helena's upbringing was steeped in the martial traditions of her family, within the fortified walls of **[[Broadshield Keep]]**. Her education, rigorous and diverse, included mastery of blacksmithing, tactics, and the martial arts, alongside commerce. She quickly distinguished herself not only as a skilled warrior but also as a shrewd negotiator, expanding the reach and reputation of her family's armoury.

#### Founding of Three Bridges

By 805 ES, Helena recognised the strategic potential of the nascent trading post at the confluence of two major rivers. Her vision and leadership were crucial in transforming it into the thriving mercantile hub of **[[Three Bridges]]**, where she was instrumental in establishing the Armoury District, now a cornerstone of the city’s economy.

### Marriage and Family

Helena's personal life was marked by her marriage to **[[Edward Stonearm|Lord Edward Stonearm]]** in 815 ES. Together, they had two children, who were raised in the bustling environment of Three Bridges:

- **Lord Roland Broadshield (b. 817 ES, d. 886 ES)**
- **Lady Gwendolyn Broadshield (b. 820 ES, d. 878 ES)**

### Later Life and Legacy

Helena continued to lead Broadshield Armoury with distinction, further cementing its status as a key supplier for kingdoms and mercenary companies alike, until her death in 865 ES. Her legacy endures through her innovations in armoury and her pivotal role in establishing Three Bridges as a trade capital.

#### Descendants

Helena's descendants have upheld the Broadshield tradition of excellence in craftsmanship and martial prowess, ensuring that the Broadshield name remains emblematic of quality and resilience in the armament industry.
