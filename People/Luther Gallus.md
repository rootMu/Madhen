### **Overview**
Luther Gallus, known as "The Fat Cat," is the slaver responsible for overseeing the [[Spell Slingers]] in the [[Gilded Dominion||Gilded Dominion]]. Despite his significant power and authority, he is notorious for his laziness and lack of diligence.

### **Appearance**
Luther is a rotund man with a perpetually dishevelled appearance. His clothes are often stained, and he exudes an air of indifference towards his responsibilities. 

### **Personality**
Luther is known for his slovenly and negligent nature. He prefers to indulge in the luxuries afforded by his status rather than diligently managing his [[Spell Slingers]]. His lack of attention to detail often results in lapses of control, though he vehemently denies any such issues.

### **Responsibilities**
- **Management of Spell Slingers:** Luther is in charge of all [[Spell Slingers]], ensuring their training and indoctrination.
- **Hiring Mages:** He is responsible for hiring mages to create the magical munitions that [[Spell Slingers]] use.
- **Maintaining Order:** Despite his lax approach, Luther is tasked with maintaining order among the [[Spell Slingers]], a task he frequently fails to accomplish effectively.

### **Reputation**
While Luther claims to have complete control over his [[Spell Slingers]], there are frequent reports of rebellion and unrest among them. These issues are often attributed to his poor management, though Luther dismisses them as mere rumours. His most trusted slave, known only as "The Panther," plays a crucial role in maintaining order. The Panther is an assassin who has successfully eliminated all rebel Spell Slingers and quashed potential uprisings before they could escalate. The reason for the Panther's unwavering loyalty remains a mystery, but rumours suggest they originated from the depths of [[Marvolo|Marvolo the Chainweaver's]] infamous [[Citadel of Chains]], specifically from a place referred to only as the "Sanctuary of Whispers."

### **Conclusion**
Luther Gallus represents the flawed yet powerful figure within the Gilded Dominion. His role as the overseer of Spell Slingers highlights the delicate balance of control and power, underscored by his notorious negligence and the ever-present threat of rebellion among his charges.
