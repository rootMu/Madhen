### **Overview**
Madam Gertrude Hardwick, commonly referred to as "Gertie", is a prominent figure in [[Strakhaven || Strakhaven's]] security forces. Her dedication and effectiveness in maintaining order have made her a respected and feared enforcer in the chaotic streets of Strakhaven.

### **Appearance**
Madam Gertie is a tall, imposing figure, standing at 6 feet with a robust build that reflects her years of rigorous training. Her hair, once a fiery red, is now streaked with gray, and she wears it in a tight bun atop her head. Her face is lined with the marks of time and experience, but her sharp, blue eyes retain their keen, penetrating gaze. She is always seen in her impeccably maintained uniform, complete with polished boots and a heavy belt laden with various tools and keys.

### **Background**
Born into a lineage of dedicated enforcers, Gertie rose through the ranks with a blend of shrewdness, strict discipline, and an unwavering sense of duty. Her methods, though often seen as harsh, are undeniably effective. Gertie takes pride in maintaining order and security in the chaotic streets of [[Strakhaven]], and she does so with an iron fist wrapped in a velvet glove.

### **Methods of Control**
Gertie employs a unique method to ensure the absolute obedience of her captives. She uses a magical item known as the [[Leash of Arcane Suppression]]. This leash prevents spell casters from using their magic without her permission and allows her to direct their spells toward targets of her choosing.

### **Responsibilities**
As part of [[Strakhaven||Strakhaven's]] general security, Gertie is responsible for maintaining law and order, overseeing the training of new recruits, and managing high-risk prisoners. She is particularly adept at handling magic users, using specialised equipment like the Leash of Arcane Suppression to keep them in check.

### **Current Role** 
Gertie is currently tasked with controlling high-risk prisoners, particularly those with magical abilities. She uses her [[Leash of Arcane Suppression]] to prevent any magical mischief and ensure they remain under control. Gertie takes her job very seriously and maintains a strict regimen to enforce obedience and discipline among her captives.

### **Characteristics**
**Personality:** Madam Gertie is stern and no-nonsense, with a commanding presence that demands respect. She has a soft spot for discipline and order, believing firmly in the adage "spare the rod, spoil the child." Despite her severity, she is fiercely protective of those under her care, albeit in a somewhat overbearing manner.

### **Reputation**
Madam Gertie is known for her strict but fair leadership. Her name is synonymous with discipline and control within the [[Gilded Dominion]]. While her methods are often harsh, they are effective, and she is respected by her peers and feared by her captives.
