### **Overview**
Marvolo the Chainweaver is one of the chief slavers of [[Strakhaven]], using his powers to enforce his will and maintain order in the [[Gilded Dominion]]. His mastery of chains is both literal and metaphorical, as he binds the wills of those around him.

### **Appearance**
Marvolo is a tall, imposing figure with a commanding presence. He has dark, piercing eyes that seem to see through to one's soul. His hair is black and kept short, and he often wears intricately designed chain mail that symbolises his dominion over chains. His skin is pale, a stark contrast to his dark attire.

### **Background**
Rising through the ranks due to his ruthless tactics and dark magical abilities, Marvolo earned his title by subduing many rebellions and turning powerful enemies into obedient slaves. He is known for his unyielding control and innovative methods of enslavement.

### **Methods of Control**
Marvolo employs a unique method to ensure the absolute obedience of his slaves. He uses special magic jars known as [[Torment Vessels]]. The Torment Vessels function through a dark and twisted alchemical process, allowing the subject to be changed in both body and mind. 

### **Responsibilities**
As a chief slaver, Marvolo oversees the acquisition, training, and management of slaves within the [[Gilded Dominion]]. He ensures that the slaves are loyal and effective, utilising strict physical training regimes and mental training exercises to maintain order and control. His responsibilities include training elite slaves, who often end up in prominent roles such as leading major brothels or becoming top gladiators.

### **Public Perception**
Among the citizens of Strakhaven, Marvolo is seen as a respectable figure. They perceive him as a strict but fair slaver who maintains order without the overt brutality displayed by others. His slaves are known for their unwavering loyalty, and he is often praised for his ability to manage them without resorting to public beatings.

### **Notable Slaves and Possessions**
- **The Panther**: An elite assassin who was trained by Marvolo and now serves as a secret enforcer for Luther Gallus. The Panther's loyalty is absolute, and their skills are unmatched among the slaves.
- **Grothar the Mighty**: A strength-based gladiator known for his incredible physical form and prowess, his unmatched combat skills in the coliseums of [[Strakhaven]] make him a crowd favourite. 
- **Aveline Fireheart**: A magic-based gladiator who wields immensely powerful spells in the arena, captivating audiences with her arcane abilities. 
- **Lady Aurelia**: The esteemed matron of the prominent brothel, [[The Velvet Rose]], in Strakhaven. A retired courtesan, she now oversees the establishment, ensuring it remains a place of luxury and discretion. Her years of experience and wisdom command respect among her peers and patrons alike.
- **Isolde the Enchantress**: A current courtesan renowned for her beauty and charm. Isolde is a favourite among the elite and uses her skills to maintain influence within [[Strakhaven||Strakhaven's]] high society. Clients often find it difficult to agree on the specifics of her appearance, yet they unanimously describe her as the most beautiful creature they have ever seen. Her captivating presence makes her unforgettable, while her exact visage remains mysteriously elusive.

### **Reputation**
Marvolo is known for his absolute control over his slaves and his effective, albeit secretive, methods. His name is synonymous with obedience and power within the [[Gilded Dominion]]. While his darker methods remain hidden, his public persona is one of strict yet fair leadership.

### **Conclusion**
Marvolo the Chainweaver is a figure of immense power and influence within the [[Gilded Dominion]]. His innovative methods and strict training regimes have cemented his place as a formidable slaver. Despite the fear he instills in his slaves, he is viewed as a respectable figure by the citizens of Strakhaven, maintaining order and stability in a realm where control is paramount.

***Chain Mastery.*** Marvolo can use his action to animate chains within 60 feet of him. These chains have a reach of 15 feet and can grapple or attack, using Marvolo's attack roll and ability scores. Grappled creatures are restrained and take 10 (3d6) piercing damage at the start of each of Marvolo's turns.
