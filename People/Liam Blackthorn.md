#### **Overview**

**Liam "The Fool" Blackthorn** is a living legend within Ardenshire, known for his mischievous nature and unwavering loyalty to his best friend, [[King Anthranar Serbius III]]. Although now tending goats in the [[Windswept Moors]], his past is filled with tales of adventure, cunning, and loyalty. His current life is shrouded in mystery, with many unaware of his true identity and the significant role he played in Ardenshire's history.

#### **Early Life and Friendship with Anthranar**

Liam was born into a humble family in [[Ulran]], where he quickly became known for his playful nature and sharp wit. His friendship with Anthranar Serbius III began in their childhood, as the two were inseparable, often getting into trouble with their pranks and schemes. Despite their playful antics, their bond was genuine, and Liam remained a steadfast companion to Anthranar throughout his life.

When Anthranar II passed away, and Anthranar III ascended to the throne at the young age of 15 in 1200 ES, Liam stood by his side, offering support and counsel. Known affectionately as "The Fool," Liam's cleverness and humour were invaluable to the young king.

#### **The Siege of Arnette and Liam's Disappearance**

In 1211 ES, during the height of the Arnealian threat, Liam was instrumental in persuading the Krial merchants to ferry King Anthranar and his six greatest champions across the [[Arkadian Expanse]] to [[Ruban]]. Their mission was to siege and set fire to the city of Arnette, forcing the Arnealian raiders to retreat.

During the siege, Liam's cunning and bravery were crucial in ensuring that Anthranar and two of the champions survived. It was believed that Liam had sacrificed himself to allow their escape, but in reality, he was left behind, trapped in Ruban. For 15 years, he lived under various pseudonyms, his life a mystery even to those who knew him best.

#### **Return and Life in Windswept Moors**

Upon his return to Ardenshire, Liam discovered that Anthranar had been dethroned and was presumed dead. Grieving and disillusioned, he chose to hide his true identity and took up a humble life as a goat farmer in the [[Windswept Moors]]. He frequently travels to [[Ulran]] to sell his goats and goat-related products, living a quiet, unassuming life.

#### **Physical Description**

- **Before Ruban:** Liam was a thin, clean-shaven young man with a mischievous glint in his eyes. His appearance was unremarkable, allowing him to blend in easily and use his wits to navigate various situations.
- **After Ruban:** The years in Ruban transformed Liam into a rugged, heavily bearded man with a lean, sinewy frame. His eyes, once filled with youthful mischief, now carry a depth of wisdom and a hint of sorrow. His time in Ruban remains a mystery, and the skills and knowledge he acquired during those years are unknown.

#### **Notable Possessions**

Liam carries a distinct ring given to him by King Anthranar III before the burning of Arnette. This ring is one of the few personal items he kept from his past life, a symbol of his loyalty and the deep bond he shared with Anthranar. The ring is ornate, featuring intricate designs and the royal crest, making it easily recognisable to those familiar with Ardenshire's history.

#### **Conclusion**

Liam "The Fool" Blackthorn's life is a tale of loyalty, cunning, and mystery. From his mischievous beginnings in Ulran to his pivotal role in the siege of Arnette, and finally to his quiet life in the Windswept Moors, Liam's story is a testament to his resilience and unwavering loyalty to his friend and king. Despite living in obscurity, his legacy endures in the annals of Ardenshire's history.
