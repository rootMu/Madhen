**born:** 789 ES **died:** 863 ES

Lady Rowena Swiftstream, hailing from the enchanting landscapes of [[The Marshes]], emerged as a significant figure and one of the [[Three Bridges#Founding Merchant Lords|founding members]] of [[Three Bridges]]. Known for her mastery in the trade of rare herbs and potions, Rowena brought with her an extensive knowledge of botanicals and alchemical substances from her lush homeland and beyond.

#### Early Life and Education
Rowena grew up surrounded by the diverse flora of The Marshes, where she learned the secrets of herbalism and potion making from local wise women and travelling alchemists. Her formal education was supplemented by extensive travels across different realms, where she expanded her understanding of exotic plants and their uses in medicine and magic.

#### Founding of Three Bridges
In 804 ES, Rowena recognised the strategic importance of establishing a trade hub at the confluence of major rivers. Her insight into the potential of such a location was pivotal in the founding of [[Three Bridges]]. She was instrumental in establishing the [[Alchemists' Tower]], a centre for alchemical study and trade, situated just south of [[Three Bridges]]. The tower attracted scholars and practitioners from far and wide.

### Marriage and Family
Rowena married [[Elric Stormcaller|Lord Elric Stormcaller]] in 813 ES. Together, they blended their respective expertise in alchemy and meteorology to enhance their business ventures. They raised two children who inherited their parents' love for nature and science:
- **Lord Alaric Swiftstream (b. 817 ES, d. 876 ES)**
- **Lady Mirabel Swiftstream (b. 820 ES, d. 885 ES)**

### Later Life and Legacy
Rowena continued to innovate in the field of alchemy and herbal trade until her death in 863 ES. Her legacy is evident in the thriving alchemical community of Three Bridges, which remains a critical centre for the study and trade of herbal and alchemical products.

#### Descendants
Rowena Swiftstream’s descendants uphold her legacy of botanical knowledge and alchemical innovation. The Swiftstream family continues to manage the [[Alchemists' Tower]], ensuring that her contributions to the scientific and magical communities are preserved and advanced.
