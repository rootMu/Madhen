### **Overview**
The Tempestas Mountains, located centrally within the vast expanse of the [[Umbral Plains]], are a formidable and ancient range that has long been a source of both wonder and trepidation. These mountains are known for their towering peaks, frequent lightning storms, and the deep, verdant canyons that split the range in two—the [[Veridian Chasms]].

### **Geography**
The Tempestas Mountains are characterised by their dramatic landscape, with jagged peaks that reach high into the sky, often shrouded in storm clouds. The mountain range is divided into two parts: the higher, more rugged eastern range, and the slightly lower western range. Between them lies the [[Veridian Chasms]], a verdant rift filled with dense vegetation and charged with a palpable sense of magic. This division creates a striking contrast between the barren, rocky heights of the mountains and the lush, verdant sanctuary of the chasms below.

On the eastern side, the mountains are higher and more treacherous, with sheer cliffs and narrow passes that challenge even the most experienced climbers. The western side is slightly lower but equally rugged, with old mining tunnels and caves that speak to the region’s history of mineral extraction.

The highest peaks are perpetually shrouded in dark clouds, from which powerful lightning bolts frequently strike, giving the mountains their name. These storms are often accompanied by torrential rains and howling winds, making the mountain paths extremely dangerous. 

### **Flora and Fauna**
Despite the harsh conditions, the Tempestas Mountains are home to a surprising variety of flora and fauna. Hardy alpine plants cling to the rocky slopes, and ancient, gnarled trees with thick bark resistant to lightning strikes can be found in sheltered valleys. The lower slopes are covered with thick pine forests, while the upper regions are barren, save for patches of resilient moss and lichen.

The mountains are home to a range of wildlife, including mountain goats, eagles, and wolves. However, the most notable and feared inhabitant is the young lightning dragon that is rumoured to have made its lair deep within the highest peaks. This dragon is said to control the storms that ravage the mountains, and its presence has deterred many would-be adventurers.

### **Settlements**
There are very few settlements near the Tempestas Mountains due to the dangerous conditions. However, some hardy communities do exist on the lower slopes and in the valleys. 

- **[[Stormwatch Keep]]**: A small, fortified outpost located on the eastern slopes of the Tempestas Mountains. It serves as a lookout and refuge for those who dare to venture into the mountains. The keep is manned by a group of rangers who are well-versed in the dangers of the region.

- **[[Thunderfall]]**: A remote village situated at the base of the mountains, Thunderfall is home to a rugged group of miners who brave the mountains to extract valuable ores and minerals. The village is named for the waterfall that cascades down from the mountains, creating a constant, thunderous noise.

- **[[Stormveil]]**: Perched on the higher passes of the eastern range, Stormveil is a thriving community of miners, traders, and adventurers. The settlement stretches across the mountains, with sturdy bridges connecting the eastern side to the western interior, where old mining tunnels and caverns have been repurposed into homes and workshops. Stormveil’s position provides both security and access to the rich resources of the mountains, making it a vital outpost in the [[Umbral Plains]].

### **Rumours and Mysteries**
The Tempestas Mountains are shrouded in mystery and local legends. 

- **Dragon’s Lair**: It is widely rumoured that a young lightning dragon has made its lair in the highest peaks of the Tempestas Mountains. Many adventurers have sought out the dragon, but few have returned, leading to speculation about the true nature of the creature and its lair.

- **The Hidden Path**: There are whispers of a hidden path that leads through the mountains to a secret valley where ancient, forgotten treasures lie. This path is said to be guarded by powerful spirits and is only visible during the fiercest storms.

- **Lost Expeditions**: Over the years, many expeditions have been lost in the Tempestas Mountains. Some believe that these adventurers were captured by the dragon, while others think they found the hidden path but were unable to return.

### **Conclusion**
The Tempestas Mountains are a region of untamed beauty and deadly peril. While the promise of treasure and adventure draws many to its peaks, the mountains' dangers are very real, and only the bravest or most foolhardy dare to explore their depths. The presence of a young lightning dragon, the possibility of hidden paths and lost treasures, and the ever-present threat of the elements make the Tempestas Mountains one of Karth's most intriguing and hazardous locations. With settlements like Stormveil bridging the gap between the eastern and western ranges, the Tempestas Mountains continue to be a vital, if treacherous, part of the Umbral Plains.
