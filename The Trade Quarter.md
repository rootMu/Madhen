### **Overview**
The Trade Quarter is the industrial heart of the [[Allspar Market]], serving as the primary hub for bulk goods and essential supplies in [[Ruban]]. This bustling area is characterised by large warehouses and expansive stalls where merchants engage in lively negotiations over commodities such as grains, textiles, and raw materials. The Trade Quarter plays a crucial role in supporting the economic framework of [[Madhen]] and [[Strakhaven]], providing the necessary resources for various industries and enterprises.

### **Market Layout**
The Trade Quarter is a labyrinth of wide avenues lined with large stalls and storage facilities. The atmosphere is a mix of organised chaos, with traders haggling over prices and labourers moving goods. The section is designed to facilitate the efficient movement of goods, ensuring that transactions are conducted swiftly and with minimal disruption.

#### **Permanent Merchants**
- **Grain Merchant**: Selling bulk grains and cereals, this merchant caters to bakers, brewers, and large households.
- **Textile Trader**: Offering bolts of fabric and raw textiles, this trader deals in everything from cotton to silk.
- **Metal Supplier**: A key source for blacksmiths and artisans, this supplier offers raw and processed metals.

#### **Temporary Stalls**
- **Lumberjack's Stand**: Featuring timber and wood products, this stall is frequented by carpenters and builders.
- **Stonecutter's Corner**: Seasonal vendors offering various types of stone and masonry materials.
- **Livestock Pen**: Temporary pens where livestock is sold for meat, labour, and breeding purposes.

### **Detailed Walkthrough**

#### **Grain Merchant**
Located near the main entrance of the Trade Quarter, the Grain Merchant is a cornerstone of this section. The stall is dominated by large sacks of grains and cereals, from wheat and barley to rice and oats. The merchant is always busy, dealing with bakers and brewers looking to stock up on essential ingredients. The prices here are competitive, reflecting the high volume of trade.

#### **Textile Trader**
Further into the Trade Quarter, visitors will find the Textile Trader. This expansive stall is filled with bolts of fabric in a multitude of colours and textures. From everyday cotton to luxurious silk, the Textile Trader has something for everyone. The merchant prides themselves on the quality of their goods and often negotiates large orders with tailors and clothing manufacturers.

#### **Metal Supplier**
On the western side of the Trade Quarter, the Metal Supplier's stall is a hub of activity. Large ingots of raw metal, sheets of processed materials, and various alloys are stacked high. Blacksmiths and artisans frequent this stall, sourcing the metals needed for their craft. The merchant is knowledgeable and can provide advice on the best materials for specific projects.

#### **Lumberjack's Stand**
Interspersed among the permanent merchants are the temporary stalls like the Lumberjack's Stand. This vendor specialises in timber and wood products, from rough-cut logs to finely crafted planks. Carpenters and builders come here to source the wood they need for construction and woodworking projects. The variety and quality of the wood change with the seasons, ensuring a fresh supply.

#### **Stonecutter's Corner**
Seasonal vendors at the Stonecutter's Corner offer a variety of stone and masonry materials. These stalls are popular among builders and architects looking for high-quality stone for construction and decorative purposes. From granite and marble to sandstone and limestone, the Stonecutter's Corner provides essential materials for the building trade.

#### **Livestock Pen**
The Livestock Pen is a temporary feature of the Trade Quarter, where animals are sold for meat, labour, and breeding. Cattle, sheep, goats, and even more exotic livestock can be found here, each pen filled with animals ready for purchase. Farmers and butchers are frequent visitors, negotiating prices and selecting the best stock for their needs.

### **Conclusion**
The Trade Quarter is an essential part of the [[Allspar Market]], driving the economic engine of [[Ruban]] and [[Strakhaven]]. It is a place where bulk goods and essential supplies are traded, ensuring the smooth operation of various industries and enterprises. With its mix of permanent merchants and temporary stalls, the Trade Quarter provides a dynamic and efficient marketplace for traders and buyers alike. Whether sourcing raw materials or negotiating large-scale deals, visitors to the Trade Quarter will find it a vital hub of commerce and trade.
