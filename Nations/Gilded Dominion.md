#### Location
Situated on the northwestern coast of [[Ruban]], The Gilded Dominion features stark contrasts—from lush coastal plains to barren, rocky interiors.  
  
#### Economy and Society
The economy is heavily reliant on the slave trade, deeply embedded as both an economic. activity and a grim cultural institution. The caste system places slavers at the elite tier, influencing both societal structure and daily life.

#### Government and Military
Governed by the [[Slaver King]], supported by a council of high-ranking slavers, the Dominion enforces its rule through the [[Iron Chain Legion]]. This military force is notorious for its brutality, tasked with quelling uprisings, protecting slaving operations, and expanding territorial influence.

#### Culture and Beliefs
Power and domination are revered, epitomised in the worship of [[Strakh]], the mythical God of Chains. The culture is characterised by brutal gladiatorial combats as both public spectacle and religious ceremony, underscoring the Dominion’s values.

#### Security and Enforcement
To protect this vast network of trade and ensure compliance with its tithing system, the Gilded Dominion maintains a formidable military presence, both at sea and within its territories. This military, known as [[Iron Chain Legion]], is tasked not only with defending against external threats but also with enforcing trade agreements and tithes, ensuring that economic sanctions and repercussions are swiftly and effectively applied to those who breach their agreements.

This framework of slavery-based economics, bolstered by strategic diplomacy and rigorous enforcement, forms the backbone of the Gilded Dominion’s power and influence on the international stage, making it a formidable and often contentious player in global politics.

### Slave Trade and Diplomatic Practices of The Gilded Dominion

#### The Slave Trade
The Gilded Dominion's economy is significantly bolstered by the slave trade, which operates both internally and through extensive international networks. This trade is not merely an economic activity but a deeply ingrained cultural institution upheld by both legal and military forces. Slavery is central to the Dominion's wealth, and as such, it has developed a complex system of trade relations that supports this practice.

#### Tribute and Tithe System
To facilitate and regulate trade, the Gilded Dominion has instituted a tribute system, commonly referred to as a "tithe," which is a mandatory fee that foreign nations must pay to maintain trade relations and gain access to lucrative markets like [[Allspar Market]], in [[Strakhaven]]. This tithe can be paid in two forms:

- Slave Tithe: Nations that are compliant or complicit in the slave trade can pay their tithes by sending slaves to the Gilded Dominion. This method is often used by governments that find it economically or politically expedient to dispose of surplus populations, prisoners, or other marginalised groups by selling them into slavery.
 
- Monetary Tithe: Nations that do not engage in the slave trade or those that have outlawed slavery, must pay exorbitant fees each lunar cycle to ensure continued access to the markets and trading privileges within the Dominion. This fee is deliberately set high to coerce these nations into considering the slave tithe as a more viable economic alternative.

#### Diplomatic Strategies
The Gilded Dominion employs a cadre of skilled diplomats who travel to various nations to foster diplomatic relationships and secure trade agreements. Their primary objectives are:

- Currying Favour: Diplomats from the Dominion are well-versed in the arts of persuasion and negotiation. They offer promises of wealth, power, and exclusive trading rights to those nations willing to accept their terms of trade, which include permitting Gilded Dominion slavers to operate within their borders.
  
- Establishing Presence: By negotiating terms that allow their slavers to work internationally, the Dominion aims to expand its influence and integrate its slave trade practices globally. This not only increases their economic reach but also entrenches the cultural and political influence of the Dominion abroad.

#### Impact on International Relations

- Economic Dependence: Nations that become reliant on the economic benefits offered by the Gilded Dominion may find themselves politically and morally compromised, as they must weigh the benefits of trade against the ethical implications of supporting or engaging in slavery.

- Cultural and Ethical Conflicts: The Dominion's aggressive promotion of slavery can lead to cultural clashes and ethical debates within nations that either oppose or are divided on the issue of slavery. This can lead to internal strife or changes in foreign policy, as governments navigate the complex dynamics between economic benefits and ethical governance.

### Gladiatorial Arenas of the Gilded Dominion
The Gilded Dominion hosts a vast network of gladiatorial arenas, each classified based on its scale, prestige, and the nature of the spectacles it hosts. These arenas are distributed across the entire dominion, providing entertainment and enforcing the cultural values of power and dominance throughout the region.

This classification and distribution of arenas across the Gilded Dominion ensure that the spectacle of gladiatorial combat is a pervasive aspect of life, reinforcing the cultural values of dominance and strength throughout the region. Each arena not only serves as a centre of entertainment but also as a tool for social control and a reflection of the hierarchical nature of this society.

##### Class VI: The Crucible of Champions
Located in the heart of [[Strakhaven]] within [[Aurelian Heights]], this arena stands as the pinnacle of gladiatorial combat in the Gilded Dominion. It is the most prestigious venue, hosting grand spectacles and high-stakes battles attended by the elite of society.

###### Arenas
- [[The Crucible of Champions]] is the singular, most revered arena in this class, serving as a cultural landmark.

##### Class V: Elite Proving Grounds
These arenas are known for their elaborate settings and feature magical and exotic combats. They are typically located in major cities or strategic locations within the Dominion to draw large crowds and high-profile attendees.

###### Arenas
- [[The People’s Arena]]: Positioned strategically to be accessible from different social strata in Strakhaven, known for its magical and underwater battles.
- [[The Arena of Mysts]]: Located near a major mage's guild, this arena utilises enchanted fog and illusions.
- [[The Celestial Coliseum]]: Situated in a city known for its astronomical studies, it hosts battles under magically simulated celestial events.

##### Class IV: Grand Arenas
Large-scale arenas that attract regional attention, located in various provincial capitals or important towns within the Dominion. These venues host traditional gladiatorial contests and are a step up from the more localised fights.

###### Arenas
- [[The Titan’s Bowl]]: Known for its massive scale, suitable for large creature battles.
- [[The Minotaur’s Maze]]: Features a complex layout, enhancing the challenge of each combat.
- [[The Serpent’s Pit]]: Specialises in venomous creatures, located in a region known for its deadly fauna.

##### Class III: Guild Arenas
Sponsored by trade guilds, these medium-sized arenas are dispersed across various trade hubs within the Dominion, focusing on showcasing local professional fighters.

###### Arenas
- [[The Smith’s Anvil]]: Favoured by the Blacksmith’s Guild for testing weapons and armour.
- [[The Alchemist’s Crucible]]: Features battles with chemical and magical hazards.
- [[The Weaver’s Circle]]: Known for its strategic combat setups involving terrain and obstacles.

##### Class II: Guild Arenas
These community arenas host local fighters and minor slaves beginning their careers, located in smaller towns and villages across the Dominion.

###### Arenas
- [[The Dust Bowl]]: A basic sand arena for rookie gladiators.
- [[The Pit of Trials]]: Focuses on raw melee combat.
- [[The Gauntlet]]: Known for its series of physical challenges.

##### Class I: Local Pits
The smallest arenas, these are informal venues found in the least populous areas of the Dominion, offering spontaneous and often brutal contests.

###### Arenas
- [[The Scrapper’s Ring]]: Popular for impromptu challenges among locals.
- [[The Shadow Den]]: Occasionally hosts unsanctioned matches.
- [[The Gutter’s End]]: A harsh venue where survival is the only prize.

### Major Slavers of Strakhaven in The Gilded Dominion

The Gilded Dominion, with its capital in Strakhaven, is infamous for its thriving slave trade, supported by a cadre of powerful and ruthless slavers. These individuals not only drive the economic and cultural lifeblood of the dominion but also wield significant political and social influence.

#### [[Slaver King|The Slaver King]]
- **Power and Longevity**: He has maintained his rule for thousands of years, employing dark rituals and magical means to extend his life. These rituals involve the horrific sacrifice of infants in utero and the theft of life essence from slaves.
- **Support Staff**: His longevity is further supported by a dedicated chronomancer, whose sole purpose is to enhance the king's lifespan. This chronomancer trains a single apprentice who will take over upon their passing, ensuring continuity in this dark art.
- **Influence**: As the pinnacle of power in the Gilded Dominion, the Slaver King not only governs the political landscape but also oversees the operations and regulations of the slave trade.

#### [[Marvolo|Marvolo the Chainweaver]]
- **Master of Manipulation**: Marvolo is a fearsome figure in the slave trade, renowned for his cruel and intricate methods of mental and physical manipulation. His mastery over chains is not just literal but metaphorical, as he binds the wills of those he enslaves, ensuring complete obedience through a combination of dark magic and psychological torment.
- **Architect of Suffering**: Operating from the [[Citadel of Chains]], Marvolo designs and oversees a range of harsh training programs that break spirits and bodies alike. His methods involve the use of cursed chains that leech strength and willpower, transforming slaves into highly obedient yet powerful entities.
- **Charismatic Leader**: Despite his notorious reputation, Marvolo possesses a dark charisma that allows him to influence and intimidate both his victims and his peers. This charm makes him a respected and feared leader within the slaving community of [[Strakhaven]], capable of swaying masses and bending the elite to his will.

#### Sylas Greyshade
- **Role**: A master of covert operations and espionage within the slave trade.
- **Tactics**: Sylas is known for his ability to infiltrate and manipulate slave markets and rival slaver networks, ensuring the Dominion's supremacy in the slave trade.
- **Influence**: Despite his lesser-known public profile, his actions have significant implications for the flow of slaves and the stability of slave markets throughout the dominion.

#### Lady Thessalia Vane
- **Elegance and Brutality**: Known for her deceptive elegance, Lady Thessalia combines high society charm with brutal efficiency in managing her extensive slave operations.
- **Luxury Slave Trade**: Specialises in the trade of luxury slaves, trained for high society roles, ranging from sophisticated courtesans to expert artisans, whose skills fetch exorbitant prices.
- **Social Influence**: Her social gatherings, masked as opulent parties, often serve as fronts for slave auctions and networking amongst the dominion’s elite.

#### Kregor the Ironfist
- **Physical Prowess**: As a former champion gladiator, Kregor utilises his combat experience to train slave warriors for the arenas.
- **Arena Operations**: He oversees several training compounds where slaves are moulded into gladiators, pit fighters, and private enforcers for wealthy patrons.
- **Reputation**: His brutal training regimens are as feared as they are respected; his name alone can command obedience and fear in equal measure.

#### Cassandra the Silkweaver
- **Weaver of Destinies**: Cassandra is known in Strakhaven as the Silkweaver, not only for her intricate dealings in the slave market but also for her ability to manipulate the fates of those she ensnares. Using her extensive network of spies and informants, she weaves intricate plots that ensure her continued dominance in the trade.
- **Cultured Collector**: Unlike many of her brutal counterparts, Cassandra specialises in the acquisition and training of slaves destined for intellectual and artistic roles. She prides herself on a collection of poets, musicians, and scholars, whose refined talents fetch high prices among the Dominion's elite.
- **Patron of the Arts**: She often hosts lavish gatherings where her slaves perform before crowds of the wealthy and powerful, using these events as subtle marketplaces for her living wares. Her influence extends into the cultural life of Strakhaven, making her a key figure in both its economic and social spheres.
