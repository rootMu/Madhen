### **Overview**
Spell Slingers are a unique class of trusted slaves within the Gilded Dominion, known for their innate magical abilities. Despite the collars they wear that prevent them from casting their own spells, they are trained to sling pre-prepared magical projectiles. These projectiles, imbued with various spells, make Spell Slingers invaluable assets for both defence and offence within the Dominion.

### **The Spell Slings**
Each projectile used by a Spell Slinger is imbued with a spell prepared by a mage. These can range from offensive spells like fireballs and lightning bolts to supportive spells such as mass heals and protective wards. The variety and potency of these spells make Spell Slingers versatile and highly effective in combat and strategic situations.

### **Training and Indoctrination**
The training of Spell Slingers is rigorous and intense, often bordering on brainwashing to ensure loyalty. This process includes:
- **Magical Aptitude Testing:** Identifying slaves with innate magical abilities.
- **Indoctrination:** A combination of psychological manipulation, magical coercion, and harsh discipline to instil unwavering obedience.
- **Combat Training:** Physical and magical training to hone their abilities in slinging spells accurately and effectively.
- **Loyalty Conditioning:** Continuous reinforcement of loyalty through rewards for good behaviour and severe penalties for disobedience.

### **Life as a Spell Slinger**
Once their training is complete, Spell Slingers are treated relatively well compared to other slaves. They are provided with individual apartments and a "wage" that is promised to be sent upon their release after 20 years of service. However, bad behaviour can result in elongation of their service period.

### **The Spell Collars**
The collars worn by Spell Slingers are foolproof devices that prevent them from casting their own magic. However, these collars do not hinder their ability to sling prepared spells. The collars are enchanted to suppress the wearer's innate magical abilities but allow them to channel the magic imbued in their projectiles.

### **Notable Figures**

#### **Luther "The Fat Cat" Gallus** 
[[Luther Gallus]], commonly referred to as "The Fat Cat," is the slaver in charge of all Spell Slingers. Despite his laziness and slovenly appearance, he holds significant power within the Gilded Dominion. He is responsible for hiring mages to create the magical munitions used by Spell Slingers. However, Luther's lax management often results in him losing control over his Spell Slingers, though he vehemently denies any such failures.

### **Challenges and Opportunities**
Despite the collars and rigorous training, not all Spell Slingers are loyal. Many harbour resentment and will seize any opportunity to rebel against their slaver. This constant undercurrent of potential rebellion makes the management of Spell Slingers a delicate and dangerous task.

### **Conclusion**
Spell Slingers represent both a powerful asset and a significant risk within the Gilded Dominion. Their magical abilities, harnessed through a combination of coercion and indoctrination, make them formidable forces on the battlefield. However, the ever-present threat of rebellion and the questionable management by figures like Luther Gallus highlight the fragile balance of control and power within the Dominion's ranks.
