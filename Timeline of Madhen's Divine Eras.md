### **Timeline of Madhen's Divine Eras**

The history of Madhen is divided into distinct eras, each marked by the rule and influence of deities, both greater and lesser. Below is an overview of these significant periods leading up to 0ES.

### **The Old Ways OW (Unknown Timeline)**
This era is characterised by the rule of the [[Greater Deities]], also known as the [[Greater Deities||Old Gods]]. These deities, often in horrific and monstrous forms, ruled over cities and nations that reflected their aspects. Each Old God embodied a specific sin or virtue, such as gluttony, lust, or war, and their domains were manifestations of their divine nature. This era is shrouded in mystery, with only fragmented historical records and ancient ruins bearing witness to its existence.

#### **Key Events:**
  - **Rule of the Old Gods:** Cities and nations flourished under the dominion of greater deities, with civilisations reflecting the divine aspects of their rulers.
  - **The Civil War of the Old Gods:** A monumental conflict erupted among the Old Gods, dividing them into opposing factions. Those deities who sought balance and harmony clashed with those who embodied chaos, malevolence and selfishness. The resulting battles were cataclysmic, reshaping the very landscape of Madhen.
  - **Geological Changes:** The war resulted in significant geographical formations, such as the Arph Scar, the height difference between the Windswept Moors and Crownshield, and the ravine beneath the Serin Mountains.
  - **Departure of the Old Gods:** The surviving greater deities, weary of the surface world, retreated to the Sundered Paradise, a sub-terrestrial paradise within the hollow world of Madhen. This marked the end of their direct rule over the surface.

### **The Epoch of Dominion ED (Approx. 100 BES - 8000 BES)**
Following the departure of the [[Greater Deities]], their offspring, the [[Lesser Deities]], assumed control. This era spans roughly 8000 years and is marked by the lesser deities ruling cities and kingdoms in their true forms in a twisted homage to their parents. 

#### **Key Events:**
  - **Dominion of Lesser Deities:** Lesser deities established their rule, each creating cities and kingdoms that reflected their divine aspects.
  - **Emergence of the Seven:** A group of powerful lesser deities, known as the Seven, began to renounce their sinful ways. The Mother was the first to do so, leading the others by example.
  - **Transformation and Elevation:** The Seven, through war and persuasion, convinced other lesser deities to renounce their ways. They helped build new paradises for their followers, eventually elevating themselves to greater deities.
  - **Clashes and Wars:** Numerous battles and conflicts occurred as the Seven sought to bring other lesser deities into their fold. This period was fraught with strife and war.
  - **Final Showdown:** The final battle saw the Seven facing off against 100 lesser deities. The Seven emerged victorious, binding the lesser deities' true forms and preventing them from physically manifesting on Madhen.

### **The Eyes of the Seven ES (0ES - Present)**
The current era began at 0ES, marked by the departure of the Seven to join their parents in the Sundered Paradise. They left the Arkadians in charge of defending the only known entry to this paradise.

#### **Key Events:**
  - **Departure of the Seven:** The Seven left Madhen to join the greater deities in Sundered Paradise, marking the start of a new era.
  - **Arkadian Guardianship:** The Arkadians were tasked with protecting the entrance to Sundered Paradise and ensuring its secrecy.
  - **Sealed Forms:** The physical presence of all deities was sealed from Madhen, allowing them only brief entry via hosts or surrogates.
  - **Mortal Dominion:** With the deities' direct influence diminished, mortals began to shape their own destinies, guided by the teachings and legacies of the Seven.

This timeline provides a structured overview of the divine eras in Madhen, highlighting the significant events and transformations that have shaped its history up to the present day.
