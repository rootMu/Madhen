
#### Overview

**Name**: Orin Falkner  
**Role**: Manager of the Token Lottery  
**Age**: Late 40s  
**Appearance**: Orin is a shrewd and savvy individual, well-versed in assessing the value of goods and deeds traded for lottery tokens. He has graying hair, a well-kept beard, and sharp, calculating eyes that miss nothing. Orin dresses in well-maintained but modest clothing, often in shades of brown and grey, reflecting his practical nature. He is always seen carrying a small ledger and a pouch filled with tokens.

#### Background

Orin Falkner grew up in the bustling heart of the Undying Market, where he learned the art of trade and negotiation from a young age. His parents were well-known merchants who dealt in rare and exotic goods, instilling in Orin a keen sense for business and value. Over the years, Orin's reputation for fairness and sharp judgment grew, earning him the position of Manager of the Token Lottery—a role that requires both integrity and discernment.

#### Responsibilities

As the Manager of the Token Lottery, Orin oversees the entire process of exchanging money, crafted goods, or deeds for tokens that grant audiences with King Vorenus. The lottery system is crucial in maintaining order and fairness, ensuring that everyone has an equal chance to present their case or request directly to the king.

#### Personality

Orin is charismatic and persuasive, with a knack for making people feel at ease while maintaining an aura of authority. He is known for his fair yet firm approach, balancing empathy with the needs of the city. Orin's shrewdness makes him an excellent judge of character and value, allowing him to manage the lottery with precision and fairness.

#### Abilities and Skills

- **Negotiation**: Orin excels in negotiating and assessing the true value of goods and services.
- **Organisation**: His meticulous record-keeping ensures that the lottery process runs smoothly and transparently.
- **Communication**: Orin's ability to communicate clearly and effectively helps him manage the expectations of those participating in the lottery.

#### Daily Routine

1. **Morning**: Orin starts his day early, reviewing the previous day's transactions and ensuring all records are up-to-date.
2. **Midday**: He spends time in the lottery office, meeting with citizens who wish to trade goods or deeds for lottery tokens. Orin personally inspects and assesses each trade, maintaining fairness and transparency.
3. **Afternoon**: Orin manages the weekly draw preparations, ensuring all tokens are accounted for and the process remains unbiased.
4. **Evening**: He often attends meetings with other city officials to discuss any issues or improvements related to the lottery system.

#### Key Relationships

- **King Vorenus**: Orin respects and admires King Vorenus for his wisdom and leadership. He takes his role seriously, knowing that it directly impacts the king's ability to govern effectively.
- **Merchants and Artisans**: Orin has strong connections with many merchants and artisans, often advising them on the best ways to present their goods for trade.
- **Citizens**: He is well-liked by the citizens for his fairness and approachability, often seen as a trusted figure in the community.

#### Notable Incidents

- **The Great Trade Dispute**: Orin once resolved a significant dispute between two rival merchants who both claimed ownership of a rare artefact. His fair and insightful judgment not only resolved the conflict but also strengthened the trust in the lottery system.
- **The Mysterious Token**: On one occasion, Orin discovered a counterfeit token in the lottery. His keen eye and thorough investigation prevented potential corruption and reinforced the integrity of the process.

#### Quotes

- "Fairness is the cornerstone of our society. Without it, we crumble."
- "Every trade tells a story; it's my job to ensure it's a fair one."
- "The value of a token is not in the gold it's worth, but in the opportunity it grants."

Orin Falkner remains a pillar of integrity and fairness within Veron, ensuring that all who seek an audience with King Vorenus have an equal and just chance.
