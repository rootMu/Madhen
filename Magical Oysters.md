
### Overview
Magical Oysters are giant mollusks found along the northern coast of Mirrormere, particularly in the town of Shimmering Sands. These oysters are known for their large size and their ability to produce magical pearls.

### Physical Description
- **Size**: Large
- **Colour**: Iridescent shell, typically in shades of blue, green, and purple
- **Armour Class**: 15 (natural armour)
- **Hit Points**: 30 (4d10 + 8)
- **Speed**: Immobile

### Abilities
- **Blindsight**: 30 ft.
- **Damage Resistances**: Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from Nonmagical Attacks
- **Condition Immunities**: Prone, Stunned

### Habitat
These oysters thrive in the shallow waters around Shimmering Sands, attaching themselves to rocks and the ocean floor.

### Behaviour
Magical Oysters are largely immobile and passive. They filter-feed and occasionally close their shells when disturbed.

### Harvesting Pearls
Adventurers and pearl farmers seek these oysters for the valuable and magical pearls they produce. 

## Pearl Classification Guide

Magical pearls are classified based on their shape and magical quality. The classification includes a number (1-7) for shape and a gemstone name for quality.

### Shape Classification
- **1**: Perfectly round
- **2**: Near-round
- **3**: Oval
- **4**: Button
- **5**: Drop
- **6**: Baroque
- **7**: Irregular

### Quality Classification
- **Diamond**: Best magical quality
- **Ruby**: High magical quality
- **Emerald**: Good magical quality
- **Sapphire**: Moderate magical quality
- **Topaz**: Fair magical quality
- **Amethyst**: Poor magical quality
- **Cobalt**: Worst magical quality

### Examples
- **Diamond 1**: Perfectly round, best magical quality
- **Ruby 2**: Near-round, high magical quality
- **Emerald 3**: Oval, good magical quality
- **Sapphire 4**: Button, moderate magical quality
- **Topaz 5**: Drop, fair magical quality
- **Amethyst 6**: Baroque, poor magical quality
- **Cobalt 7**: Irregular, worst magical quality

### Uses of Magical Pearls
Magical pearls are used in various magical crafts, potions, and enchantments. Their quality and shape influence their effectiveness and value in these applications.
